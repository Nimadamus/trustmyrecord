Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Bruins took 49.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 16th in the NHL) and the Hurricanes took 59.3% (2nd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 50.5% to 60.8%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 49.4% to 59.8%.

The goals followed a different split: the Bruins took 71.7% of five on five goals and the Hurricanes 60.0%. The Bruins outscored their shot share by 22.3 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Bruins ran at 1.076 (12.6% shooting, .950 saves) and the Hurricanes at 0.997 (9.8%, .899). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Bruins' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Bruins were the heavier side, 22.0 hits per 60 minutes to 21.5; the Bruins blocked 15.4 shots per 60 to 11.3 for the Hurricanes; in puck management the Bruins had 4.3 takeaways and 17.0 giveaways per 60, the Hurricanes 4.7 and 16.1.

Deployment tilted too: the Hurricanes started 62.5% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 49.1% for the Bruins.
