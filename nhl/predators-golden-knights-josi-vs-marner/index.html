Nashville Predators breakdown

Team breakdown

The Predators are 10-4-5 with 25 points, 2nd in the Central Division and 4th in the Western Conference. They're 7-1-2 over their last 10, with 36 goals for and 23 against. They've won 6 straight. On the road this season they're 4-1-3.

Offensively, the Predators scored 3.05 goals a game this season (18th) on 26.0 shots (23rd), converting 11.7% of them (14th). The power play ran at 26.6% (8th) on 3.37 chances a game (9th). Team breakdown

The goals followed a different split: the Predators took 47.1% of five on five goals and the Golden Knights 53.2%. The Predators outscored their shot share by 1.9 points and the Golden Knights outscored their shot share by 3.1 points.
