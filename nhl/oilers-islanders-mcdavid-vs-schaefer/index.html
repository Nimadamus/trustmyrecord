Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Oilers took 47.7% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 23rd in the NHL) and the Islanders took 51.8% (12th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 47.7% to 51.1%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 51.1% to 53.9%.

The goals followed a different split: the Oilers took 54.2% of five on five goals and the Islanders 54.3%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 6.6 points and the Islanders outscored their shot share by 2.5 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Oilers ran at 1.037 (13.3% shooting, .904 saves) and the Islanders at 1.012 (9.3%, .919). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' and the Islanders' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Oilers were the heavier side, 25.9 hits per 60 minutes to 24.1; the Oilers blocked 13.7 shots per 60 to 13.5 for the Islanders; in puck management the Oilers had 3.5 takeaways and 14.9 giveaways per 60, the Islanders 3.5 and 13.9.
