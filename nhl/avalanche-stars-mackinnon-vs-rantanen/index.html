Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Avalanche took 51.8% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 12th in the NHL) and the Stars took 50.8% (16th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 52.7% to 51.9%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 52.6% to 51.2%.

The goals followed a different split: the Avalanche took 66.7% of five on five goals and the Stars 66.7%. The Avalanche outscored their shot share by 14.9 points and the Stars outscored their shot share by 15.9 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Avalanche ran at 1.071 (16.7% shooting, .905 saves) and the Stars at 1.046 (9.8%, .948). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Avalanche's and the Stars' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Stars were the heavier side, 21.7 hits per 60 minutes to 17.7; the Avalanche blocked 14.2 shots per 60 to 15.1 for the Stars; in puck management the Avalanche had 4.8 takeaways and 16.7 giveaways per 60, the Stars 2.8 and 13.6.

Deployment tilted too: the Avalanche started 47.1% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 42.9% for the Stars.
