Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Panthers took 57.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 3rd in the NHL) and the Senators took 44.4% (27th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 56.1% to 45.0%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 59.6% to 41.8%.

The goals followed a different split: the Panthers took 53.3% of five on five goals and the Senators 50.0%. The Panthers scored 4.1 points below their shot share and the Senators outscored their shot share by 5.6 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Panthers ran at 0.995 (9.2% shooting, .903 saves) and the Senators at 1.014 (9.7%, .917). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Senators' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Panthers were the heavier side, 31.1 hits per 60 minutes to 23.6; the Panthers blocked 9.9 shots per 60 to 16.8 for the Senators; in puck management the Panthers had 3.7 takeaways and 15.5 giveaways per 60, the Senators 4.2 and 15.4.

Deployment tilted too: the Panthers started 58.8% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 45.2% for the Senators.
