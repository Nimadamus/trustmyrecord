Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Golden Knights took 50.7% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 11th in the NHL) and the Oilers took 46.9% (27th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 52.2% to 46.7%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 50.6% to 49.8%.

The goals followed a different split: the Golden Knights took 50.9% of five on five goals and the Oilers 54.4%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 7.4 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Golden Knights ran at 0.988 (9.3% shooting, .896 saves) and the Oilers at 1.032 (11.7%, .915). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000. A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Golden Knights.

The Oilers were the heavier side, 23.6 hits per 60 minutes to 22.3; the Golden Knights blocked 13.8 shots per 60 to 13.2 for the Oilers; in puck management the Golden Knights had 3.9 takeaways and 16.5 giveaways per 60, the Oilers 3.8 and 14.7.
