Tampa Bay Lightning breakdown

Team breakdown

The Lightning are 15-8-2 with 32 points, 3rd in the Atlantic Division and 5th in the Eastern Conference. They're 5-5-0 over their last 10, with 31 goals for and 31 against. They've lost 3 straight. On the road this season they're 5-4-2.

Offensively, the Lightning scored 3.48 goals a game this season (8th) on 28.8 shots (8th), converting 12.1% of them (11th). The power play ran at 21.5% (18th) on 3.16 chances a game (14th). That's 0.33 goals a game above the league average of 3.15. Without the puck they allowed 3.04 goals a game (15th) and 25.2 shots (4th), with a team save percentage of .879 (25th). The penalty kill worked at 74.6% (22nd), shorthanded 2.52 times a game (3rd). Few teams allowed fewer shots, so the goals against came from the save percentage, which puts more weight on who starts in goal.

The crease is still open: two goalies on the roster and no starter reported yet.

The Lightning are without Erik Cernak (illness), Conor Geekie (shoulder) and Pontus Holmberg (lower body) and have Victor Hedman listed day to day.

The profile: the Lightning were top eight in the league in goals per game, shots per game, shots allowed and five on five shot attempt share; bottom eight in team save percentage.

Florida Panthers breakdown

Team breakdown

The Panthers are 15-8-3 with 33 points, 2nd in the Atlantic Division and 4th in the Eastern Conference. They're 6-3-1 over their last 10, with 32 goals for and 29 against. They've won 3 straight. At home this season they're 7-3-0.

Offensively, the Panthers scored 3.23 goals a game this season (14th) on 29.2 shots (5th), converting 11.1% of them (23rd). The power play ran at 24.0% (9th) on 3.85 chances a game (1st). Without the puck they allowed 2.92 goals a game (11th) and 25.4 shots (5th), with a team save percentage of .885 (16th). The penalty kill worked at 84.8% (5th), shorthanded 3.81 times a game (32nd).

No starter is reported here either, with two goalies on the roster.

The Panthers are without Garnet Hathaway (lower body) and Jonah Gadjovich (neck).

The profile: the Panthers were top eight in the league in shots per game, shots allowed, penalty kill and five on five shot attempt share.

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Lightning took 54.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 3rd in the NHL) and the Panthers took 53.7% (5th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 54.1% to 52.8%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 54.5% to 55.7%.

The goals followed a different split: the Lightning took 55.8% of five on five goals and the Panthers 46.8%. The Panthers scored 6.9 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Lightning ran at 1.006 (11.1% shooting, .896 saves) and the Panthers at 0.979 (8.5%, .893). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Panthers.

The Panthers were the heavier side, 32.1 hits per 60 minutes to 22.3; the Lightning blocked 12.5 shots per 60 to 10.5 for the Panthers; in puck management the Lightning had 3.3 takeaways and 15.3 giveaways per 60, the Panthers 4.0 and 15.5.
