Tampa Bay Lightning breakdown

Team breakdown

The Lightning are 11-4-2 with 24 points, 3rd in the Atlantic Division and 5th in the Eastern Conference. They're 6-2-2 over their last 10, with 42 goals for and 34 against. On the road this season they're 3-4-2.

Offensively, the Lightning scored 3.71 goals a game this season (6th) on 29.9 shots (5th), converting 12.4% of them (8th). The power play ran at 19.4% (21st) on 3.65 chances a game (4th). That's 0.57 goals a game above the league average of 3.14. Without the puck they allowed 3.06 goals a game (17th) and 24.7 shots (3rd), with a team save percentage of .876 (27th). The penalty kill worked at 72.9% (25th), shorthanded 2.82 times a game (6th). Few teams allowed fewer shots, so the goals against came from the save percentage, which puts more weight on who starts in goal.

The crease is still open: two goalies on the roster and no starter reported yet.

The Lightning are without Brayden Point (undisclosed).

The profile: the Lightning were top eight in the league in goals per game, shots per game, shots allowed and shooting percentage; bottom eight in team save percentage and penalty kill.

Buffalo Sabres breakdown

Team breakdown

The Sabres are 8-6-3 with 19 points, 6th in the Atlantic Division and 10th in the Eastern Conference. They're 5-3-2 over their last 10, with 31 goals for and 31 against. At home this season they're 4-5-0.

Offensively, the Sabres scored 2.94 goals a game this season (21st) on 28.4 shots (10th), converting 10.4% of them (24th). The power play ran at 14.3% (30th) on 3.29 chances a game (14th). Without the puck they allowed 3.29 goals a game (20th) and 27.1 shots (13th), with a team save percentage of .879 (25th). The penalty kill worked at 71.2% (26th), shorthanded 3.47 times a game (26th).

No starter is reported here either, with three goalies on the roster.

The Sabres are without Konsta Helenius (upper body), Alex Lyon (shoulder), Justin Danforth (upper body) and Matt Grzelcyk (lower body).

The profile: the Sabres were bottom eight in team save percentage, power play and penalty kill.

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Lightning took 55.7% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 3rd in the NHL) and the Sabres took 51.9% (9th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 55.4% to 51.8%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 54.9% to 50.4%.

The goals followed a different split: the Lightning took 57.7% of five on five goals and the Sabres 51.5%. The Lightning outscored their shot share by 2.0 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Lightning ran at 1.009 (11.6% shooting, .893 saves) and the Sabres at 0.993 (9.5%, .898).

The Lightning were the heavier side, 20.5 hits per 60 minutes to 18.4; the Lightning blocked 12.7 shots per 60 to 12.7 for the Sabres; in puck management the Lightning had 3.6 takeaways and 15.2 giveaways per 60, the Sabres 3.8 and 14.7.

Deployment tilted too: the Lightning started 56.9% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 52.1% for the Sabres.
