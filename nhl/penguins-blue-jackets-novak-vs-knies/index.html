Five on five

At five on five in 2025-26, the Penguins took 50.2% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 13th in the NHL) and the Blue Jackets took 51.1% (11th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 50.5% to 50.5%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 50.0% to 51.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Penguins took 53.7% of five on five goals and the Blue Jackets 51.8%. The Penguins outscored their shot share by 3.6 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Penguins ran at 1.012 (11.1% shooting, .901 saves) and the Blue Jackets at 1.003 (9.1%, .911). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Penguins' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.
