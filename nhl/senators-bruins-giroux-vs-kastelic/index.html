Ottawa Senators breakdown

Team breakdown

The Senators are 1-0-0 with 2 points. So far they beat the Maple Leafs 3-2. Last season they finished 44-27-11.

Offensively, the Senators scored 3.35 goals a game in 2025-26 (8th) on 28.9 shots (9th), converting 11.6% of them (10th). The power play ran at 24.0% (8th) on 3.21 chances a game (3rd). Five on five

At five on five in 2025-26, the Senators took 52.6% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 5th in the NHL) and the Bruins took 49.0% (19th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 53.7% to 49.1%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 52.1% to 49.2%.

The goals followed a different split: the Senators took 52.3% of five on five goals and the Bruins 54.9%. The Bruins outscored their shot share by 5.8 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Senators ran at 0.993 (10.0% shooting, .893 saves) and the Bruins at 1.022 (10.6%, .916). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Bruins' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Senators were the heavier side, 23.6 hits per 60 minutes to 22.4; the Senators blocked 13.3 shots per 60 to 14.9 for the Bruins; in puck management the Senators had 5.0 takeaways and 13.7 giveaways per 60, the Bruins 4.5 and 15.7.

Deployment tilted too: the Senators started 51.2% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 46.8% for the Bruins.
