The goals followed a different split: the Red Wings took 50.0% of five on five goals and the Avalanche 59.6%. The Avalanche outscored their shot share by 6.2 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Red Wings ran at 0.987 (8.1% shooting, .906 saves) and the Avalanche at 1.030 (12.9%, .901). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Red Wings.
