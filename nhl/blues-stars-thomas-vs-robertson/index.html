Five on five

At five on five in 2025-26, the Blues took 48.3% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 24th in the NHL) and the Stars took 47.1% (27th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 48.0% to 48.1%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 47.6% to 46.2%.

The goals followed a different split: the Blues took 48.5% of five on five goals and the Stars 55.2%. The Stars outscored their shot share by 8.1 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Blues ran at 1.000 (9.8% shooting, .902 saves) and the Stars at 1.024 (10.8%, .916). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Stars' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.
