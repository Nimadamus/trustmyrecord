The preview · 875 words

Why this is the featured game

We looked at all 3 games on the September 30, 2026 slate, and this is the one we're featuring.

It's the Battle of Pennsylvania, a rivalry that doesn't need a good standings reason to get heated. It's a Metropolitan Division game, so whatever the winner takes comes straight out of a direct rival's column. It puts Sidney Crosby, who led the Penguins with 74 points in 2025-26, on the ice against Travis Konecny, who led the Flyers with 68. It's the Flyers' home opener. It's a national US broadcast on HBO MAX, TNT, TRUTV. And the market has it close enough that the price itself is part of the story.

Current form

The Penguins open their season here. Last season they finished 41-25-16 with 98 points, going 20-13-8 at home and 21-12-8 on the road, and scored 293 goals while allowing 268.

The Flyers open their season here. Last season they finished 43-27-12 with 98 points, going 20-13-8 at home and 23-14-4 on the road, and scored 250 goals while allowing 243.

Players to watch

For the Penguins, Sidney Crosby led the club with 74 points in 2025-26, and Bryan Rust led in goals with 29.

For the Flyers, Travis Konecny led the club with 68 points in 2025-26, and Owen Tippett led in goals with 28.

Worth flagging: Bryan Rust is on the injury report.

The offensive matchup

The Penguins scored 3.54 goals a game last season (2025-26), 3rd in the league, on 28.6 shots a game. The Flyers scored 2.93, 21st, on 25.5 shots.

The Penguins converted 12.4% of their shots on goal in 2025-26, to 11.5% for the Flyers. Neither side relied on unusual finishing, so shot volume carries most of the weight.

The defensive matchup

Defensively, the Penguins allowed 3.15 goals a game last season (2025-26), 24th in the league, and 27.4 shots against. The Flyers gave up 2.91 goals (9th) on 25.5 shots a game.

The shot differential is where the gap shows. The Penguins finished at +1.2 shots a game in 2025-26 against +0.0 for the Flyers, and the team that owns the puck usually decides what kind of game this is.

In goal

Neither club has named a starter yet. The goalie cards above update through the day and switch the moment a starter is reported.

Special teams

The Penguins' power play ran at 24.1% last season (2025-26), 7th in the league, and it goes up against the Flyers' penalty kill, which worked at 77.6% (22nd). The other way, the Flyers' power play clicked at 15.7% (32nd) against the Penguins' kill at 81.4% (6th).

Add the two units together and the Penguins carry a special teams edge of 12.3 points on 2025-26 numbers. In a game priced this tight, one power play goal can be the whole difference.

In the faceoff circle the Flyers won 49.5% of their draws in 2025-26, to 48.2% for the Penguins.

Injuries and lineup notes

The Penguins are without Andrei Kuzmenko (personal), Ben Kindel (lower body), Blake Lizotte (upper body) and Bryan Rust (upper body), with 6 more on the injury report.

The Flyers are without Denver Barkey (lower body) and Helge Grans (lower body) and have Nikita Grebenkin listed day to day.

How the game should be played

By volume in 2025-26, the Penguins took 28.6 shots a game and allowed 27.4, while the Flyers took 25.5 and allowed 25.5.

So the likely shape of this one has the Penguins spending more time in the offensive zone, and the Flyers needing their goaltender and their transition game to make fewer looks count.

Games involving these two averaged 53.4 total shots on goal, against a league norm of 55.7. That's a lower event pairing than usual.
