At five on five in 2025-26, the Penguins took 50.2% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 13th in the NHL) and the Flyers took 47.6% (26th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 50.5% to 48.6%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 50.0% to 47.4%.

The goals followed a different split: the Penguins took 53.7% of five on five goals and the Flyers 51.9%. The Penguins outscored their shot share by 3.6 points and the Flyers outscored their shot share by 4.3 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Penguins ran at 1.012 (11.1% shooting, .901 saves) and the Flyers at 1.008 (10.2%, .905). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Penguins' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.
