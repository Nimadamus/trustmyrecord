New Jersey Devils breakdown

Team breakdown

The Devils are 8-5-0 with 16 points, 3rd in the Metropolitan Division and 7th in the Eastern Conference. They're 7-3-0 over their last 10, with 37 goals for and 25 against. On the road this season they're 2-3-0.

Offensively, the Devils scored 3.31 goals a game this season (11th) on 32.6 shots (2nd), converting 10.1% of them (25th). The power play ran at 17.5% (22nd) on 3.08 chances a game (19th). Without the puck they allowed 2.92 goals a game (9th) and 25.1 shots (6th), with a team save percentage of .883 (20th). The penalty kill worked at 72.4% (24th), shorthanded 2.23 times a game (1st). Few teams allowed fewer shots, so the goals against came from the save percentage, which puts more weight on who starts in goal.

The crease is still open: three goalies on the roster and no starter reported yet.

The Devils are without Seamus Casey (knee).

The profile: the Devils were top eight in the league in shots per game, shots allowed and five on five shot attempt share; bottom eight in shooting percentage.

Vegas Golden Knights breakdown

Team breakdown

The Golden Knights are 9-4-1 with 19 points, 2nd in the Pacific Division and 3rd in the Western Conference. They're 6-3-1 over their last 10, with 34 goals for and 29 against. At home this season they're 6-2-0.

Offensively, the Golden Knights scored 3.57 goals a game this season (9th) on 29.7 shots (8th), converting 12.0% of them (11th). The power play ran at 25.6% (8th) on 3.07 chances a game (22nd). That's 0.45 goals a game above the league average of 3.12. Without the puck they allowed 2.79 goals a game (7th) and 25.1 shots (8th), with a team save percentage of .889 (13th). The penalty kill worked at 67.6% (26th), shorthanded 2.43 times a game (2nd).

No starter is reported here either, with three goalies on the roster.

The Golden Knights are without Parker Wotherspoon (upper body) and have Carter Hart listed day to day.

The profile: the Golden Knights were top eight in the league in goals allowed, shots per game, shots allowed and power play; bottom eight in penalty kill.

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Devils took 57.5% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 2nd in the NHL) and the Golden Knights took 52.4% (11th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 56.8% to 54.0%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 57.4% to 53.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Devils took 52.7% of five on five goals and the Golden Knights 55.4%. The Devils scored 4.8 points below their shot share and the Golden Knights outscored their shot share by 3.0 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Devils ran at 0.988 (8.6% shooting, .902 saves) and the Golden Knights at 1.003 (9.4%, .909). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Devils.

The Golden Knights were the heavier side, 20.8 hits per 60 minutes to 16.8; the Devils blocked 12.2 shots per 60 to 13.7 for the Golden Knights; in puck management the Devils had 4.5 takeaways and 16.0 giveaways per 60, the Golden Knights 4.0 and 17.3.
