Dallas Stars breakdown

Team breakdown

The Stars are 2-3-1 with 5 points, 7th in the Central Division and 12th in the Western Conference. They're 2-3-1 over their last 6, with 13 goals for and 11 against. On the road this season they're 1-1-1. Last season they finished 50-20-12.

Offensively, the Stars scored 2.17 goals a game this season (30th) on 25.0 shots (24th), converting 8.7% of them (27th). The power play ran at 14.3% (25th) on 2.33 chances a game (29th). The penalty kill worked at 85.0% (10th), shorthanded 3.33 times a game (15th).

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Stars took 50.8% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 15th in the NHL) and the Avalanche took 51.8% (13th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 51.9% to 52.7%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 51.2% to 52.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Stars took 66.7% of five on five goals and the Avalanche 66.7%. The Stars outscored their shot share by 15.9 points and the Avalanche outscored their shot share by 14.9 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Stars ran at 1.046 (9.8% shooting, .948 saves) and the Avalanche at 1.071 (16.7%, .905). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Stars' and the Avalanche's results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Stars were the heavier side, 21.7 hits per 60 minutes to 17.7; the Stars blocked 15.1 shots per 60 to 14.2 for the Avalanche; in puck management the Stars had 2.8 takeaways and 13.6 giveaways per 60, the Avalanche 4.8 and 16.7.

Deployment tilted too: the Avalanche started 47.1% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 42.9% for the Stars.
