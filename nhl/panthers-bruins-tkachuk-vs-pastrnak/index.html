Florida Panthers breakdown

Team breakdown

The Panthers are 9-6-2 with 20 points, 5th in the Atlantic Division and 8th in the Eastern Conference. They're 6-4-0 over their last 10, with 36 goals for and 31 against. On the road this season they're 5-4-2.

Offensively, the Panthers scored 3.11 goals a game this season (16th) on 29.5 shots (7th), converting 10.5% of them (25th). The power play ran at 23.5% (10th) on 4.50 chances a game (1st). Without the puck they allowed 3.06 goals a game (14th) and 25.2 shots (6th), with a team save percentage of .879 (23rd). The penalty kill worked at 85.7% (5th), shorthanded 4.28 times a game (32nd). Few teams allowed fewer shots, so the goals against came from the save percentage, which puts more weight on who starts in goal.

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Panthers took 54.7% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 4th in the NHL) and the Bruins took 48.6% (21st). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 53.4% to 49.5%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 57.0% to 48.2%.

The goals followed a different split: the Panthers took 42.9% of five on five goals and the Bruins 68.1%. The Panthers scored 11.8 points below their shot share and the Bruins outscored their shot share by 19.5 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Panthers ran at 0.962 (7.9% shooting, .883 saves) and the Bruins at 1.068 (12.5%, .943). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Bruins' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000. A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Panthers.

The Panthers were the heavier side, 32.2 hits per 60 minutes to 20.9; the Panthers blocked 10.7 shots per 60 to 14.6 for the Bruins; in puck management the Panthers had 3.8 takeaways and 15.2 giveaways per 60, the Bruins 4.0 and 16.6.

Deployment tilted too: the Panthers started 55.0% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 48.8% for the Bruins.
