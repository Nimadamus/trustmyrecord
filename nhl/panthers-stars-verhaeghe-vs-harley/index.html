Florida Panthers breakdown

Team breakdown

The Panthers are 12-7-2 with 26 points, 5th in the Atlantic Division and 7th in the Eastern Conference. They're 7-3-0 over their last 10, with 38 goals for and 27 against. They've won 3 straight. On the road this season they're 7-5-2.

Offensively, the Panthers scored 3.29 goals a game this season (13th) on 29.4 shots (5th), converting 11.2% of them (20th). The power play ran at 24.4% (11th) on 4.09 chances a game (1st). Without the puck they allowed 2.90 goals a game (11th) and 26.0 shots (7th), with a team save percentage of .888 (14th). The penalty kill worked at 85.5% (7th), shorthanded 3.95 times a game (31st).

The crease is still open: two goalies on the roster and no starter reported yet.

The Panthers are without Aleksander Barkov (knee), Brad Marchand (knee) and Jonah Gadjovich (neck).

The profile: the Panthers were top eight in the league in shots per game, shots allowed, penalty kill and five on five shot attempt share.

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Panthers took 53.6% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 5th in the NHL) and the Stars took 50.9% (10th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 52.3% to 52.6%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 56.4% to 52.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Panthers took 46.8% of five on five goals and the Stars 49.3%. The Panthers scored 6.9 points below their shot share and the Stars scored 1.6 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Panthers ran at 0.981 (8.7% shooting, .893 saves) and the Stars at 0.981 (9.0%, .891). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Panthers and the Stars.

The Panthers were the heavier side, 31.9 hits per 60 minutes to 19.8; the Panthers blocked 10.9 shots per 60 to 14.6 for the Stars; in puck management the Panthers had 3.8 takeaways and 15.4 giveaways per 60, the Stars 3.7 and 13.9.

Deployment tilted too: the Panthers started 52.3% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 47.7% for the Stars.
