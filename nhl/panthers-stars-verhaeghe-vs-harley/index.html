Team breakdown

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Panthers took 53.8% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 5th in the NHL) and the Stars took 50.5% (13th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 52.4% to 51.9%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 56.3% to 51.8%.

The goals followed a different split: the Panthers took 44.6% of five on five goals and the Stars 48.5%. The Panthers scored 9.2 points below their shot share and the Stars scored 1.9 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Panthers ran at 0.973 (8.5% shooting, .888 saves) and the Stars at 0.981 (9.4%, .887). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Panthers and the Stars.

The Panthers were the heavier side, 31.9 hits per 60 minutes to 19.7; the Panthers blocked 10.8 shots per 60 to 14.5 for the Stars; in puck management the Panthers had 3.8 takeaways and 15.3 giveaways per 60, the Stars 3.8 and 14.3.

Deployment tilted too: the Panthers started 53.2% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 47.6% for the Stars.
