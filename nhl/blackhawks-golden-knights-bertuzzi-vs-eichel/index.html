The preview · 793 words

Why this is the featured game

We looked at both games still to be played on the September 29, 2026 slate, and this is the one we're featuring.

It's a Western Conference game with seeding implications down the line. It's the Golden Knights' home opener. It's a national US broadcast on ESPN.

Current form

The Blackhawks open their season here. Last season they finished 29-39-14 with 72 points, going 14-19-8 at home and 15-20-6 on the road, and scored 213 goals while allowing 275.

The Golden Knights open their season here. Last season they finished 39-26-17 with 95 points, going 20-12-9 at home and 19-14-8 on the road, and scored 265 goals while allowing 250.

The offensive matchup

The Blackhawks scored 2.56 goals a game last season (2025-26), 30th in the league, on 24.6 shots a game. The Golden Knights scored 3.22, 14th, on 29.0 shots.

The Golden Knights converted 11.1% of their shots on goal in 2025-26, to 10.4% for the Blackhawks. Neither side relied on unusual finishing, so shot volume carries most of the weight.

The defensive matchup

Defensively, the Blackhawks allowed 3.29 goals a game last season (2025-26), 27th in the league, and 30.0 shots against. The Golden Knights gave up 2.95 goals (12th) on 24.4 shots a game.

The shot differential is where the gap shows. The Golden Knights finished at +4.6 shots a game in 2025-26 against -5.4 for the Blackhawks, and the team that owns the puck usually decides what kind of game this is.

In goal

Spencer Knight is confirmed to start for the Blackhawks. He went 19-25-11 with a .902 save percentage and a 2.82 goals against average in 2025-26, over 55 games. On the road that season he saved .909. He's 0-3-1 against the Golden Knights since the start of 2024-25, with a .868 save percentage in 4 games. Over his last 5 appearances he stopped .851 of the shots he faced.

Carter Hart is confirmed to start for the Golden Knights. He went 11-3-3 with a .891 save percentage and a 2.71 goals against average in 2025-26, over 18 games. At home that season he saved .888. Over his last 5 appearances he stopped .942 of the shots he faced.

That's a real gap in the crease: Spencer Knight's save percentage sits about 11 points higher, and over 30 shots that works out to roughly 0.3 goals a night.

Special teams

The Blackhawks' power play ran at 16.9% last season (2025-26), 29th in the league, and it goes up against the Golden Knights' penalty kill, which worked at 81.4% (7th). The other way, the Golden Knights' power play clicked at 24.6% (6th) against the Blackhawks' kill at 83.6% (2nd).

Add the two units together and the Golden Knights carry a special teams edge of 5.4 points on 2025-26 numbers. That's often where a favorite pulls away, or where an underdog finds the goal it needs.

In the faceoff circle the Golden Knights won 51.0% of their draws in 2025-26, to 46.0% for the Blackhawks.

Injuries and lineup notes

The Blackhawks are without Andrew Mangiapane (lower body) and Connor Bedard (shoulder).

The Golden Knights are without Lukas Cormier (undisclosed).

How the game should be played

By volume in 2025-26, the Blackhawks took 24.6 shots a game and allowed 30.0, while the Golden Knights took 29.0 and allowed 24.4.

So the likely shape of this one has the Golden Knights spending more time in the offensive zone, and the Blackhawks needing their goaltender and their transition game to make fewer looks count.

Games involving these two averaged 54.0 total shots on goal, against a league norm of 55.7. That's a lower event pairing than usual.
