Carolina Hurricanes breakdown

Team breakdown

The Hurricanes are 4-1-1 with 9 points, 1st in the Metropolitan Division and 1st in the Eastern Conference. They're 4-1-1 over their last 6, with 23 goals for and 16 against. They've won 4 straight. On the road this season they're 3-0-0. Last season they finished 53-22-7.

Offensively, the Hurricanes scored 3.83 goals a game this season (5th) on 32.2 shots (5th), converting 11.9% of them (14th). The power play ran at 26.9% (7th) on 4.33 chances a game (4th). Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Hurricanes took 59.0% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 2nd in the NHL) and the Flyers took 37.1% (32nd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 60.6% to 35.5%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 59.8% to 36.9%.

The goals followed a different split: the Hurricanes took 52.0% of five on five goals and the Flyers 20.0%. The Hurricanes scored 7.0 points below their shot share and the Flyers scored 17.1 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Hurricanes ran at 0.970 (9.8% shooting, .872 saves) and the Flyers at 0.955 (4.5%, .909). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Hurricanes and the Flyers.

The Flyers were the heavier side, 24.1 hits per 60 minutes to 23.8; the Hurricanes blocked 11.6 shots per 60 to 17.2 for the Flyers; in puck management the Hurricanes had 3.8 takeaways and 17.3 giveaways per 60, the Flyers 3.9 and 16.7.

Deployment tilted too: the Hurricanes started 61.0% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 34.4% for the Flyers.
