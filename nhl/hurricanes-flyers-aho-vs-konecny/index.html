Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Hurricanes took 58.5% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 2nd in the NHL) and the Flyers took 38.1% (32nd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 59.6% to 37.0%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 58.7% to 38.0%.

The goals followed a different split: the Hurricanes took 56.7% of five on five goals and the Flyers 20.0%. The Hurricanes scored 1.8 points below their shot share and the Flyers scored 18.1 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Hurricanes ran at 0.991 (10.8% shooting, .883 saves) and the Flyers at 0.948 (4.8%, .900). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Flyers.

The Flyers were the heavier side, 23.5 hits per 60 minutes to 22.3; the Hurricanes blocked 10.9 shots per 60 to 16.8 for the Flyers; in puck management the Hurricanes had 4.2 takeaways and 17.1 giveaways per 60, the Flyers 4.2 and 16.9.

Deployment tilted too: the Hurricanes started 61.4% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 34.8% for the Flyers.
