Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Hurricanes took 60.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 1st in the NHL) and the Rangers took 48.0% (24th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 62.0% to 47.2%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 60.4% to 48.3%.

The goals followed a different split: the Hurricanes took 66.7% of five on five goals and the Rangers 49.4%. The Hurricanes outscored their shot share by 6.2 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Hurricanes ran at 1.021 (10.8% shooting, .913 saves) and the Rangers at 1.005 (9.3%, .912). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Hurricanes' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Rangers were the heavier side, 25.3 hits per 60 minutes to 19.7; the Hurricanes blocked 11.8 shots per 60 to 14.6 for the Rangers; in puck management the Hurricanes had 4.8 takeaways and 15.4 giveaways per 60, the Rangers 4.0 and 14.2.

Deployment tilted too: the Hurricanes started 58.3% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 45.6% for the Rangers.
