Team breakdown

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Hurricanes took 59.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 2nd in the NHL) and the Oilers took 47.3% (22nd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 60.7% to 47.2%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 59.3% to 51.5%.

The goals followed a different split: the Hurricanes took 58.5% of five on five goals and the Oilers 56.2%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 8.9 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Hurricanes ran at 0.991 (10.3% shooting, .888 saves) and the Oilers at 1.052 (15.3%, .899). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Oilers were the heavier side, 24.5 hits per 60 minutes to 21.5; the Hurricanes blocked 11.3 shots per 60 to 13.9 for the Oilers; in puck management the Hurricanes had 4.4 takeaways and 15.8 giveaways per 60, the Oilers 3.2 and 15.7.

Deployment tilted too: the Hurricanes started 61.9% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 51.7% for the Oilers.
