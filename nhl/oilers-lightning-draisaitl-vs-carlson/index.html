Edmonton Oilers breakdown

Team breakdown

The Oilers are 17-3-3 with 37 points, 1st in the Pacific Division and 1st in the Western Conference. They're 8-1-1 over their last 10, with 36 goals for and 19 against. They've won 6 straight. On the road this season they're 10-0-1.

Offensively, the Oilers scored 3.96 goals a game this season (2nd) on 24.8 shots (29th), converting 15.9% of them (1st). The power play ran at 35.1% (2nd) on 3.35 chances a game (9th). That's 0.82 goals a game above the league average of 3.13. Without the puck they allowed 2.61 goals a game (5th) and 26.7 shots (13th), with a team save percentage of .902 (5th). The penalty kill worked at 80.3% (12th), shorthanded 3.30 times a game (22nd).

Devon Levi gets the net, confirmed. He saved .907 over 11 games this season.

The Oilers are without Alec Regula (undisclosed), Frederik Andersen (knee), Matt Savoie (lower body) and Mattias Janmark (undisclosed) and have Ryan Nugent-Hopkins listed day to day.

The profile: the Oilers were top eight in the league in goals per game, goals allowed, shooting percentage and team save percentage; bottom eight in shots per game and five on five shot attempt share.

Tampa Bay Lightning breakdown

Team breakdown

The Lightning are 14-5-2 with 30 points, 1st in the Atlantic Division and 2nd in the Eastern Conference. They're 7-2-1 over their last 10, with 41 goals for and 27 against. At home this season they're 9-1-0.

Offensively, the Lightning scored 3.76 goals a game this season (5th) on 29.3 shots (7th), converting 12.8% of them (5th). The power play ran at 20.3% (18th) on 3.29 chances a game (11th). That's 0.63 goals a game above the league average of 3.13. Without the puck they allowed 2.86 goals a game (10th) and 25.1 shots (4th), with a team save percentage of .886 (16th). The penalty kill worked at 72.2% (24th), shorthanded 2.57 times a game (3rd).

The crease is still open: two goalies on the roster and no starter reported yet.

The Lightning are without Brandon Hagel (lower body) and Conor Geekie (upper body) and have Zemgus Girgensons listed day to day.

The profile: the Lightning were top eight in the league in goals per game, shots per game, shots allowed and shooting percentage.

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Oilers took 47.0% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 27th in the NHL) and the Lightning took 54.0% (3rd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 46.6% to 54.1%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 49.6% to 54.5%.

The goals followed a different split: the Oilers took 56.6% of five on five goals and the Lightning 60.4%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 9.7 points and the Lightning outscored their shot share by 6.5 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Oilers ran at 1.038 (11.5% shooting, .922 saves) and the Lightning at 1.027 (12.1%, .905). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' and the Lightning's results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Oilers were the heavier side, 22.8 hits per 60 minutes to 21.8; the Oilers blocked 13.2 shots per 60 to 12.8 for the Lightning; in puck management the Oilers had 3.6 takeaways and 14.6 giveaways per 60, the Lightning 3.4 and 15.8.

Deployment tilted too: the Lightning started 54.9% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 51.8% for the Oilers.
