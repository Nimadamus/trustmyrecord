Edmonton Oilers breakdown

Team breakdown

The Oilers are 17-3-3 with 37 points, 1st in the Pacific Division and 2nd in the Western Conference. They're 8-1-1 over their last 10, with 36 goals for and 19 against. They've won 6 straight. On the road this season they're 10-0-1.

Offensively, the Oilers scored 3.83 goals a game this season (4th) on 24.8 shots (28th), converting 15.5% of them (1st). The power play ran at 33.8% (2nd) on 3.33 chances a game (9th). Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Oilers took 46.9% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 27th in the NHL) and the Lightning took 54.0% (3rd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 46.4% to 54.2%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 49.4% to 54.5%.

The goals followed a different split: the Oilers took 55.8% of five on five goals and the Lightning 60.6%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 8.9 points and the Lightning outscored their shot share by 6.6 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Oilers ran at 1.035 (11.4% shooting, .921 saves) and the Lightning at 1.027 (12.0%, .906). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' and the Lightning's results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Oilers were the heavier side, 23.0 hits per 60 minutes to 21.6; the Oilers blocked 13.0 shots per 60 to 13.0 for the Lightning; in puck management the Oilers had 3.7 takeaways and 14.8 giveaways per 60, the Lightning 3.4 and 15.4.

Deployment tilted too: the Lightning started 54.9% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 51.6% for the Oilers.
