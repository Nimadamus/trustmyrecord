Carolina Hurricanes breakdown

Team breakdown

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Hurricanes took 59.7% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 1st in the NHL) and the Lightning took 54.4% (3rd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 61.5% to 54.2%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 59.7% to 54.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Hurricanes took 67.3% of five on five goals and the Lightning 57.5%. The Hurricanes outscored their shot share by 7.6 points and the Lightning outscored their shot share by 3.1 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Hurricanes ran at 1.026 (10.8% shooting, .919 saves) and the Lightning at 1.013 (11.1%, .902). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Hurricanes' and the Lightning's results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Lightning were the heavier side, 22.0 hits per 60 minutes to 19.9; the Hurricanes blocked 12.4 shots per 60 to 12.6 for the Lightning; in puck management the Hurricanes had 4.7 takeaways and 15.0 giveaways per 60, the Lightning 3.3 and 15.4.
