Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Ducks took 48.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 24th in the NHL) and the Flames took 50.7% (14th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 48.4% to 49.7%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 47.2% to 50.2%.

The goals followed a different split: the Ducks took 47.1% of five on five goals and the Flames 37.1%. The Flames scored 13.6 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Ducks ran at 0.995 (10.5% shooting, .890 saves) and the Flames at 0.957 (6.7%, .890). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Flames.

The Ducks were the heavier side, 25.1 hits per 60 minutes to 22.6; the Ducks blocked 14.6 shots per 60 to 13.7 for the Flames; in puck management the Ducks had 3.3 takeaways and 15.0 giveaways per 60, the Flames 3.7 and 14.9.
