The goals followed a different split: the Oilers took 56.2% of five on five goals and the Devils 55.0%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 8.8 points and the Devils scored 2.6 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Oilers ran at 1.042 (13.1% shooting, .911 saves) and the Devils at 0.995 (9.1%, .904). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.
