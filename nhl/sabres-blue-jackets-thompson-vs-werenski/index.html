Five on five

At five on five in 2025-26, the Sabres took 49.6% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 14th in the NHL) and the Blue Jackets took 51.1% (11th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 49.8% to 50.5%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 51.0% to 51.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Sabres took 55.3% of five on five goals and the Blue Jackets 51.8%. The Sabres outscored their shot share by 5.7 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Sabres ran at 1.020 (10.6% shooting, .914 saves) and the Blue Jackets at 1.003 (9.1%, .911). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Sabres' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.
