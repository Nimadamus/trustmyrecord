Detroit Red Wings breakdown

Team breakdown

The Red Wings are 4-4-3 with 11 points, 7th in the Atlantic Division and 12th in the Eastern Conference. They're 4-3-3 over their last 10, with 29 goals for and 32 against. On the road this season they're 1-1-2.

Offensively, the Red Wings scored 2.55 goals a game this season (26th) on 29.8 shots (6th), converting 8.5% of them (30th). The power play ran at 21.1% (15th) on 3.46 chances a game (6th). That's 0.55 goals a game below the league average of 3.10. Without the puck they allowed 2.91 goals a game (9th) and 25.5 shots (9th), with a team save percentage of .886 (15th). The penalty kill worked at 65.8% (30th), shorthanded 3.46 times a game (23rd).

The crease is still open: three goalies on the roster and no starter reported yet.

The Red Wings are without Marco Kasper (upper body), Mason Appleton (undisclosed) and Simon Edvinsson (contract dispute) and have Daniil Tarasov listed day to day.

The profile: the Red Wings were top eight in the league in shots per game and five on five shot attempt share; bottom eight in goals per game, shooting percentage and penalty kill.

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Red Wings took 53.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 8th in the NHL) and the Oilers took 47.5% (23rd). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 55.0% to 47.3%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 50.9% to 51.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Red Wings took 52.8% of five on five goals and the Oilers 50.9%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 3.4 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Red Wings ran at 0.996 (7.7% shooting, .919 saves) and the Oilers at 1.025 (13.5%, .890). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Oilers were the heavier side, 26.0 hits per 60 minutes to 23.2; the Red Wings blocked 15.1 shots per 60 to 13.9 for the Oilers; in puck management the Red Wings had 4.3 takeaways and 14.6 giveaways per 60, the Oilers 3.2 and 15.8.
