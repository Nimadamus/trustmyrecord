Vegas Golden Knights breakdown

Team breakdown

The Golden Knights are 5-1-0 with 10 points, 1st in the Pacific Division and 1st in the Western Conference. They're 5-1-0 over their last 6, with 23 goals for and 14 against. They've won 4 straight. Last season they finished 39-26-17.

Offensively, the Golden Knights scored 3.67 goals a game this season (5th) on 33.0 shots (2nd), converting 11.1% of them (16th). The power play ran at 30.4% (5th) on 3.83 chances a game (7th). That's 0.66 goals a game above the league average of 3.01. Without the puck they allowed 2.33 goals a game (6th) and 24.0 shots (8th), with a team save percentage of .903 (11th). The penalty kill worked at 81.2% (15th), shorthanded 2.67 times a game (3rd).

The crease is still open: two goalies on the roster and no starter reported yet.

The Golden Knights are without Lukas Cormier (undisclosed).

The profile: the Golden Knights were top eight in the league in goals per game, goals allowed, shots per game and shots allowed.

Minnesota Wild breakdown

Team breakdown

The Wild are 3-1-1 with 7 points, 2nd in the Central Division and 6th in the Western Conference. They're 3-1-1 over their last 5, with 15 goals for and 9 against. Last season they finished 46-24-12.

Offensively, the Wild scored 3.00 goals a game this season (16th) on 29.0 shots (8th), converting 10.3% of them (22nd). The power play ran at 37.5% (2nd) on 3.20 chances a game (16th). Without the puck they allowed 1.80 goals a game (2nd) and 28.0 shots (20th), with a team save percentage of .936 (2nd). The penalty kill worked at 94.1% (2nd), shorthanded 3.40 times a game (17th). Their goaltending covered for a lot of shots against.

No starter is reported here either, with three goalies on the roster.

The Wild are without Brock Faber (skull), Filip Gustavsson (hip) and Nick Foligno (upper body) and have Marcus Foligno and Zach Bogosian listed day to day.

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Golden Knights took 56.5% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 5th in the NHL) and the Wild took 49.4% (17th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 58.4% to 50.2%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 55.3% to 53.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Golden Knights took 52.2% of five on five goals and the Wild 50.0%. The Golden Knights scored 4.3 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Golden Knights ran at 0.978 (7.8% shooting, .899 saves) and the Wild at 0.996 (6.5%, .931). A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Golden Knights.

The Wild were the heavier side, 21.5 hits per 60 minutes to 19.9; the Golden Knights blocked 11.4 shots per 60 to 13.8 for the Wild; in puck management the Golden Knights had 3.6 takeaways and 17.6 giveaways per 60, the Wild 3.7 and 17.3.

Deployment tilted too: the Golden Knights started 59.2% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 53.2% for the Wild.
