Utah Mammoth breakdown

Team breakdown

The Mammoth are 5-2-0 with 10 points, 1st in the Central Division and 1st in the Western Conference. They're 5-2-0 over their last 7, with 27 goals for and 19 against. On the road this season they're 3-2-0. Last season they finished 43-33-6.

Offensively, the Mammoth scored 3.86 goals a game this season (5th) on 31.3 shots (4th), converting 12.3% of them (12th). The power play ran at 34.5% (2nd) on 4.14 chances a game (4th). Five on five

At five on five this season, the Mammoth took 57.4% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 3rd in the NHL) and the Oilers took 47.8% (21st). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 58.9% to 47.4%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 57.7% to 54.6%.

The goals followed a different split: the Mammoth took 61.5% of five on five goals and the Oilers 63.3%. The Mammoth outscored their shot share by 4.2 points and the Oilers outscored their shot share by 15.5 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Mammoth ran at 1.012 (9.8% shooting, .915 saves) and the Oilers at 1.091 (18.6%, .905). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Mammoth's and the Oilers' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Mammoth were the heavier side, 27.1 hits per 60 minutes to 26.9; the Mammoth blocked 14.8 shots per 60 to 13.6 for the Oilers; in puck management the Mammoth had 4.0 takeaways and 16.6 giveaways per 60, the Oilers 3.5 and 16.4.

Deployment tilted too: the Mammoth started 53.5% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 49.0% for the Oilers.
