Team breakdown

Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Wild took 50.5% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 12th in the NHL) and the Bruins took 49.6% (18th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 52.0% to 50.0%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 51.1% to 48.0%.

The goals followed a different split: the Wild took 62.3% of five on five goals and the Bruins 64.5%. The Wild outscored their shot share by 11.8 points and the Bruins outscored their shot share by 14.9 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Wild ran at 1.031 (9.5% shooting, .936 saves) and the Bruins at 1.054 (12.1%, .933). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Wild's and the Bruins' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Wild were the heavier side, 23.0 hits per 60 minutes to 20.8; the Wild blocked 15.6 shots per 60 to 14.1 for the Bruins; in puck management the Wild had 4.2 takeaways and 15.6 giveaways per 60, the Bruins 4.1 and 16.6.
