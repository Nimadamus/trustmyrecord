Advanced stats

Five on five

At five on five this season, the Oilers took 47.8% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 22nd in the NHL) and the Mammoth took 56.2% (5th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 48.5% to 56.5%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 52.6% to 57.2%.

The goals followed a different split: the Oilers took 64.1% of five on five goals and the Mammoth 54.3%. The Oilers outscored their shot share by 16.3 points and the Mammoth scored 1.9 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Oilers ran at 1.088 (17.6% shooting, .912 saves) and the Mammoth at 0.990 (9.2%, .898). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Oilers' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000. A PDO under .990 usually says a club was unlucky, which points to better results ahead for the Mammoth.

The Mammoth were the heavier side, 27.7 hits per 60 minutes to 26.6; the Oilers blocked 14.6 shots per 60 to 14.1 for the Mammoth; in puck management the Oilers had 3.4 takeaways and 15.6 giveaways per 60, the Mammoth 3.8 and 16.3.
