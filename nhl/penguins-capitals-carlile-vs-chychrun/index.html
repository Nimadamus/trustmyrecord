Pittsburgh Penguins breakdown

Team breakdown

The Penguins are 2-1-0 with 4 points. So far they beat the Flyers 7-0, then beat the Canadiens 6-5 in overtime, then lost to the Jets 3-2. Last season they finished 41-25-16.

Offensively, the Penguins scored 3.54 goals a game in 2025-26 (3rd) on 28.6 shots (10th), converting 12.4% of them (4th). The power play ran at 24.1% (7th) on 2.83 chances a game (21st). That's 0.46 goals a game above the league average of 3.08. Without the puck they allowed 3.15 goals a game (24th) and 27.4 shots (12th), with a team save percentage of .885 (24th). Five on five

At five on five in 2025-26, the Penguins took 50.2% of the shot attempts (Corsi, 13th in the NHL) and the Capitals took 49.5% (15th). Counting only unblocked attempts (Fenwick) it was 50.5% to 50.2%. With the score close, where teams play straight up, the split was 50.0% to 49.7%.

The goals followed a different split: the Penguins took 53.7% of five on five goals and the Capitals 54.9%. The Penguins outscored their shot share by 3.6 points and the Capitals outscored their shot share by 5.4 points. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Penguins ran at 1.012 (11.1% shooting, .901 saves) and the Capitals at 1.018 (10.2%, .916). A PDO above 1.010 is hard to hold, so the Penguins' and the Capitals' results leaned on percentages that usually drift back toward 1.000.

The Capitals were the heavier side, 19.5 hits per 60 minutes to 17.3; the Penguins blocked 14.4 shots per 60 to 15.8 for the Capitals; in puck management the Penguins had 4.9 takeaways and 15.7 giveaways per 60, the Capitals 4.5 and 14.6.

Deployment tilted too: the Penguins started 50.4% of their five on five shifts in the offensive zone, against 48.0% for the Capitals.
