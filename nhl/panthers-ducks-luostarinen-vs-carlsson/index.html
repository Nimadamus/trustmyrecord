The goals followed a different split: the Panthers took 47.2% of five on five goals and the Ducks 47.4%. The Panthers scored 4.6 points below their shot share and the Ducks scored 4.8 points below their shot share. PDO, shooting percentage plus save percentage at five on five, explains most of that: the Panthers ran at 0.989 (9.6% shooting, .893 saves) and the Ducks at 0.981 (8.9%, .893).
