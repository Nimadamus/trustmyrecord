Descriptions

Copy any of these verbatim. They are written to be accurate rather than flattering, and they are kept in step with what is actually live.

One line

TMR Coin is the currency of sports competition on TrustMyRecord: a fixed supply token on Base that members earn by making winning picks and spend on the platform.

Short, about fifty words

TMR Coin is the currency of sports competition on TrustMyRecord, a platform that grades members' sports picks against completed results. Members earn TMR for graded wins, correct predictions and community activity, and spend it on reports, tipping and challenges. The token itself is a fixed supply ERC-20 on Base with no owner and no mint function.

Long, about one hundred and fifty words

TMR Coin is the currency of sports competition on TrustMyRecord. Sports picks communities have always kept score informally: who called the game, who was right, who owes who. TrustMyRecord grades picks against completed results, and TMR Coin turns that graded truth into a unit members earn by being right and spend inside the platform, on matchup reports, badges, tipping and member versus member challenges settled by the same grading engine. Alongside the on-site balance, TMR Coin exists as a fixed supply ERC-20 on Base at a verified contract with no owner, no admin role, no mint function, no pause, no blacklist and no upgrade path, so the supply cannot be increased and the code cannot be altered by anyone, including TrustMyRecord. A public Uniswap v3 market has been open since 17 August 2026. TrustMyRecord does not sell TMR and offers it as no kind of investment.

What TMR Coin Is, And Is Not

It is

  • The unit members earn for graded wins and spend on the platform
  • A fixed supply ERC-20 on Base with no owner and no mint function
  • Publicly tradable by anyone, on a market we do not control
  • The stake and the prize in member versus member challenges
  • Backed one for one by treasury tokens where it is earned on the site

It is not

  • An investment, or offered as one. No return or appreciation is promised or implied
  • Sold by TrustMyRecord. We take no payment for it and have never distributed tokens to members
  • Withdrawable today. An earned balance cannot be sent to a wallet or sold
  • A wager of real money. TrustMyRecord is not a sportsbook
  • A reason to connect a wallet to the site. We never ask for a signature or a seed phrase

Two things worth knowing before writing about the market: initial liquidity is very small, so trades of roughly twenty dollars or more will not fill, and the liquidity position is held by the treasury rather than locked or burned. Both are disclosed on every page where a price appears.
