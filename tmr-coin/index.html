TMR Coin is now a publicly traded cryptocurrency

TMR Coin is a real cryptocurrency, and from today it trades in public. There is a live pool on Base, a market price set by actual trading, and anyone with a wallet can buy or sell it without going through TrustMyRecord. It has a real cash value: TMR can be exchanged for ETH right now, at the price shown further down this page.

What that means in practice. The token is on Base at a verified contract with a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 and no mint function, so nobody can print more of it. It is not a promise, a voucher, or an IOU from us. It is a token you hold in your own wallet, and its price is whatever people are willing to pay for it at that moment.

Two things we want you to hear from us first

  • The market starts small on purpose. The pool holds roughly $17 of ETH, so that is about the most cash that can come out of it today, and trades of about $20 or more will not fill at all. This was opened with money the project already had rather than by asking anyone for funding, and it starts at the size that money allowed.
  • TMR you earn on the site is not the same as TMR in your own wallet. An earned balance lives in TrustMyRecord's records, which is what makes competing instant and free of gas. It is backed one for one by treasury reserves. On-chain withdrawal does not exist yet. Only the token you buy and hold yourself can be traded right now. If withdrawal ever becomes active you will read it here first.

What we are working on next

We are working to grow this properly, and the honest version of that is two specific things. Deeper liquidity, funded from TrustMyRecord revenue as it arrives rather than from anyone's pocket, so that larger trades can fill and the price moves less violently. And more real uses for TMR inside TrustMyRecord, so holding it does something beyond sitting in a wallet.

That is work on the liquidity and the product. It is not a promise about the price, and you should not read it as one. Nobody at TrustMyRecord controls what TMR trades at. It can go up, it can go to nothing, and you should never put in money you cannot afford to lose.

One TMR Economy, Two Places It Lives

TMR earned on TrustMyRecord is part of the same TMR Coin economy as the token on Base. What differs is where a balance sits today and what you can do with it right now.

In your account

TMR earned here

  • Earned by competing, spent on site features.
  • Instant and free to use. No wallet, no gas, no transaction to sign.
  • Backed by treasury reserves, one real TMR Coin set aside for each one earned.
  • There is no way to take it off the site. No withdrawal, no sale, no conversion. The ledger keeps the separate balance states a withdrawal system would need, but that system has not been built and would need a legal review first.
On Base

TMR Coin, on chain

  • A fixed supply ERC-20 you hold in your own wallet.
  • Transferable and tradable on a decentralised exchange today.
  • Has a market price, set entirely by whatever people trade at.
  • Bought with ETH, not earned on the site.
On-chain withdrawal to your own wallet does not exist. TrustMyRecord holds treasury reserves intended to back earned TMR one for one with real TMR Coin as the withdrawal system is built. That is a reserve of tokens, not a guaranteed dollar value: the market price is whatever people trade at, and the pool is small enough that the quoted value of all earned balances is not what everyone could sell for at once.

You can check the reserve yourself at any time: the reserve position is published live, computed from the ledger and from Base rather than stored.

How You Get TMR

There are exactly two ways. Both give you TMR, and what differs is where it sits and what you can do with it today.

Path one, free

Compete for TMR

  • 25 TMR for signing up, once.
  • 10 for your first sportsbook pick, once.
  • 5 for your first forum thread, once.
  • That is 40 TMR in total, and each one pays a single time. Four smaller repeating rewards sit on top, capped at 13 TMR a day between them.

  • Monthly competition prizes, shared from a capped pool. Winning one is the only way to earn a large amount.

Spend it on the site: BetLegend Pro reports at 5 each after the free daily one, a badge at 500, custom forum flair at 1,500, priority support at 3,000. Spending destroys the TMR you spend. Worth being straight with you: 40 TMR covers the reports, and nothing else on that list is reachable from the sign-up rewards alone.

Path two, costs money

Buy the token

  • Swap ETH for TMR on Base using the verified contract address below.
  • It sits in your own wallet. We never hold it and never see your keys.
  • You can sell it back to the pool at whatever the market price is.
  • The price is set by trading. It can fall to nothing.

Liquidity is very small right now, so keep trades small and leave your wallet's slippage protection on.

Earning TMR here does not put tokens in your wallet today, and buying tokens does not add to your site balance. Connecting the two is what a withdrawal system would do. None has been built. Until it is live, we will not describe your TMR as something you can cash out.

How To Buy or Hold TMR

TMR trades on a decentralised exchange, so there is no account to open and nothing to buy from TrustMyRecord. You will need a self-custody wallet and ETH on the Base network.

1
Get a walletAny self-custody wallet that supports Base, such as Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask, Rabby. You control the keys; TrustMyRecord never sees them and never needs them.
2
Switch to BaseBase is an Ethereum layer-2 network, chain ID 8453. Most wallets have it built in. Fees are typically a fraction of a cent.
3
Fund it with ETH on BaseBridge ETH to Base or buy it directly in your wallet. You need ETH both to swap and to pay network fees.
4
Swap using the verified addressPaste the contract address above into the exchange rather than searching by name. Check it character by character, fake tokens copy real names.
5
Add the token to your walletIf your balance does not appear, use your wallet's "import token" option with the same contract address, symbol TMR and 18 decimals.
6
Start small and set slippageLiquidity is very small, so keep trades small and leave your wallet's slippage protection on. It will refuse a trade that would fill at a bad price, which is exactly what you want here.

Risks, Please Read

  • You can lose everything you put in. Crypto prices are volatile. TMR could fall to effectively zero and stay there. Never spend money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Initial liquidity is very small, a few tens of dollars, not thousands. A small trade moves the price sharply, and trades of roughly $20 or more will simply not fill. Keep slippage protection on.
  • The liquidity is not locked. The pool position is held by the TrustMyRecord treasury and could be withdrawn. It is disclosed here rather than glossed over so you can weigh it.
  • The treasury holds the large majority of supply. Like any holder, it could sell. Nothing has been sold, and any movement is visible on-chain.
  • Nothing here is an investment offer. No return, profit, price increase or future listing is promised or implied. TrustMyRecord does not sell TMR and takes no payment for it.
  • On-chain withdrawal does not exist yet. Until it is live, TMR moves within TrustMyRecord. Reserves back every earned TMR one for one, which is a reserve of tokens rather than a guaranteed dollar value.
  • Verify the contract address every time, from this page. Fake tokens reuse real names and logos.
  • There is no official TMR Coin Telegram, Discord or chat group. None exists. If you were invited to one, it is not run by us. The only official surfaces are this website and the email address on it.
  • Nobody from TrustMyRecord will ever DM you an offer, ask you to connect a wallet to the site, ask for a seed phrase, or ask you to send funds. Anyone doing so is running a scam.
