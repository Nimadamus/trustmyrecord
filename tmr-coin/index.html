What Is TMR Coin?

TMR Coin is an internal rewards-points system for TrustMyRecord members. You earn points by taking part on the site — posting picks, starting forum threads, and so on — and you spend them on things inside the site.

Points are not a cryptocurrency, not an ERC-20 token, not an investment, not a cash-equivalent asset, and not a transferable fixed-supply token. They exist only in TrustMyRecord's own records. They are not currently redeemable for cash and are not traded on any exchange. The full terms are on the TMR Coin terms page.

Maximum issuance, and how it is enforced
Total issuance of TMR Coin points is capped at 1,000,000,000 units. The cap is enforced by the platform database itself: any award that would take total issuance past the cap is rejected outright, including when several awards land at the same moment.
TMR Coin points are not a cryptocurrency and are not sold. Nobody at TrustMyRecord will ever ask you to buy TMR Coin, send funds, connect a wallet, or pay gas. Any message, DM, or site offering to sell you TMR Coin is a scam.

About the Blockchain Contracts

TrustMyRecord has separately experimented with blockchain contracts bearing the TMR name. Those experiments are not connected to your points balance in any way.

  • No tokens have ever been distributed to any member, and none are offered.
  • There is no way to convert points into any token. No bridge, claim, swap, or withdrawal exists or is being built.
  • The contracts have no market, no liquidity, and no price.
  • Your points balance is unaffected by anything on any blockchain.

If that ever changes it would require legal review first, and it would be announced on this page before anything else happened.

Using TMR Coin Today

Points can be used inside TrustMyRecord to:

  • Tip another member for a good forum post.
  • Enter selected site contests.
  • Run additional BetLegend Pro reports, at 5 points each after the free daily one.
  • Unlock cosmetic badges, profile customization, and other non-financial perks as they are added to the rewards catalog.

Spending points destroys them — they are not transferred to TrustMyRecord and are not resold. Nothing here is a promise of financial return, price appreciation, or liquidity.

Risks
