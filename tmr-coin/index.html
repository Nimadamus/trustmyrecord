How to buy, hold or sellSeven steps, wallet and ETH on Base
  1. Get a wallet. Any self-custody wallet that supports Base, such as Coinbase Wallet, MetaMask or Rabby. You control the keys.
  2. Switch to Base. An Ethereum layer-2 network, chain ID 8453. Fees are typically a fraction of a cent.
  3. Fund it with ETH on Base. Bridge ETH or buy it in your wallet. You need ETH to swap and to pay network fees.
  4. Swap using the verified address. Open the trade link in the live market panel. Paste the contract address into the token search rather than searching by name, and check it character by character. KyberSwap may say it cannot estimate the price impact for TMR, so read the quote before you confirm.
  5. Add the token to your wallet. If your balance does not appear, import the same contract address, symbol TMR, 18 decimals.
  6. Start small and set slippage. Slippage protection refuses a trade that would fill at a bad price, which is exactly what you want.
  7. Selling. Same screen, TMR as the token you pay and ETH as the token you receive. Check the quote first.

Only the TMR in the pool can be bought. The rest is locked in standard OpenZeppelin contracts anyone can read. The vesting contract releases nothing until 25 October 2027, then evenly to the company Safe over 48 months, ending 4 October 2031. Any use of the reserve timelock must be announced on chain 30 days ahead. The pool position sits in a 365 day timelock with no admin and holds three ranges: TMR above the price, ETH below it, and one across the current price. The company Safe needs 2 of 3 signatures. Locked means delayed and public, not burned, and nothing here is a promise of price or value.

Site credits have their own ceiling of 250,000,000, which has nothing to do with the token supply.

All risks, please readNine things to know before you trade
  • You can lose everything you put in. Crypto prices are volatile. TMR could fall to effectively zero and stay there. Never spend money you cannot afford to lose.
  • Prices move fast. Check every quote before you confirm and keep slippage protection on.
  • The liquidity lock is a timelock, not a burn. The pool position can be withdrawn, but only by a call scheduled on chain in public 365 days in advance. Collecting pool fees goes through the same delay.
  • The company still owns most of the supply. It is locked, but from 25 October 2027 vested TMR reaches the company Safe, and any 30 day reserve use is announced on chain first. Like any holder, the company could then sell. Any movement is visible on chain.
  • Nothing here is an investment offer. No return, profit, price increase or future listing is promised or implied. Nobody at TrustMyRecord controls what TMR trades at, and TrustMyRecord does not sell TMR tokens or take payment for them.
  • Site credits are not TMR. They are not backed by tokens. TrustMyRecord records a cash reserve from credit revenue, which does not support or guarantee the market price and does not buy TMR. Card purchases of credits were turned off on 25 October 2026.
  • Verify the contract address every time, from this page. Fake tokens reuse real names and logos.
  • There is no official TMR Coin Telegram, Discord or chat group. If you were invited to one, it is not run by us. The only official surfaces are this website and the email address on it.
  • Nobody from TrustMyRecord will ever DM you an offer, ask you to connect a wallet to the site, ask for a seed phrase, or ask you to send funds. Anyone doing so is running a scam.
