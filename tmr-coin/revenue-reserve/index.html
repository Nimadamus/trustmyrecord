TMR Revenue Reserve

When members use the simulator credits they bought, half of what we keep after card fees goes to the business and half goes to a public TMR reserve. The reserve only buys TMR from people who choose to sell it, and everything it does is listed below with its Base transaction.

How It Works

50% of net revenue to the business 50% to the revenue reserve

Credits get used

A member buys simulator credits at a fixed dollar price and spends them on runs. Credits that have not been used are never counted, so they stay refundable.

Revenue is split

For every credit used, the net amount after card fees is split between the business and the reserve. Refunds and chargebacks reverse their share.

Bids wait below the price

The reserve converts its share to ETH and places small ETH only bids in the public pool, 3% to 30% below the current price. A bid fills only when someone outside chooses to sell TMR.

TMR is held for good

Filled bids move into the reserve wallet. TMR in the reserve is never sold, burned, lent or paid out.

What This Is, And What It Is Not

  • Half of the net revenue from simulator credits that members have used goes to the business; the other half is set aside for the TMR revenue reserve.
  • Money for credits that have not been used yet is never allocated, so it stays available for refunds.
  • The reserve buys TMR only from people who choose to sell it, through ETH only bids placed below the current price. It never trades against the company's own liquidity.
  • Filled bids are moved into the public revenue reserve wallet. TMR in that wallet is held permanently: it is not sold, burned, lent or used to pay anyone.
  • Every transaction is listed here with its Base transaction hash so anyone can check it.
  • The reserve is not an investment program. It gives no member a share of revenue, a return or any claim on the reserve, and TrustMyRecord makes no promise about the price of TMR.
  • Site credits are bought for simulator access at a fixed dollar price. They are not TMR tokens and cannot be withdrawn or sold.
