TMR Coin · Revenue policy

The cash reserve

Half of the net revenue from simulator credits that members use is set aside as a reserve in the TrustMyRecord business bank account. Each month is checked against the bank statement and posted here.

Recording

Three different things

Site credits

For simulator use

Site credits bought in the TrustMyRecord store pay for simulator runs. They are priced in US dollars, they are not money, and they can never be withdrawn or turned into TMR tokens.

Cash reserve

US dollars in a bank account

The cash reserve is US dollars that TrustMyRecord sets aside in its own business bank account from credit revenue.

TMR

A cryptocurrency token

TMR is a cryptocurrency token on Base. Site credits and the cash reserve are separate from it: no credit turns into TMR, and no TMR is withdrawn from the site today. The reserve does not support or guarantee the market price.

How the reserve is funded

When a member uses simulator credits they bought, half of the net revenue from those credits is set aside. Net means after the card fees our card processor actually charged.

Credits that have not been used are not counted. Unused purchased credits can be refunded on request, as the store terms describe.

If a purchase is refunded, in full or in part, or a card dispute is lost, its share comes back out. A refund larger than the share for its month is carried into the following months.

How each amount is calculated

Status. Recording since 23 October 2026, 4:37 AM Pacific. The split is fixed at 50% business and 50% reserve. The first monthly close is 2 November 2026. Until a month is closed and deposited, nothing has moved.

Value of one used credit. The amount paid for the pack, minus the card fee, divided by the credits in the pack. Example: a $10.00 pack of 500 credits with a $0.59 fee is worth $9.41 / 500 = 1.882 cents per credit.

Order of use. Free credits are used first, then bought credits from the oldest purchase. Only bought credits that were actually used count.

Monthly split. Used credits times their value gives the month's net. Half goes to the reserve; an odd half cent goes to the reserve. Refunds and lost card disputes are subtracted, and any excess is carried into later months.

Release. A month's reserve can be released only after its 120 day card dispute window has passed, so the earliest possible release is February 2027, and only for a purpose posted here first.

What it does not do

  • The reserve does not support or guarantee the market price.
  • The reserve does not buy, hold, sell or lock TMR, and it does not set any TMR price.
  • Nothing here is a promise that TMR, site credits or anything else will rise in value.
  • The reserve does not make TMR or site credits easier to sell, and nobody is promised a buyer.
  • Site credits cannot be exchanged for money from the reserve, and the reserve is not a fund anyone can withdraw from.
  • It is not an investment. It pays nothing to members, and it gives no member a share of revenue or any claim on it.

Who owns it, and what it may be used for

The account and the money in it belong to TrustMyRecord. It is not held in trust or escrow for members, and members have no claim on it.

The money stays in the reserve account. It can be released only for a purpose TrustMyRecord approves separately, and any release is posted here before it happens.

How you can check it

Each month, closed on Pacific time, we post what was set aside, what was deposited, the closing bank balance, and whether they match.

For each bank statement we post a fingerprint (SHA 256). Anyone later shown that statement can confirm it is the same document.
