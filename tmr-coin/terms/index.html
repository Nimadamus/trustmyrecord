TMR Coin Terms & Disclosures

These terms govern TMR you hold on TrustMyRecord. TMR Coin is a fixed supply ERC-20 token on Base; TrustMyRecord holds your TMR in its accounting ledger so site activity is instant and gasless. On-chain withdrawal to a wallet you control is being activated. TMR is not offered as an investment.

In short

You earn TMR by taking part on TrustMyRecord, and you spend it on things inside TrustMyRecord. That is the whole of what these terms cover. TrustMyRecord holds that TMR for you in its accounting ledger. On-chain withdrawal to your own wallet is being activated. TrustMyRecord separately published a TMR Coin token on the Base blockchain which anyone may hold and trade in their own wallet: it is a different thing, nothing here gives you a claim on it, and there is no conversion between the two in either direction.

The terms

What these terms cover

These terms cover the TMR balance recorded in your TrustMyRecord account, which you earn by taking part on the site and spend on the site. TrustMyRecord separately published a TMR Coin token on the Base blockchain, which anyone may hold and trade in their own wallet. That token is not governed by these terms, is not sold by TrustMyRecord, and is not connected to your balance: there is no deposit, withdrawal, conversion or redemption between the two in either direction. Everything below describes the balance on the site.

No guaranteed monetary value

TMR Coin has no guaranteed monetary value. It is a record of participation on TrustMyRecord and nothing more. TrustMyRecord does not represent that TMR Coin has, will have, or will come to have any cash value.

Not an investment

TMR Coin is not an investment, a security, a share, a stake in TrustMyRecord, or a claim on any of its revenue, assets, or profits. Holding TMR Coin gives you no ownership rights and no expectation of profit. Nothing on this site should be read as an offer to sell, or a solicitation to buy, any financial instrument.

The balance on this site cannot be purchased

The TMR balance in your account cannot be purchased. There is no price for it, no sale, no presale, and no way to exchange money for it. It is only earned through participation on the site, or granted by an administrator. TrustMyRecord does not sell the on-chain token either, and will never contact you offering to sell you anything.

Cannot currently be cashed out

On-chain withdrawal to a wallet you control is being activated and is not live yet. Until it is, TMR moves within TrustMyRecord. TrustMyRecord does not operate a cash payout and does not exchange TMR for fiat currency.

Not transferable outside the site

TMR Coin exists only inside TrustMyRecord and cannot be moved off it. The only transfers permitted are the approved in-site functions described in these terms, such as tipping another member for a post. It cannot be sent to a wallet, an exchange, another platform, or another person outside those functions.

Withdrawal status

TMR Coin on Base is a real token that trades publicly. TMR held in your TrustMyRecord account is backed one for one by reserves and on-chain withdrawal is being activated; until that is live, TrustMyRecord does not exchange TMR for fiat currency and gives no way to convert into it. TrustMyRecord sets treasury tokens aside against earned balances as a matter of policy, which is a reserve of tokens rather than a guarantee of value or an entitlement to redeem.

May be modified or discontinued

TrustMyRecord may change the reward rules, the amounts, the ways TMR Coin can be used, or discontinue TMR Coin entirely, at any time and for any reason, subject to these terms. Balances may be adjusted or removed where they were earned through error, duplicate awards, automation, or abuse.

Earned through participation, not owed

Reward amounts and eligibility limits are set by TrustMyRecord and are published on the rewards page. Meeting an activity condition is not a contractual entitlement to a payment; awards are made under the published rules and the anti-abuse limits described below.

Anti-abuse and anti-self-dealing

Awards are subject to per-rule daily, weekly, lifetime, and cooldown limits, and to automated abuse detection. Using multiple accounts, automation, reciprocal tipping between accounts you control, or any other scheme to inflate a balance may result in reversal of the affected awards, a frozen wallet, or loss of site access.

Separate from paid subscriptions

Paid TrustMyRecord subscriptions and products are bought and sold in ordinary dollars and are entirely separate from TMR Coin. No paid subscription requires you to acquire, hold, or spend TMR Coin, and TMR Coin cannot be used to pay for one.

Your record

Every change to your balance is written to a permanent, append-only record: what happened, when, how much, and what your balance was before and after. That record cannot be edited or deleted after the fact. You can read your full history, and download it, from your wallet.

If you ask us to delete your account, that record is removed along with it.
