TMR Coin Terms & Disclosures

These terms govern site credits on TrustMyRecord: the internal balance in your TrustMyRecord wallet. TMR Coin is a separate fixed supply ERC-20 cryptocurrency on Base. Site credits cannot be withdrawn and cannot be converted into TMR Coin. Neither is offered as an investment.

Version 1.3.0 Effective 29 October 2026 TMR Coin overview Your wallet

In short

You earn site credits by taking part on TrustMyRecord, and you use them inside TrustMyRecord for simulator runs, tips and the marketplace. That is the whole of what these terms cover. Site credits cannot be withdrawn. TrustMyRecord separately published TMR Coin, a token on the Base blockchain which anyone may hold in their own wallet: it is a different thing, nothing here gives you a claim on it, and nothing converts one into the other in either direction.

The terms

What these terms cover

These terms cover the site credits recorded in your TrustMyRecord account, which you earn by taking part on the site and use on the site. TrustMyRecord separately published a TMR Coin token on the Base blockchain, which anyone may hold in their own wallet. That token is not governed by these terms, is not sold by TrustMyRecord, and is not connected to your balance: there is no deposit, withdrawal, conversion or redemption between the two in either direction. Everything below describes the balance on the site.

No guaranteed monetary value

Site credits have no guaranteed monetary value. TrustMyRecord does not represent that site credits or TMR Coin have, will have, or will come to have any cash value.

Not an investment

Neither site credits nor TMR Coin is an investment, a security, a share, an ownership interest in TrustMyRecord, or a claim on any of its revenue, assets, or profits. Holding either gives you no ownership rights and no expectation of profit. Nothing on this site should be read as an offer to sell, or a solicitation to buy, any financial instrument.

Card purchases of site credits are closed

Site credits were sold by card at $0.02 per credit ($5 for 250, $10 for 500, $25 for 1,250) until card purchases were turned off on 25 October 2026. Purchases are currently closed. Today site credits are only earned through participation on the site, or granted by an administrator. TrustMyRecord does not sell the on-chain token, and will never contact you offering to sell you anything.

Cannot currently be cashed out

Withdrawals are off and no withdrawal has ever been made. Site credits stay within TrustMyRecord. Redemption and cash redemption are off: TrustMyRecord does not operate a cash payout and does not exchange site credits for money.

Not transferable outside the site

Site credits exist only inside TrustMyRecord and cannot be moved off them. The only transfers permitted are the approved in-site functions described in these terms, such as tipping another member for a post. It cannot be sent to a wallet, an exchange, another platform, or another person outside those functions.

Not backed by TMR Coin, and the cash reserve

Site credits are not backed by TMR Coin and cannot be converted into it. Deposits, withdrawals, transfers, claims and redemption are all off. Separately, TrustMyRecord records a cash reserve from the net revenue of bought credits when they are used. The reserve does not support or guarantee the market price, does not buy TMR, and gives no entitlement to redeem. Details are on the cash reserve page.

May be modified or discontinued

TrustMyRecord may change the reward rules, the amounts, the ways site credits can be used, or discontinue site credits entirely, at any time and for any reason, subject to these terms. Balances may be adjusted or removed where they were earned through error, duplicate awards, automation, or abuse.

Earned through participation, not owed

Reward amounts and eligibility limits are set by TrustMyRecord and are published on the rewards page. Meeting an activity condition is not a contractual entitlement to a payment; awards are made under the published rules and the anti-abuse limits described below.

Anti-abuse and anti-self-dealing

Awards are subject to per-rule daily, weekly, lifetime, and cooldown limits, and to automated abuse detection. Using multiple accounts, automation, reciprocal tipping between accounts you control, or any other scheme to inflate a balance may result in reversal of the affected awards, a frozen wallet, or loss of site access.

Separate from paid subscriptions

Paid TrustMyRecord subscriptions and products are bought and sold in ordinary dollars and are entirely separate from site credits and TMR Coin. No paid subscription requires you to acquire, hold, or spend either, and neither can be used to pay for one.

Your record
