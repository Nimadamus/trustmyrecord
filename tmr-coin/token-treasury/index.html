How money reaches the treasury

A share of the net revenue from site credits that members have used goes to the cash reserve, a US dollar bank account. Credits not yet used are never counted.

A release from the reserve is converted to ETH at an exchange and sent to the treasury Safe on Base. The bank reference, the exchange receipt and the transfer are listed below.

The Safe places ETH bids below the market price. A bid buys TMR only when someone chooses to sell at that price. Collected TMR stays in the Safe and is shown here.

Paying with TMR

Paying with TMR is not open.

When it opens, members can pay for TMR Pro with TMR from their own linked wallet. The amount of TMR is fixed for 15 minutes and is worked out from what the pool would pay for it. TMR received this way is listed here by month.

What it does not do

  • The treasury does not set, support or promise any TMR price, and nothing here says TMR will rise in value.
  • Bids are liquidity. If the price moves back through a bid before it is collected, the bid sells that TMR again.
  • The treasury never trades against TrustMyRecord's own liquidity: its bids may not overlap the company's own ranges.
  • Site credits cannot be exchanged for TMR or for money through the treasury.
  • It is not an investment. It pays nothing to members, and holding TMR gives no share of TrustMyRecord or any claim on revenue.
