The reserve report counts every active account of type real, which is a slightly wider scope than the participation figures above, so the two member balance totals differ by a small amount. Both are computed rather than stored, and neither is rounded to flatter the other.
A reserve is a designation of tokens, not a dollar guarantee, and blockchain withdrawal is not active, so an earned balance cannot be sent to an outside wallet or sold today.
