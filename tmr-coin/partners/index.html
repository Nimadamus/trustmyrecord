The One Line Version

TMR Coin is the currency of sports competition. Sports picking communities already run on scorekeeping: who called it, who was right, who owes who. That settlement layer has always been informal. TMR Coin makes it a real balance you earn by being right, spend on the platform, and hold as a fixed supply token on Base that nobody can print more of.

TrustMyRecord is the first place it runs. Members post picks, the platform grades them against completed results, and winning picks pay TMR. The same unit backs on site rewards, tipping between members, and paid features. The token itself is public infrastructure: an ordinary ERC-20 with no owner, no admin and no ability for anyone, including us, to freeze, tax or mint it.

Why A Sports Community Would Use It

Competition that settles itselfPicks are graded against completed results, and the reward is paid by the same system that does the grading. No spreadsheet, no honour system, no chasing anyone for a payout.
Member to member, not house against playerThe interesting unit in a picks community is one member challenging another. TMR is the stake, the scoreboard and the prize in that exchange, and the platform is the referee rather than the counterparty.
Tipping a good callSomeone posts a winner and the room wants to say thank you with something better than a like. Tipping between members is live on TrustMyRecord today.
A record worth carryingReputation in this space is trapped inside whichever platform issued it. A fixed supply token on a public chain is portable in a way that a leaderboard row is not.
Access and perksReports, badges, flair and entry to selected contests are already priced in TMR, which gives an ordinary member a reason to earn it that has nothing to do with speculation.
Neutral railsThe contract has no owner and no privileged function, so a partner integrating it is not depending on our goodwill to keep balances usable.
