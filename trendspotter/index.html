    Trend Spotter describes what has already happened in completed games. It is not a prediction and not a betting recommendation.

    How Trend Spotter works

    1. Pick a matchup from today's slate, or search any team.
    2. Choose a market and the conditions you care about — venue, favorite or underdog, line range, date range.
    3. Run the trend. You get the record, units, ROI and every game behind the number.

    Results come from completed games with verified final scores, settled at the closing line each game actually carried. Trend Spotter never re-grades history at a line you type in, and never invents a price it does not have.
