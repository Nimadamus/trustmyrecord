What TMR Match is

A peer-to-peer betting board. You post the bet you want — game, market, selection, price in American odds and the TMR you are putting up — and it sits there until a member takes the other side. No house, no book setting the number.

Taken in full or in part. Post 500 TMR, a taker wants 200, the other 300 stays open. Your TMR is held at post time, so every offer on the board is funded, and anything nobody takes comes back when you withdraw it.

Held in escrow once matched, until the game settles, then paid in full to the winner. No fee on a match and no juice past the price the two of you agreed. Pushes and postponed games return both sides.

Where the money is shows the TMR sitting behind each pick and price, so you can read which side the board is leaning on before you post. Everything settles in TMR held on TrustMyRecord and is not redeemable off site.
