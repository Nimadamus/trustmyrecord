How NHL competition works on TrustMyRecord

TrustMyRecord is built on one rule: a record only counts when somebody other than the person claiming it agrees. NHL challenges work the same way. You create a challenge on NHL 26 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox, an opponent accepts it, you both play it on your own hardware, and one of you reports the final score. It becomes official when the other player confirms it, or when an admin verifies it after a dispute.

Until that confirmation lands, the result does not exist as far as the site is concerned. It does not move the rankings, it does not touch your win-loss record, and it does not appear in your head-to-head history. That is the difference between a NHL record here and a NHL record anywhere somebody can simply type one in.

What an NHL result records

A confirmed NHL result stores the final score and a match stat line for both sides:

  • Goals
  • Shots on goal
  • Saves
  • Hits
  • Power play

Shots and saves are the fields that tell you which kind of game it was. A 2-1 win on forty-one shots against a goalie having the night of his life is a different result from a 2-1 win on nine shots, and the record keeps enough to tell them apart.

Formats you can play

Single game

One game, one result. The quickest way onto the NHL board.

Best-of series

Three, five or seven games against the same opponent as one challenge, with a running series score.

Playoff series

A best-of-seven run against one opponent, tracked from game one to elimination.

Online leagues

Round-robin league play with public standings built from confirmed results only. Start one from the leagues page.

Overtime, shootouts and how a result is recorded

Agree up front whether ties go to overtime, to a shootout, or stand as a draw in league play, and put it in the challenge rules. A result is recorded as the final score after whatever tiebreak you agreed, so a shootout win is a win on the board. If the two players report different finals, the challenge goes to admin review with screenshot proof and stays off the rankings until it is settled.

Predictions on NHL matches

You do not have to own the game to take part in this section. Any member can make a free Arena Pick on a scheduled NHL challenge before it starts, using free Arena Points. Call the winner correctly and your points go up; miss and they go down. Predictions lock at match start, you can never predict on your own match, and prediction records are tracked separately from sports pick records. Arena Points have no cash value, cannot be purchased and cannot be withdrawn.

NHL gaming questions

Which platforms can I play NHL challenges on?

NHL 26 challenges run on PlayStation 5 and Xbox. The platform is set when the challenge is created and shown on the open board.

Can I run an NHL league?

Yes. Online leagues run on a round-robin schedule with public standings built from confirmed results. As commissioner you set the player cap and the season start date.

Can I play NHL for money here?

No. There is no cash entry, no prize pool and no wagering. Challenges are played for bragging rights and for a verified record.

Do shootout wins count?

Yes, if that is what you agreed in the challenge rules. The result recorded is the final score after the tiebreak both players accepted.

Free to play, no wagering

NHL competition on TrustMyRecord is free to play. No real-money wagering, no deposits, no payouts and no betting odds on gaming challenges. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn. No money is wagered.
