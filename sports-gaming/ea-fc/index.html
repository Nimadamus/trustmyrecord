How EA Sports FC competition works on TrustMyRecord

TrustMyRecord is built on one rule: a record only counts when somebody other than the person claiming it agrees. EA Sports FC challenges work the same way. You create a challenge on EA FC 26 for PlayStation 5, Xbox and PC, an opponent accepts it, you both play it on your own hardware, and one of you reports the final score. It becomes official when the other player confirms it, or when an admin verifies it after a dispute.

Until that confirmation lands, the result does not exist as far as the site is concerned. It does not move the rankings, it does not touch your win-loss record, and it does not appear in your head-to-head history. That is the difference between a EA Sports FC record here and a EA Sports FC record anywhere somebody can simply type one in.

What an EA Sports FC result records

A confirmed EA FC result stores the scoreline and a match stat line for both sides:

  • Goals
  • Shots
  • Possession
  • Passes
  • Fouls

Possession and shots are what separate a deserved 1-0 from a smash and grab, and both are kept. Over a season of challenges the difference between a player who wins on chances and a player who wins on finishing shows up in the numbers rather than in the argument afterwards.

Formats you can play

Single match

One match, one result. The standard EA FC challenge and the fastest way onto the board.

Two-leg tie

Home and away against the same opponent as one challenge, with the aggregate tracked.

Best-of series

Three, five or seven matches against the same opponent, kept as a single series score.

Online leagues

Round-robin league play with public standings built from confirmed results. Start one from the leagues page.

Squad rules belong in the challenge, not in the chat

EA FC arguments usually start with squads. Set the terms when you create the challenge: whether it is club teams or national teams, any rating cap, whether custom squads are allowed, half length and difficulty. The agreed terms sit on the public challenge page, so they cannot be renegotiated after a 3-0 first half and an admin reviewing a dispute can see exactly what both players accepted.

Predictions on EA Sports FC matches

You do not have to own the game to take part in this section. Any member can make a free Arena Pick on a scheduled EA Sports FC challenge before it starts, using free Arena Points. Call the winner correctly and your points go up; miss and they go down. Predictions lock at match start, you can never predict on your own match, and prediction records are tracked separately from sports pick records. Arena Points have no cash value, cannot be purchased and cannot be withdrawn.

EA Sports FC gaming questions

Which platforms can I play EA FC challenges on?

EA FC 26 challenges run on PlayStation 5, Xbox and PC. The platform is chosen when the challenge is created and shown on the open board.

Can I play a two-leg tie?

Yes. Set the challenge to a best-of format and play home and away against the same opponent. The tie is tracked as one challenge with a running aggregate.

Can I play EA Sports FC for money here?

No. There is no cash entry, no prize pool and no wagering. Challenges are played for bragging rights and for a verified record.

What stops someone claiming a win they did not get?

The losing player has to confirm the result, or an admin has to verify it with screenshot proof. An unconfirmed result never reaches the rankings.

Free to play, no wagering

EA Sports FC competition on TrustMyRecord is free to play. No real-money wagering, no deposits, no payouts and no betting odds on gaming challenges. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn. No money is wagered.
