How MLB The Show competition works on TrustMyRecord

TrustMyRecord is built on one rule: a record only counts when somebody other than the person claiming it agrees. MLB The Show challenges work the same way. You create a challenge on MLB The Show 26 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox, an opponent accepts it, you both play it on your own hardware, and one of you reports the final score. It becomes official when the other player confirms it, or when an admin verifies it after a dispute.

Until that confirmation lands, the result does not exist as far as the site is concerned. It does not move the rankings, it does not touch your win-loss record, and it does not appear in your head-to-head history. That is the difference between a MLB The Show record here and a MLB The Show record anywhere somebody can simply type one in.

What a MLB The Show result records

MLB The Show is the one title on TrustMyRecord that carries a complete box score rather than a final score alone. A confirmed result stores:

  • Runs, hits and errors for both sides
  • A line score, inning by inning
  • Home runs and walks
  • Strikeouts and pitching lines

That is why the MLB The Show board reads like a real league table instead of a list of scorelines. A ten-game rivalry has ten box scores behind it, and every one of them was confirmed by the player who lost.

Formats you can play

Single game

One game, one result. The quickest way onto the board and the format most open challenges use.

Best-of series

Three, five or seven games against the same opponent, tracked as one challenge with a running series score.

Online leagues

Round-robin league play with public standings built from confirmed results: wins, losses and run differential. Start one from the leagues page.

Stat league

Season-long play with uploaded box scores and full batting and pitching stats, run separately in the MLB The Show Stat League.

The MLB The Show Stat League

Beyond one-off challenges, TrustMyRecord runs a season-long MLB The Show Stat League. Players upload box scores after each game, and the league keeps cumulative batting and pitching stats on top of the win-loss standings, so a season produces a stat line and not just a record. It is free to join and open to PlayStation and Xbox players.

Predictions on MLB The Show matches

You do not have to own the game to take part in this section. Any member can make a free Arena Pick on a scheduled MLB The Show challenge before it starts, using free Arena Points. Call the winner correctly and your points go up; miss and they go down. Predictions lock at match start, you can never predict on your own match, and prediction records are tracked separately from sports pick records. Arena Points have no cash value, cannot be purchased and cannot be withdrawn.

MLB The Show gaming questions

Which platforms can I play MLB The Show challenges on?

MLB The Show 26 challenges run on PlayStation 5 and Xbox. You pick the platform when you create the challenge, and it is shown on the open board so an opponent knows before they accept.

Do I have to upload a box score?

For a standard head-to-head challenge you only need the final score, confirmed by your opponent. Box score fields are available on MLB The Show and are required in the MLB The Show Stat League.

Can I play MLB The Show for money here?

No. There is no cash entry, no prize pool and no wagering. Challenges are played for bragging rights and for a verified record.

How do MLB The Show rankings work?

Rankings are built from confirmed results only, and can be sorted by wins or win percentage. A reported result that the opponent has not confirmed does not move anyone on the board.

Free to play, no wagering

MLB The Show competition on TrustMyRecord is free to play. No real-money wagering, no deposits, no payouts and no betting odds on gaming challenges. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn. No money is wagered.
