What sports gaming means on TrustMyRecord

TrustMyRecord exists because a claimed record is worth nothing and a confirmed one is worth something. The Arena applies that rule to sports video games. Anyone can say they never lose at Madden. Far fewer people will post an open challenge, play the game, and let the loser confirm the score in public.

That is the whole product. Pick a game, set the terms, find an opponent, play it on your own console, report the result. Your opponent confirms it or disputes it. Confirmed games build a public gaming record that sits on your profile next to your sports pick record, and they roll up into player rankings, head-to-head rivalry histories and league standings. Nothing counts until somebody other than you agrees it happened.

How a gaming challenge works

  1. Post it or accept itCreate an open challenge anyone can take, or call out a specific member. Set the game, the console, the series length and any house rules up front, so there is nothing to argue about later.
  2. Agree the termsThe opponent accepts or declines. Once accepted, the challenge is locked to the rules you both signed off on and it appears on the public board with a scheduled time.
  3. Play it on your own consoleTrustMyRecord does not host the game. You play it where you already play it, on PlayStation, Xbox or PC, then come back with the score.
  4. Report and confirmOne player reports the final score. The opponent confirms it, or disputes it and it goes to admin review with screenshot proof. Only confirmed and admin-verified results count.
  5. It becomes part of your recordThe result updates your wins, losses, win percentage and current streak, your head-to-head record against that specific opponent, and the ranking table for that game.

What gets tracked

Records and rankings

Wins, losses, win percentage and current streak, per game and overall. The gaming rankings board sorts by wins or win percentage and is built only from confirmed results.

Rivalries and head-to-head

Every pairing keeps its own history. Play the same opponent five times and the site holds the series score, not five loose results.

Box scores and stats

MLB The Show carries a full box score: innings, runs, hits, home runs, walks, strikeouts and errors. NBA 2K and Madden carry player stat lines.

Leagues and standings

Online gaming leagues run on a round-robin schedule with standings computed from confirmed results only: wins, losses and differential.

Free predictions, no wagering

All of it is free. There is no cash entry, no prize pool and no wagering on gaming challenges. If you are not the one playing, you can still call the winner: free Arena Pick predictions let you put free Arena Points on somebody else's match before it starts. Arena Points have no cash value, cannot be purchased and cannot be withdrawn, and nobody can predict on their own match.

Sports gaming is not a handicapper challenge

TrustMyRecord runs three kinds of head-to-head competition and they are deliberately kept apart. If you came looking for one of the other two, these are the right pages.

Handicapper Challenges

Pick against pick on real sporting events. Two handicappers each post picks over a set window and the graded records decide it. No video games involved.

TMR Challenges

Member against member on a graded outcome, with TMR Coin staked by both sides and held until it settles. Explained in full on peer-to-peer sports challenges.

Questions about sports gaming on TrustMyRecord

What is sports gaming on TrustMyRecord?

Head-to-head sports video game competition between members. You challenge someone in MLB The Show, Madden NFL, NBA 2K, EA Sports FC or NHL, play the match on your own console, report the score, and the result becomes part of a public record once your opponent confirms it.

Which sports video games are supported?

MLB The Show 26, Madden NFL 26, NBA 2K26, EA FC 26 and NHL 26. MLB The Show carries full box scores. NBA 2K and Madden carry player stat lines.

Can I play for money?

No. There is no cash entry, no cash prize and no wagering. Challenges are played for bragging rights and for the record. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value.

How is a result verified?

One player reports it and the opponent confirms it. A disagreement goes to admin review with screenshot proof. Unconfirmed results never touch the rankings.

Do I need a console to take part?

To play a challenge, yes. To follow the section, no. Browsing challenges, reading rankings and rivalry records, and making free predictions are open to any member.

Free to play, no wagering

TrustMyRecord sports gaming is free to play. No real-money wagering, no deposits, no payouts and no betting odds on gaming challenges. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn. No money is wagered.
