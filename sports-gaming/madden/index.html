How Madden NFL competition works on TrustMyRecord

TrustMyRecord is built on one rule: a record only counts when somebody other than the person claiming it agrees. Madden NFL challenges work the same way. You create a challenge on Madden NFL 26 for PlayStation 5, Xbox and PC, an opponent accepts it, you both play it on your own hardware, and one of you reports the final score. It becomes official when the other player confirms it, or when an admin verifies it after a dispute.

Until that confirmation lands, the result does not exist as far as the site is concerned. It does not move the rankings, it does not touch your win-loss record, and it does not appear in your head-to-head history. That is the difference between a Madden NFL record here and a Madden NFL record anywhere somebody can simply type one in.

What a Madden result records

A confirmed Madden NFL result stores the final score and a team stat line for both sides:

  • Points scored
  • Total yards
  • First downs
  • Turnovers
  • Time of possession

Those five fields are enough to settle most Madden arguments on their own. A 34-31 win with a five-minute time of possession deficit and three takeaways is a different game from a 34-31 win where you held the ball for thirty-eight minutes, and the record keeps both.

Formats you can play

Single game

One game, one result. The fastest route onto the Madden board.

Best-of series

Three, five or seven games against the same opponent as one challenge, with a running series score.

Online leagues

Round-robin league play with public standings built from confirmed results: wins, losses and point differential. Start one from the leagues page.

Rivalry runs

Play the same opponent repeatedly and the site keeps the head-to-head series rather than a pile of separate games.

Madden rules are agreed before kickoff, not after

Most Madden disputes are really rules disputes. The challenge form is where you settle them: quarter length, skill level, playbook restrictions, whether accelerated clock is on, house rules on going for it. Whatever you both accept is attached to the challenge and visible on the public challenge page, so if a result is disputed, an admin has the terms in front of them and does not have to take anyone's word for what was agreed.

Madden NFL gaming questions

Which platforms can I play Madden challenges on?

Madden NFL 26 challenges run on PlayStation 5, Xbox and PC. You choose the platform when you create the challenge and it is shown on the open board.

Can I run a full Madden league here?

Yes. Online leagues run on a round-robin schedule with public standings built from confirmed results only. You set the player cap and the season start date as commissioner.

Can I play Madden for money on TrustMyRecord?

No. There is no cash entry, no prize pool and no wagering. Madden challenges are played for bragging rights and for a verified record.

What happens if we disagree about the score?

Either player can dispute a reported result. The challenge goes to admin review with screenshot proof, and nothing counts toward rankings until it is resolved.

Free to play, no wagering

Madden NFL competition on TrustMyRecord is free to play. No real-money wagering, no deposits, no payouts and no betting odds on gaming challenges. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn. No money is wagered.
