How NBA 2K competition works on TrustMyRecord

TrustMyRecord is built on one rule: a record only counts when somebody other than the person claiming it agrees. NBA 2K challenges work the same way. You create a challenge on NBA 2K26 for PlayStation 5, Xbox and PC, an opponent accepts it, you both play it on your own hardware, and one of you reports the final score. It becomes official when the other player confirms it, or when an admin verifies it after a dispute.

Until that confirmation lands, the result does not exist as far as the site is concerned. It does not move the rankings, it does not touch your win-loss record, and it does not appear in your head-to-head history. That is the difference between a NBA 2K record here and a NBA 2K record anywhere somebody can simply type one in.

What an NBA 2K result records

NBA 2K carries a fuller stat line than most titles here. A confirmed result stores, for both sides:

  • Points, rebounds and assists
  • Steals, blocks and turnovers
  • Field goal percentage
  • Three point percentage

Shooting splits are the part most 2K players actually argue about, so they are kept alongside the result. A win on 32 percent from three reads differently from a win on 52 percent, and over a run of games the splits say more about a player than the record does.

Formats you can play

Single game

One game, one result. The quickest way onto the NBA 2K board.

Best-of series

Three, five or seven games against the same opponent as one challenge, with a running series score.

Online leagues

Round-robin league play with public standings built from confirmed results only. Start one from the leagues page.

Rivalry runs

Repeat matchups keep their own head-to-head history instead of dissolving into the overall record.

Rankings that are hard to inflate

An NBA 2K ranking is only worth reading if it is difficult to pad. Two rules do most of that work here. A result counts only when the losing player confirms it or an admin verifies it, so you cannot post wins against nobody. And head-to-head history is kept per pairing, so beating the same opponent twenty times shows up as a twenty game series against one player rather than twenty wins against the field. The rankings board can be read by wins or by win percentage for exactly that reason.

NBA 2K gaming questions

Which platforms can I play NBA 2K challenges on?

NBA 2K26 challenges run on PlayStation 5, Xbox and PC. The platform is set when the challenge is created and shown on the open board.

Are player stats tracked for NBA 2K?

Yes. Points, rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, turnovers, field goal percentage and three point percentage are all recorded on a confirmed result.

Can I play NBA 2K for money here?

No. There is no cash entry, no prize pool and no wagering. Challenges are played for bragging rights and for a verified record.

How do I get on the NBA 2K rankings?

Win a confirmed challenge. Rankings are built from confirmed results only, so a single verified win puts you on the board.

Free to play, no wagering

NBA 2K competition on TrustMyRecord is free to play. No real-money wagering, no deposits, no payouts and no betting odds on gaming challenges. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn. No money is wagered.
