What an MLB Game File covers

Starting pitching

Season line, recent form, repertoire and usage, and the platoon split that actually matters against the lineup in front of them — not just an ERA next to a name.

Bullpen state

Who threw yesterday, who threw three of the last four, and which high-leverage arms are realistically available tonight.

Lineups and handedness

Confirmed lineups when they post, projected before that, and clearly labelled either way. Handedness splits are shown with their plate appearances.

Sample size, always

A 5-plate-appearance batter-versus-pitcher history is presented as trivia, because that is what it is. A 500-PA split is presented as evidence. We do not let the two look alike.
