    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Where do the pending picks come from?

    From public posts by handicappers TrustMyRecord tracks on Covers, X, TheRX, Reddit and other sites. We grade every pick they post against the final score, so each one on the board comes with that handicapper's real record and units. Handicappers are shown by tracking number, never by name. Only games that have not started are shown.

    Are TrustMyRecord members' picks on the board?

    No. Members' picks stay private until their games start, for everyone, including TMR Pro subscribers.

    Does TMR Pro change how my record looks?

    No. A subscription buys research tools and nothing else. It does not change your verification, your badges, your public standing, how your picks are graded, how many picks you can post, or how you rank on any leaderboard. Nobody can buy credibility here; the whole point of the site is that the record speaks for itself.

    Do I need TMR Pro to use TrendSpotter?

    No. The record, the sample size, the win rate, the date range, the season splits and the qualifying games are all free, so you can check any trend yourself. TMR Pro adds the profitability side: units, ROI, average price, the market-implied rate and the deeper charts.

    Is Model Builder a backtester?

    Not in the main sense. A model you build starts tracking from that moment and measures results forward as games settle, which is what makes the record honest. There is a separate historical preview component that looks backwards; that one is the only part doing anything retrospective.

    Is Bet Legend Pro the same thing I can buy on its own?

    Yes, the same tool. It is sold separately at $49 a month, and a TMR Pro subscription includes it in full.

    Can I cancel any time?

    Yes. Use Cancel subscription on this page whenever you like. There is no contract and no cancellation fee.

    How is billing handled?

    Payments run through Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's secure checkout, not on TrustMyRecord, and Stripe's billing page is where you update your card, see invoices or cancel. Monthly is $49.99 a month; annual is $500 a year.

    What happens when I cancel?

    You keep TMR Pro until the end of the period you already paid for and you are not charged again. After that your account goes back to the free version of each tool. Your account, your record and your picks stay exactly as they are.

    Do the handicappers on the board win?

    Some do and many do not. When the verified figures are available, this page shows the graded result of the proven group live, with the dates it covers, whichever way it goes. It describes past picks only. It is not a forecast, an edge or a promise of profit.
