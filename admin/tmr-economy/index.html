Supply

Every figure below is derived from the ledger and the treasury counters. There is no field anywhere that sets a circulating-supply number by hand, and there must never be one.

Integrity

Two checks this panel owns: every wallet's locked balance equals the sum of its open escrow rows, and the treasury counter equals the treasury ledger it is derived from. A failure here means stop and investigate before moving any TMR.

Feature flags

These are the legally sensitive capabilities. Each is OFF by default and cannot be enabled without a written legal sign-off note recorded here — the backend refuses the change without one, so this is not a UI convention. Do not enter a placeholder: this note is the record of who authorised it and on what basis.

Economy settings

Fees, limits and operational switches. Nothing here is hard-coded in the product — the platform fee on a creator sale, the transfer ceiling and the challenge rake all read from this table on every transaction. Every change is written to a history table with who made it.

Treasury & burns

Fees and rake land in the treasury. Burning destroys treasury-held TMR permanently and cannot be undone. This cannot reach a member's wallet — adjusting a member is the per-wallet admin tool, which writes its own ledger row against them.
