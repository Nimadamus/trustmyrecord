Thursday card, the Yankees run line at a steep price and the 49ers getting a field goal

Thursday September 10. Two plays, 3.5 units to win and 5.65 units at risk, so the card needs 61.75 percent to break even. Both prices below are the numbers on the ticket.

New York Yankees -1 -230 1.5 units
San Francisco 49ers +3.5 -110 2 units

New York Yankees -1, 7:05 eastern, Yankee Stadium. A -1 run line means New York has to win by 2 or more. A one run win is a push and the money comes back, which is the only reason a price this steep is worth discussing at all.

Max Fried against Ryan Feltner is the widest starting pitcher gap on the board tonight. Fried has 17 starts, 95.2 innings, a 2.73 ERA, a 0.99 WHIP, 87 strikeouts against 29 walks, opponents hitting .199 and 0.28 home runs per nine. Feltner has 21 starts, 106.2 innings, a 5.91 ERA, a 1.58 WHIP, 81 strikeouts against 48 walks, opponents hitting .284 and 1.60 home runs per nine.

Feltner does not get better away from altitude the way Colorado starters usually do. On the road it is a 5.36 ERA, a 1.72 WHIP and a .300 opponent average across 9 starts. More traffic, not less.

The Colorado offense travels the way you would expect. 321 runs in 74 road games, 4.34 a game, hitting .237 with a .704 OPS. At home it is 362 runs in 71 games on a .774 OPS. New York at home is 335 runs in 70 games, 4.79 a game. The records are 83-62 and 55-90, and last night these same two clubs finished 6 to 1 New York.

The argument against, and it is the whole problem with this ticket. A price of -230 needs 69.70 percent. New York has won by 2 or more in 66 of 145 games this season, 45.5 percent, and only 30 of 70 at home. In Fried's 17 starts the Yankees have won by 2 or more exactly 7 times. Nothing in the season shape gets near what this price is asking.

What is on the other side of that ledger is Colorado. The Rockies have lost by 2 or more in 34 of their 74 road games and lost by exactly one run 15 times. Those 15 are the push column on a -1 rather than the loss column, which is the entire reason to be on the -1 instead of the -1.5. Take the pushes out of the count and a run line that refunds at one run reads considerably better than the raw 45.9 percent.

That is the honest version. It is a matchup bet on the biggest pitching gap of the night, and it is 1.5 units rather than 3 because the price is doing real damage.

San Francisco 49ers +3.5, 8:35 eastern, Melbourne Cricket Ground. This is a neutral site game in Australia. The Rams are the designated home team and neither club is anywhere near home, which matters for how you read the 3.5. The NFL opens for both of them tonight and every number available is from last season, which is worth saying out loud rather than burying.

Both went 12-5. San Francisco scored 437 and allowed 371. Los Angeles scored 518 and allowed 346. They split the series: the 49ers won 26 to 23 in Los Angeles, the Rams won 42 to 26 in Santa Clara.

The side getting the points is the side that already won on the other's field, by a field goal, and on Thursday there is no field to be on the wrong end of. Three is the most common margin in the sport, so the half point that carries a spread across 3 is the most valuable one on the board, and this ticket owns the outcome where the Rams win by exactly three.

The counter is straightforward. The Rams outscored the 49ers by 81 points across last season and hung 42 on them in the more recent meeting, and laying 3.5 on a neutral field is the market simply calling them the better team. If last November was the real reading and week 5 was the noise, 3.5 is not enough. At -110 this only needs 52.38 percent, which is why it is the larger unit number on a card where the other leg needs almost 70.

Grades post here once the games settle, losers included.

Edit: corrected the venue. This game is at the Melbourne Cricket Ground in Melbourne, a neutral site, not at SoFi. The original post had it wrong and the kickoff is 8:35 eastern.
