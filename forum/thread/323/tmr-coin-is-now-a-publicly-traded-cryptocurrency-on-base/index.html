TMR Coin trades in public as of today. There is a live pool on Base, a market price set by real trading, and anyone with a wallet can buy or sell it without going through this site.

Here is what that actually means, including the parts that are not flattering.

WHAT IS LIVE

Contract 0x522ddf023d0E2Cab5cB05660573165301519AE9e on Base, source verified.

Fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 TMR. There is no mint function, so nobody can print more of it.

No owner, no admin role, no pause, no blacklist, no transfer tax, no upgrade path. Nobody can freeze your tokens or change the contract, including us. That is checkable rather than promised, because the source is published.

A Uniswap v3 pool holds the liquidity, with a price you can look up yourself.

It has a real cash value. TMR can be exchanged for ETH right now at whatever the pool price is.

THE PART YOU NEED TO READ

The market starts small on purpose. The pool holds roughly 17 dollars of ETH. That is about the most cash that can come out of it today, and a trade of about 20 dollars or more will not fill at all. We opened it with money the project already had instead of asking anyone for funding, and it starts at the size that money allowed.

The liquidity position is held by the treasury and is not locked. That means it could be withdrawn. You should know that before you trade, so we are telling you rather than leaving you to find out.

The treasury holds most of the supply. Like any holder, it could sell. Nothing has been sold, and every movement is visible on chain to anyone who looks.

YOUR TMR COIN POINTS ARE NOT THIS TOKEN

This matters more than the headline. The points in your wallet on this site are earned here and spent here. They cannot be sold, transferred, withdrawn, or turned into the token, and they do not carry a cash value. There is no bridge between the two and none is being built. If that ever changes, you will read it here first.

WHAT WE ARE WORKING ON

Deeper liquidity, funded from site revenue as it comes in rather than out of anyone's pocket, so that larger trades can fill and the price stops jumping on small orders.

More real uses for TMR inside TrustMyRecord, so that holding it does something.

That is work on the liquidity and the product. It is not a promise about the price. Nobody here controls what TMR trades at. It can go up, it can go to nothing, and you should never put in money you cannot afford to lose.

WHERE TO LOOK

The verified contract address, live pool data, current price, supply, and how to buy with a self custody wallet are all on the TMR Coin page: https://trustmyrecord.com/tmr-coin/

Last thing, and it is the important one. Nobody from TrustMyRecord will ever message you an offer, ask you to connect a wallet to this site, ask for your seed phrase, or ask you to send funds. Anyone doing that is running a scam. Check the contract address against the page above every single time.
