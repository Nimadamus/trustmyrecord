couple things rattling around my head today so im just gonna dump them here.

first. six preseason games tonight. none of them matter. im watching all six. i will be sat there at 10pm watching a third string guard get beat on a stunt like it means something. steelers packers on nfl network and i already know im gonna read too much into whatever happens. every year i say im not gonna do this and every year i do it anyway.

the thing i actually like about preseason is its the only football where the guys care more than we do. some kid on the roster bubble is playing the fourth quarter for a job that pays his family. meanwhile im on the couch with diesel and a beer treating it like a playoff game. thats the whole appeal honestly.

baseball. i dont know what to tell yall about this august. the rays went 9-0 on a road trip and it barely made the news. the mariners have lost 15 of 20 and thats the kind of stretch that erases a whole good half of a season, brutal. bregman put three out in one night. the whole month has been weird.

world series, since somebody always asks. im not gonna sit here and pretend i have some model that spits out an answer. my honest read is the teams with actual bullpen depth left in october are the ones i trust, and thats way fewer clubs than it was in may. everybodys pen is held together with tape right now. i keep watching starters throw 6 shutout and then the 7th inning shows up and the whole thing comes apart. so if you make me say something out loud, i lean toward whoever still has three arms they trust in a one run game. thats a boring answer but its the true one.

the dodgers are the dodgers. i watched freeman go into the royals dugout last night and my stomach dropped, glad hes day to day. and yeah i know picking them is like picking the sun to come up.

hockey. nobody wants to talk about it in august but im a sharks guy and were finally at the point where the young core isnt just a rebuild talking point. camp cant get here fast enough.

last thing. i keep my bets small this time of year and i think more people should say that out loud. august baseball is the hardest board of the year and my reads are less reliable than they feel. i dont have a hot streak going. i had one last week where my starter went 6 strong and the pen gave it all back in eleven pitches. venom got bit again.

anyway thats where my head is at. small bets, low confidence, six meaningless football games and a beer.
