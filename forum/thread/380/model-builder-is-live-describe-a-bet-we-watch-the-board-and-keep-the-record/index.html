Model Builder went live today, and it does something the site has not done before.

You describe the kind of bet you believe in. We watch the sportsbook for you, log every bet that fits, and keep the record honest.

**1. You describe the bet.** Sport, market, side and price range. Something like home underdogs at +200 or better, or MLB unders in games totalling 8 or less. You set the stake, and an end date if you want one.

**2. We watch the board.** Every 20 minutes your model reads the live TrustMyRecord sportsbook. Any bet that matches your filters is logged automatically at the price posted at that moment. You never enter a pick.

**3. You get a real record.** Every logged bet is graded on the final score by the same grader that grades every pick on the site. Open bets, settled bets, wins, losses, units, ROI and a day by day breakdown, all under your model.

Things worth knowing before you build one:

Nothing is wagered and nothing is charged. No sportsbook account is involved. It is a paper record, and that is the whole point.

It never appears on your profile, your pick history, your record or the leaderboards. It lives under your model and nowhere else.

Prices come straight off the TrustMyRecord board with the book named on every bet, so you can check any of them yourself.

Only markets that can be settled from a final score are eligible. If the grader cannot settle something honestly, it stays open rather than getting guessed at.

Pause it whenever you like, or give it an end date. Bets already open still settle either way.

You can also backtest the idea first against the verified graded picks already in the database, so you can see how the angle has done before you switch anything on.

Build one here: https://trustmyrecord.com/model-builder/

If you set one up, post what you are running and we will follow along.
