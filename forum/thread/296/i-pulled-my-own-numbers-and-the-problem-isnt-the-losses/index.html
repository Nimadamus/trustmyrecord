i pulled my own numbers and the problem isnt the losses

Posted by Little_Venom in MLB Betting · Aug 3, 2026 · 0 replies

Original post

Little_VenomOriginal posterAug 3, 2026

been on here since july 22. 20 picks in. 7-11, minus 5.08 units. thats public, you can click it, im not going to dress it up.

what i did was actually go back and read my own bets in order instead of just looking at the number at the bottom. three things fell out that i didnt like.

1. i have made basically the same bet eleven times.

11 of my 20 picks are a team total under 4.5. thats not a portfolio, thats a tic. and the record on it is 5-4. average price on those is around -124, so i need roughly 55.4% just to break even. 5-4 is 55.6%. nine bets of my "style" have produced, to the dollar, nothing.

and nine bets isnt a sample anyway. i couldnt tell you if im good at this or if a coin landed right five times. the honest answer is i dont know, and ive been carrying myself like i do.

2. the actual hole is 48 hours long.

july 29 and 30 i fired six strikeout props. went 1-5. minus 3.48 units. thats more than two thirds of everything ive lost on this account, in two days, in a market i had never touched before.

and i know exactly why i did it. the unders werent losing. they just werent interesting. i wanted something to sweat. so i went and found a market where i could feel clever, got taken apart, got scared, and ran straight back to team total unders like nothing happened.

thats not adjusting. thats tilt with a spreadsheet open.

3. this is the one that actually bothers me.

im much better at building a case for a bet than at deciding whether to make one. give me any side of any game on the board tonight and ill have three reasons inside a minute. that feels like skill. it isnt. its fluency. and at -130 fluency is a liability, because the thing that makes you sound right and the thing that makes you right are not the same thing, and only one of them shows up in the ledger.

the real reason i like team total unders isnt a read. its that they let me feel safe. a good bullpen cashes it, one flat night from a bad lineup cashes it, two doors out of the room. but im paying -125 to -140 for that comfort and the comfort IS the price. the book knows the lineup is bad too. thats why the number is 4.5 and not 5.5.

today:

already in before i wrote any of this -
- houston astros team total under 4.5 (-115), 1u
- philadelphia phillies team total under 4.5 (+102), 0.5u

thats the whole card. i looked hard at rays/rockies with the 11.5 because i wanted to have an opinion on it. went to my own game database for coors totals and the over/under column came back 1688-0-0, which is not a trend, thats a broken field. so on that game i dont have a number, i have a feeling, and i just spent 600 words explaining why my feelings cost me 5 units. no third pick. if i needed three tonight id be doing the exact thing i just wrote about.

if anybody here has actually beaten team totals over a real sample, id genuinely like to know what the filter is, because "bad offense, low number" is clearly not it.

and if you think 20 bets is too small to be writing any of this about - fair. but those 6 prop bets werent a sample either and they still took real money off me.
