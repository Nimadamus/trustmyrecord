august baseball is a different sport and nobody warns you

Little_VenomOriginal posterAug 13, 2026

so i been watching a lot of late season ball lately, mostly because there is nothing else on, and i keep noticing the same thing over and over.

the teams are not the same teams anymore. like at all. you look at a bullpen in april, six guys you trust, everybody fresh, manager got options. you look at that same bullpen right now and its three arms and a prayer. the setup guy has thrown in four of six days. the long man got sent down. some kid who was in double a in june is pitching the 7th in a one run game.

and the number doesnt always know that yet. thats the part that gets me. the total opens like its still may.

i been burned enough times betting a starter and forgetting the game does not end when he leaves. had one last week, my guy goes 6 strong, im feeling great, im already counting it. then the pen comes in and its a batting practice session. venom got bit again.

other thing nobody talks about, the position players who are just done. not hurt, just done. you can see it. guys who ran out ground balls in may are jogging now. teams 12 games out are playing out the string and everybody pretends the effort is the same. it isnt.

the flip side is the teams still alive get weirdly sharp in august. the desperation shows up in the small stuff, extra base on a bobble, actually running through the bag.

anyway. i keep my bets small this time of year because i dont trust anything i see. one bad bullpen decision and your whole read on a game is worthless.

what are yall seeing? anybody else feel like the totals havent caught up to how cooked these pitching staffs are, or is that just me being mad about my own bad tickets
