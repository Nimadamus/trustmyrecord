Report An Ungraded Wager Yourself, Plus Soccer Grading And Readable Messages

Posted by TMRPolls in TrustMyRecord Updates · Aug 31, 2026 · 0 replies

Original post

TMRPollsOriginal posterAug 31, 2026

Three things went live today. Two of them are fixes for problems that were costing members real answers, and one is a new option that means you never have to message anyone about a stuck wager again.

REPORT UNGRADED WAGER

If one of your wagers is still sitting on Pending long after the event finished, you can now report it yourself. Open your pending picks, find the wager, and use the Report Ungraded Wager button on the ticket.

Everything we need comes with it automatically. Ticket number, sport, league, matchup, selection, market, line, odds, event date, when you submitted it, and the current status. You do not retype any of it. There is an optional comment box if you want to add something, and that is the only thing you can type.

Two clicks and you are done. You get a confirmation straight away, and you get a notification here on TrustMyRecord once the wager has been reviewed and graded.

A wager only becomes reportable once enough time has genuinely passed for it to have been graded. If it is not there yet, the ticket tells you the exact date and time it unlocks rather than just hiding the option. If you try to report the same wager twice, we tell you it is already in the review queue instead of quietly filing it again.

WE NOW CATCH THESE BEFORE YOU DO

A check runs every hour looking for wagers that are still pending past the point they should have settled. Those open a review automatically, whether or not anybody reports them, and they land in the same queue we work through. The point is that a grading problem cannot quietly disappear any more.

SOCCER GRADING

Soccer wagers booked from the board were being stored under a general soccer label instead of the actual competition. Final scores could not reach them, so those wagers sat on Pending. Some of them for weeks. That is fixed, and those wagers have now settled, including a Manchester City moneyline that had been stuck for three days.

Four soccer wagers are still open. They are on qualifying round fixtures and one match that our results provider does not carry under the competition it was filed as. Three tennis wagers and one college football wager are open for their own separate reasons. All of them are in the review queue with a specific reason attached.

We are not going to force a grade onto a wager we cannot verify. If we cannot confirm a result properly, it stays open and visible instead of being settled on a guess. That is the whole point of a verified record.

MESSAGES

Incoming messages in your inbox were dark text on a dark background and were close to unreadable. They now sit on their own lighter bubble with proper contrast. Your sent messages stay blue. Timestamps, the reply and react and delete controls, the conversation list, the day separators and the message box were all checked as well.

WHAT TO DO IF YOU HAVE A STUCK WAGER

Go to your pending picks and use the button on the ticket. It reaches us with everything attached, and you will hear back here rather than having to chase anyone.
