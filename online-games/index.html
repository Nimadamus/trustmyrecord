What online sports gaming means here

Most sites that talk about online sports games are offering somewhere to play. TrustMyRecord is not that. You already own the game and you already have somewhere to play it. What is missing is the part that makes a win worth anything: a record of it that somebody other than you agreed to.

That is the whole idea. Pick one of the five titles above, post an open challenge or call out a specific member, agree the console, the series length and the house rules before a ball is thrown, and play it on your own hardware. One player reports the final. The other confirms it, or disputes it and it goes to admin review with screenshot proof. Only then does it count. Nothing here is a claim about yourself that you get to make alone.

Confirmed games do four things at once. They move your win-loss record and your current streak for that title. They update the head-to-head history against that specific opponent, so a five-game rivalry reads as a series and not as five loose results. They feed the player rankings for that game. And if the match was played inside a league, they move the standings. It is the same rule the rest of TrustMyRecord runs on, applied to a controller instead of a bet slip.

Pick a game, then pick an opponent

This page and the open board do different jobs, and it is worth being blunt about which is which. Online Games is a directory of titles: what is supported, what each one tracks, and where that game lives on the site. Open Challenges is the live board of individual matches waiting for an opponent right now, across all five games at once. Start here when you know the sport but not the person. Start there when you will take whatever is going.

Every game card links to that title's own page, and each of those pages carries the same four modules filtered to that game: open challenges, top players, recent verified results and online leagues. So the fastest route into a match is two clicks. Choose your game here, then accept something on its page.

Editions, and why the addresses carry no year

Challenges are currently created on MLB The Show 26, Madden NFL 26, NBA 2K26, EA FC 26 and NHL 26, and each card above says which. The pages themselves are franchise pages, not edition pages: /sports-gaming/madden/ stays exactly where it is when Madden NFL 27 arrives, and your Madden record carries across the change rather than restarting with the release. A record that resets every August is not a record. The edition lives in the page. The franchise lives in the address.

Free to play, and nothing is wagered

There is no cash entry to any of these games, no prize pool and no wagering on the outcome. Challenges are played for bragging rights and for the record they leave behind. If you are not the one holding a controller you can still take part: free Arena Pick predictions let you call the winner of somebody else's match before it starts, using Arena Points that have no cash value, cannot be purchased and cannot be withdrawn. Nobody can predict on their own match.

Questions about online sports games on TrustMyRecord

Which online sports games can I play here?

Five: MLB The Show, Madden NFL, NBA 2K, EA SPORTS FC and EA SPORTS NHL. Each has its own page carrying that game's open challenges, rankings and leagues.

Which editions are supported?

Challenges are currently created on MLB The Show 26, Madden NFL 26, NBA 2K26, EA FC 26 and NHL 26. The game pages stay evergreen across annual releases and your record carries across editions.

What is the difference between Online Games and Open Challenges?

This page lists the games. The open board lists the individual matches waiting for an opponent. Pick a game here, pick an opponent there.

Do I need to own the game?

To play a challenge, yes, along with a console or PC, because matches run on your own hardware. To browse games, rankings, leagues and results, or to make free predictions, no.

Is there any wagering?

No. No cash entry, no prize pool, no wagering. Arena Points used for predictions have no cash value and cannot be bought or withdrawn.

How does a result reach my record?

One player reports the score, the opponent confirms it, and a disagreement goes to admin review with screenshot proof. Only confirmed or admin-verified results count.

Free to play, no wagering

TrustMyRecord online sports games are free to play. No real-money wagering, no deposits, no payouts and no betting odds on gaming challenges. Predictions use free Arena Points, which have no cash value and cannot be purchased or withdrawn. No money is wagered.
