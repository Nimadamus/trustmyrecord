What line shopping actually does

Two sportsbooks rarely price the same game identically. One may hang -110 on a side while another has -105, or post a total half a point apart. Taking the worse of the two costs nothing today and a great deal over a season, because the price sets the win rate you need before you are profitable at all. Handicapping is hard. Taking the best posted number is not.
