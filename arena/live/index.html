What counts as a live match

A challenge is live from the moment both players start it until a final score is reported and confirmed. If an approved streamer is playing it, the stream appears here too, tied to the challenge it belongs to rather than floating loose. That link is the point: a stream on this page is attached to a real challenge with agreed rules and an opponent who has to confirm the result, so what you are watching is the game that will end up on the record.

Nothing live right now?

The board is quiet outside of scheduled matches, which is normal. Two things are worth doing while it is empty. Look at the open challenges board for callouts waiting on an opponent and accept one, or post your own with a scheduled time so people know when to watch. Scheduled matches also carry free Arena Pick predictions, which lock the moment the match starts, so anyone following along has a reason to be here before first pitch rather than after.

Streaming your own matches

Streaming is approval-based. Approved streamers get a private stream key and go live straight to TrustMyRecord with OBS or a capture card, with the stream attached to the challenge page and archived against their record. The streaming page covers the setup and how to apply.
