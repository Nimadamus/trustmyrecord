Verified Handicapper Badge

Handicappers the TMR Watchdog tracks on Covers, X, Reddit, TheRX and other platforms earn a verified badge once they have at least 25 graded picks. The badge shows the graded record exactly as the Watchdog holds it and links to the full profile on TrustMyRecord.

Records count every graded pick since the Watchdog began grading, at 1 unit risk per pick. A record is history, not a forecast of future results.
