How standings are calculated

Scoring: net units across every contest pick, using the standard TrustMyRecord unit P/L formula. A winner at a minus price pays the stated units; a winner at a plus price pays units multiplied by the price over 100. A loser at a minus price costs units multiplied by the price over 100; a loser at a plus price costs the stated units.
  • All 50 picks are mandatory. An entrant who has not submitted all 50 contest picks by the end of the contest is not eligible to win any prize.
  • Push: scores 0 units. It is not a win and not a loss.
  • Postponed or cancelled: the pick is voided and scores 0 units. It does not count as a win, a loss or a push.
  • Ranking: net units first. Level on net units breaks to the better win percentage, then to whoever submitted their first contest pick earliest.
  • Isolation: contest picks live in their own table and never appear in a member's public record, profile stats, grading history or the site-wide leaderboard.
  • Grading source: final scores come from the same ingested game results the rest of the site grades against. Results update automatically within about 15 minutes of a game going final.
