Contest Rules

  1. Dates: the contest opens at 12:00 AM PT (3:00 AM ET) on Thursday, September 17, 2026. September 30 is the final day: the last picks must be in before 12:00 AM PT on October 1.
  2. Field: registration closed when the contest opened. Only registered entrants can make contest picks.
  3. 50 picks per entrant, made any time during the contest. All 50 picks are mandatory. An entrant who has not submitted all 50 contest picks by the end of the contest is not eligible to win any prize.
  4. MLB only. Moneylines, run lines, game totals, team totals, First 5 moneylines, First 5 run lines and First 5 totals. Posted lines only: no alt lines, no alt totals and no props.
  5. No correlated picks. One side and one total per game: at most one of the moneyline, run line, First 5 moneyline or First 5 run line, and at most one of the game total, team total or First 5 total.
  6. Stakes: half a unit minimum. On a favorite you can win up to 5 units; on an underdog you can risk up to 5 units.
  7. Every pick is final the moment you submit it. No edits and no deletes.
  8. Picks must be in before first pitch. Once a game starts it comes off the board and can no longer be picked.
  9. Sealed until first pitch. Nobody can see which game you picked until it starts; the grid only shows your pending count. When a game goes off the board, every pick on it is revealed.
  10. Most units won wins. Ties go to the higher win percentage, then to whoever made their first pick earliest.
  11. Pushes, postponed and cancelled games score 0 units and count as neither a win nor a loss.
  12. Prizes: $2,500 cash. 1st place $1,500, 2nd place $750, 3rd place $250.
  13. Contest picks are tracked separately and never change your regular TrustMyRecord record.
  14. One entry per person and one per household. Your JustBet account must be opened through the official contest signup link.
