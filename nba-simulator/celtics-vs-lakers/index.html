How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Boston Celtics scored 117.9 per 100 last season and allowed 110.4; Los Angeles Lakers scored 116.6 and allowed 114.0. Projected pace for this matchup is 95.6 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

Los Angeles Lakers hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made Boston Celtics a 62.1% favourite by an average of 3.4 points, with a total of 221.9. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 209 and 234, and 19.2% of runs finished within three points.
