How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Dallas Mavericks scored 109.1 per 100 last season and allowed 114.7; Phoenix Suns scored 113.0 and allowed 111.6. Projected pace for this matchup is 102.2 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

Phoenix Suns hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made Phoenix Suns a 76.5% favourite by an average of 8.6 points, with a total of 229.2. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 216 and 242, and 15.8% of runs finished within three points.
