How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. On the model's profile (2025-26 blended with 2026-27 so far) Chicago Bulls score 111.1 per 100 and allow 116.1; Detroit Pistons score 114.5 and allow 107.3. Projected pace for this matchup is 104.3 possessions, which is what sets the total.
