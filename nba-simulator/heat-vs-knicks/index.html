How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. On the model's profile (2025-26 blended with 2026-27 so far) Miami Heat score 112.3 per 100 and allow 113.3; New York Knicks score 115.8 and allow 109.2. Projected pace for this matchup is 101.9 possessions, which is what sets the total.
