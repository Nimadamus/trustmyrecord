What this Heat vs Knicks simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NBA simulator on Miami Heat at New York Knicks and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs, not a single guess: the projected score, the win probability, the spread and the total all come out of the same possession-level model that powers the full simulator. How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Miami Heat scored 114.5 per 100 last season and allowed 112.3; New York Knicks scored 117.8 and allowed 110.8. Projected pace for this matchup is 102.9 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

New York Knicks hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made New York Knicks a 70.2% favourite by an average of 6.3 points, with a total of 234.7. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 222 and 247, and 17.1% of runs finished within three points.
