How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. On the model's profile (2025-26 blended with 2026-27 so far) Cleveland Cavaliers score 115.8 per 100 and allow 112.4; Boston Celtics score 117.0 and allow 108.1. Projected pace for this matchup is 98.0 possessions, which is what sets the total. Who wins is then set by the forecast model, and any player listed out moves the answer by what his minutes are worth.
