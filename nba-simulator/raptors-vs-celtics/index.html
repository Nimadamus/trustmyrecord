How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Toronto Raptors scored 113.9 per 100 last season and allowed 111.1; Boston Celtics scored 117.9 and allowed 110.4. Projected pace for this matchup is 96.1 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

Boston Celtics hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made Boston Celtics a 69.3% favourite by an average of 6.0 points, with a total of 219.3. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 207 and 231, and 18.0% of runs finished within three points.
