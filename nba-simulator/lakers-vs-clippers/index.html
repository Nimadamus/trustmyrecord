How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Los Angeles Lakers scored 116.6 per 100 last season and allowed 114.0; LA Clippers scored 115.5 and allowed 113.6. Projected pace for this matchup is 97.1 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

LA Clippers hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made LA Clippers a 53.3% favourite by an average of 1.1 points, with a total of 226.9. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 214 and 239, and 19.7% of runs finished within three points.
