Common questions

How does the Pistons simulator decide a score?

It plays the game possession by possession with the Pistons rotation on the floor. Each possession ends in a shot, a turnover or a foul drawn by a named player at his own rate, so the box score adds up to the final score rather than being distributed to fit it. Team strength comes from an opponent-adjusted fit solved from results, which currently ranks the Pistons 3rd of 30.

Does it use the current Pistons roster?

Yes. Rosters, rotations and injury designations are refreshed from the data provider several times a day, and every simulation states how old its data is. Nobody in the rotation is currently listed out.

Can I sit a Pistons player and see what changes?

Yes. Any player can be held out or put on a minutes restriction, and the projection, the box score and the player ranges are all rebuilt without him. The page reports how far the answer moved rather than leaving two screens to be compared.
