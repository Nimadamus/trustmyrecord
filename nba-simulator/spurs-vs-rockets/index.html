How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. San Antonio Spurs scored 117.5 per 100 last season and allowed 109.2; Houston Rockets scored 115.9 and allowed 110.9. Projected pace for this matchup is 99.5 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

Houston Rockets hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made San Antonio Spurs a 59.8% favourite by an average of 2.7 points, with a total of 227.1. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 214 and 239, and 18.4% of runs finished within three points.
