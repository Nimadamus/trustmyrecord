How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Los Angeles Lakers scored 116.6 per 100 last season and allowed 114.0; Golden State Warriors scored 112.7 and allowed 113.3. Projected pace for this matchup is 100.0 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

Golden State Warriors hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made Los Angeles Lakers a 58.8% favourite by an average of 2.4 points, with a total of 229.7. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 217 and 242, and 18.4% of runs finished within three points.
