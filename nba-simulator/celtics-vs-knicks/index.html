How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Boston Celtics scored 117.9 per 100 last season and allowed 110.4; New York Knicks scored 117.8 and allowed 110.8. Projected pace for this matchup is 94.6 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

New York Knicks hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made New York Knicks a 50.2% favourite by an average of 0.2 points, with a total of 217.2. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 205 and 229, and 20.1% of runs finished within three points.
