How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. Denver Nuggets scored 120.3 per 100 last season and allowed 115.3; Oklahoma City Thunder scored 117.0 and allowed 105.7. Projected pace for this matchup is 101.5 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

Oklahoma City Thunder hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made Oklahoma City Thunder a 69.3% favourite by an average of 6.0 points, with a total of 236.2. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 223 and 248, and 17.2% of runs finished within three points.
