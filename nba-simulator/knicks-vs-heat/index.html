How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. New York Knicks scored 117.8 per 100 last season and allowed 110.8; Miami Heat scored 114.5 and allowed 112.3. Projected pace for this matchup is 102.9 possessions against a league average of 101.8, which is what sets the total.

Miami Heat hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 25,000 runs the model made New York Knicks a 60.2% favourite by an average of 2.8 points, with a total of 234.7. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 222 and 247, and 18.1% of runs finished within three points.
