How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. On the model's profile (2025-26 blended with 2026-27 so far) New York Knicks score 115.3 per 100 and allow 109.3; Miami Heat score 113.3 and allow 113.6. Projected pace for this matchup is 102.8 possessions, which is what sets the total. Who wins is then set by the forecast model, and any player listed out moves the answer by what his minutes are worth.

Miami Heat hold the home floor here, worth roughly two and a half to three points. Across 10,000 runs the model made Miami Heat a 51.3% favourite by an average of 0.5 points, with a total of 232.2. The middle half of simulated totals landed between 219 and 244, and 18.3% of runs finished within three points.
