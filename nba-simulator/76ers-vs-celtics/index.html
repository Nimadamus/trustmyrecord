How the projection is built

Expected efficiency for each side is the league average moved by that offense's distance from average and by the opposing defense's distance from average, both measured per 100 possessions so speed of play does not distort the comparison. On the model's profile (2025-26 blended with 2026-27 so far) Philadelphia 76ers score 111.4 per 100 and allow 109.4; Boston Celtics score 116.4 and allow 109.8. Projected pace for this matchup is 98.6 possessions, which is what sets the total.
