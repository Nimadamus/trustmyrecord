Two Clubs, Two Different Seasons Depending on the Ground

Brentford at home against Everton away

This is a Premier League fixture, and on the overall table Everton look like the stronger club: No. 7 against Brentford at No. 10. Change the lens to where the points were actually earned, though, and the picture flips. Brentford are taking 2 points a game at the Gtech Community Stadium this season; Everton are taking 1.2 a game away from home. That gap is the whole argument for this fixture.

Brentford's home record reads 3-1-1 across 5 completed fixtures, which is the output of a club playing like a top half side in front of its own supporters. Everton's away record is 1-3-1 across 5, a run of draws that keeps them ticking over without ever really winning on the road. Five fixtures is a thin sample on each side, and nobody should pretend otherwise. But the direction is consistent, and it's the right frame for a trip to west London.

The edge

Brentford take 2 points a game at home; Everton take 1.2 away. The table hides that.

Brentford Shut the Door at Home

Goals conceded by venue

The engine of Brentford's home number is defensive. They concede 0.6 goals a game at home this season, against 1.1 a game across all 10 league fixtures, so the 11 they've let in overall have come disproportionately on the road. That's a club that is far harder to break down at the Gtech than its overall line suggests.

Everton concede 1 goal a game away from home, the same as their overall rate of 1, so they don't travel worse at the back; they just don't win. Their 4 clean sheets in 10, a 40% rate, actually beat Brentford's 3 and 30%. Everton's problem away is at the other end of the pitch, and that's exactly where the home split bites: they're running into the stingiest version of this Brentford back line.

The edge

Brentford concede 0.6 goals a game at home. Everton need goals away, and that's where they've struggled.

Why Everton Still Sit Higher

The overall numbers, and why they mislead here

Across the whole Premier League season so far Everton have 16 points from a 4-4-2 record, 1.6 a game, against Brentford's 14 from 3-5-2, 1.4 a game. That's the gap that puts Everton No. 7 and Brentford No. 10. Everton have won 40% of their fixtures this season, Brentford 30%, and on that measure alone you'd make the visitors the better side.

Yet Brentford have the better goal difference, +5 to +2, and score more, 16 goals at 1.6 a game against Everton's 12 at 1.2. Their 5 draws are what's holding the points total down, and with a 3-1-1 record at home, most of those draws have come on their travels. The overall table blends two very different Brentford sides into one middling one.

The edge

Brentford's +5 goal difference beats Everton's +2; the draws, mostly away, are what flatter the table.

Form Leans Everton, the Venue Doesn't

The last 6 fixtures for each club

Recent form tilts slightly toward Everton. They're 3-1-2 over their last 6 this season, 1.67 points a game, scoring 1.17 a game. Brentford are 2-2-2 over the same stretch, 1.33 points a game, though they're scoring 1.5 a game, more than Everton are managing.

None of that overturns the venue split. Everton's recent run sits a touch above their season rate of 1.6 points a game, but it's still well short of the 2 points a game Brentford take at home. The question this fixture asks is narrower than form: whether Everton can do on the road what they've done at 1.2 points a game so far.

The edge

Everton's 1.67 points a game over the last 6 still trails Brentford's 2 at home.

The Meetings and the Price

What the 2025 and 2026 seasons and the board say

The 2 meetings on record across the 2025 and 2026 seasons give Brentford a 1-1-0 record against Everton, and the most recent of them, last season, finished Brentford 2, Everton 2. That's no basis for a trend, but neither meeting went Everton's way. The board at DraftKings reads it similarly: Brentford +125, Everton +210 and the draw +250.

Those prices lean Brentford without making them clear favourites, which is fair given last season's draw between these clubs and Everton's 1-3-1 record away this season. Brentford on the handicap is -0.5 (+115). The goals total is 2.5, with the over at -126 and the under at -102, and a Brentford defence conceding 0.6 a game at home this season argues for the under more firmly than that price does.

The edge

Across the 2025 and 2026 seasons Everton haven't beaten Brentford, and last season's meeting ended Brentford 2, Everton 2.
