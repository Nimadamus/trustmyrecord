Last season's table was never close

Points per game last season split these clubs cleanly

This Bundesliga fixture at Signal Iduna Park comes with a table gap that was never in doubt last season. Dortmund took 2.15 points a game last season and finished No. 2 on 73 points. Werder Bremen took 0.94 a game and finished No. 15 on 32. That's more than double the rate, and it held across 34 fixtures, which is a full campaign rather than a hot month.

The records last season tell the same story from another direction. Dortmund went 22-7-5 last season while Bremen went 8-8-18, so Bremen lost nearly as many fixtures as Dortmund won. A side taking 0.94 a game last season is fighting to stay up, not chasing anyone, and it's travelling to a ground where the hosts banked 73 points over the same stretch.

The edge

Dortmund took 2.15 points a game last season; Bremen took 0.94. Everything else in this fixture is commentary on that gap.

How Dortmund built those points last season

Win rate and goal difference last season explain the table

Dortmund won 64.7% of their fixtures last season; Bremen won 23.5%. That's the cleanest way to see where the points went, because draws didn't separate them much: both clubs drew 7 or 8 times last season. The difference was what happened when a fixture got decided, and Dortmund decided 22 of them their way against Bremen's 8.

Goal difference last season backs the table rather than flattering it. Dortmund finished +36, scoring 70 and conceding 34, while Bremen finished -23 after scoring 37 and conceding 60. A table position can sometimes outrun a goal difference on luck in tight fixtures. Neither of these clubs did that last season; the goals and the points point the same way.

The edge

Last season Dortmund finished +36 and Bremen -23. The table didn't flatter anyone.

The attack and the defence last season

Goals scored and conceded per fixture last season

Dortmund scored 2.06 goals a game last season against 1.09 for Bremen. At the other end Dortmund conceded 1 a game last season while Bremen conceded 1.76. Put those together and Dortmund were winning their average fixture by a goal, while Bremen were losing theirs by more than half a goal.

The defensive line is the one that matters most for the table story. Bremen let in 60 goals last season across 34 fixtures, and a club conceding 1.76 a game needs to score twice just to draw most weeks. Bremen managed 1.09 a game last season, so the arithmetic of their 8-8-18 record wasn't bad luck, it was the shape of the side.

The edge

Bremen conceded 1.76 a game last season and scored 1.09. That is a relegation profile, not an unlucky one.

Four fixtures in, nothing has flipped

This season's thin sample against last season's full one

Four fixtures into the new Bundesliga season, Dortmund are 4-0-0 at 3 points a game and Bremen are 2-1-1 at 1.75. Bremen's start is a genuine improvement on the 0.94 they took last season, and they're scoring 2 a game across those four. Dortmund, though, have started even further above their 2.15 from last season, scoring 2.25 a game with 3 clean sheets, a 75% rate.

The home and away split is where Bremen's start looks fragile. Dortmund are 2-0-0 at home this season, taking 3 points a game and conceding 0. Bremen are 0-1-1 away, at 0.5 points a game and conceding 2.5, with 0 clean sheets anywhere, a 0% rate. Four fixtures is a thin read and should be weighted that way, but nothing in it argues against what last season's table already said.

The edge

Bremen are 0-1-1 away this season and conceding 2.5 a game on the road. The early sample only sharpens last season's picture.

The meetings and what the price already knows

Head to head and the board against last season's gap

Dortmund are 2-0-0 against Bremen across the 2 meetings on record, and the last one ended Dortmund 2, Bremen 0. Two meetings won't carry an argument alone, but they sit comfortably beside last season's table, where Dortmund took 2.15 points a game to Bremen's 0.94. There's no hidden matchup quirk here pulling against the standings.

The board knows all of this. Dortmund are -290 to win, Bremen +600 and the draw +432, which is a heavy price on the hosts. The value question shifts to the margin: Dortmund at -1.5 (-135) on the handicap asks for a two goal win, which is roughly what last season's scoring gap implies. The total sits at 3.5 with the over at -105 and the under at -125, so the market expects goals but leans slightly toward fewer than four.

The edge

At -290 the straight win is priced in. The handicap at -1.5 (-135) is where last season's gap actually gets tested.
