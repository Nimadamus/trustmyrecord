One Defence Shuts the Door, the Other Never Has

The single number that separates these two clubs

Lille host Lens in Ligue 1 at Stade Pierre-Mauroy, and the Derby du Nord arrives with one number that splits the two clubs cleanly. Lille have kept 4 clean sheets in 8 league fixtures this season, a 50% clean sheet rate. Lens have kept 0. That's a 0% rate for a side sitting No. 7 in the table, and it's the reason this fixture was picked.

The records look closer than the defensive split does. Lille are 4-3-1 and No. 3; Lens are 4-1-3 with the same number of wins. The gap sits almost entirely in the goals against column: 6 conceded by Lille, 12 by Lens. Lens have won their fixtures this season by outscoring people rather than shutting them out, and that habit tends to cost points against a side that gives very little away.

The Counterweight: Lens Can Score

The best argument against the clean sheet thesis

The honest counterweight is that Lens score freely. They have 16 goals in 8 Ligue 1 fixtures this season, 2 a game, comfortably the sharper attack in this pairing. Lille have 11 at 1.38 a game. Lille's 50% clean sheet rate was built against whoever has come before, and Lens bring more firepower than Lille's own forwards do.

Recent form trims that threat a little. Over the last 6 Lens have scored 1.67 a game, down from 2 across the season, while Lille's last 6 sit at 1.17. The goal difference reads +4 for Lens and +5 for Lille, so both routes have worked; they've simply worked in opposite ways. This is a defence that keeps doors shut meeting an attack that has cooled slightly but still outscores its host.

The tension

Lens score 2 a game. Lille concede 0.75 at home. One of those numbers has to give.
