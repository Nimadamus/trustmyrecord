The Table Says One Side

Points per game frames this fixture more bluntly than anything else

This is a Bundesliga fixture at the Allianz Arena, and the table frames it more bluntly than anything else on the page. Bayern Munich sit No. 1 with 21 points from nine fixtures, a 6-3-0 record that works out to 2.33 points a game. FC Cologne sit No. 15 on 9 points, a 2-3-4 record and exactly 1 point a game. That's the central fact of this fixture, and everything else hangs off it.

Points per game is the cleanest way to read a table this early, because it tells you how often a club turns a match into a result. Bayern have won 66.7% of their league fixtures this season; Cologne have won 22.2%. Bayern haven't lost yet. Cologne have lost more often than they've won, and nothing about the trip to Munich suggests that ratio improves here.

The edge

Bayern are banking 2.33 points a game; Cologne are banking 1. The table isn't close, and it isn't pretending to be.

The Venue Split Widens the Gap

Bayern at the Allianz Arena against Cologne on the road

Split the Bundesliga table by venue and the gap widens rather than closes. At the Allianz Arena this season Bayern are 3-1-0, taking 2.5 points a game across four home fixtures. Cologne on the road are 0-1-3, taking 0.25 points a game, a single draw from four trips. Whatever is keeping them clear of the very bottom has been earned at home, and they don't get to bring that to Munich.

The goals against tell the same story. Bayern concede 1 a game at home; Cologne concede 2 a game away from home. A club leaking two goals a match on its travels, walking into the ground of the side top of the table, is the venue split doing exactly what the overall standings predicted.

Goal Difference Is the Table in Miniature

Volume scoring behind the points total

Bayern's points total is built on volume. They've scored 29 Bundesliga goals in nine fixtures this season, 3.22 a game, and carry a goal difference of +20. Cologne have 10 goals, 1.11 a game, and a goal difference of -4. Those two goal difference figures are the table position written in a different column.

The trend runs the wrong way for the visitors. Across their last six, Bayern have scored 3.67 a game, up on their season rate, while Cologne have managed 0.83 a game, down on theirs. A club scoring under a goal a game recently probably needs a clean sheet at the Allianz Arena to take anything, and that leads straight to the other half of the comparison.

The edge

+20 against -4. The goal difference column says exactly what the points column says.

Neither Back Line Is a Wall, Only One Has Cover

Goals conceded and clean sheets on both sides

Bayern aren't impregnable. They've conceded 9 in nine fixtures this season, 1 a game, and kept only 2 clean sheets, a 22.2% rate. This is a side winning matches by outscoring people rather than shutting them down, which leaves a door slightly open for any opponent who can score.

Cologne's numbers are worse at both ends. They've conceded 14, 1.56 a game, with 1 clean sheet all season, an 11.1% rate. Even if Bayern concede, Cologne would have to stop an attack averaging 3.22 goals a game with a defence that has kept one clean sheet, and that's the arithmetic the table is already pricing in.

The mismatch

Bayern concede 1 a game. Cologne concede 1.56. One of these back lines is about to face 3.22 a game.

Form Confirms the Standings

The last six fixtures for both clubs

Recent form doesn't offer Cologne a way in either. Bayern are 4-2-0 over their last six with 2.33 points a game, identical to their season rate, so there's no slump to exploit. Cologne are 1-2-3 over the same stretch at 0.83 points a game, below the 1 point a game they're averaging across the whole season.

That matters because it means the table isn't flattering Bayern or hiding a Cologne recovery. The club at No. 1 is holding its level and the club at No. 15 is drifting. Bayern's last six have produced 3.67 goals a game; Cologne's have produced 0.83, and that's the gap the visitors carry into Munich.

Consistency

Bayern: 2.33 points a game over the season and 2.33 over the last six. There's no dip to wait for.
