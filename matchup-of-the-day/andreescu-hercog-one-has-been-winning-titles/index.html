Two trophies against none, and why that matters in a semifinal

Bianca Andreescu arrives in this Sopo semifinal with 2 titles from 20 tournaments this season, and Polona Hercog arrives without one from 14. That's the whole story of the week in a sentence: one player has already learned how to finish an event this year and the other is still trying to. Andreescu's 2-2 record in finals tells you she hasn't just been lifting trophies, she's been getting to the last day often enough to lose a couple there too. Reaching Sunday that regularly is the habit of a player who keeps surviving draws.

Hercog has to find that for the first time this year against someone who already has.

Titles Come From Not Wasting Energy

Andreescu takes rounds rather than scraping through them

Title runs are built on efficiency, and Andreescu's season is full of it. Of her 33 wins, 24 came in straight sets, a 72.7% share, and she's won 62.9% of her sets this season at 73-43. Her season game differential sits at +104, which is what a player looks like when she isn't grinding out rounds but taking them. That's how you keep your legs for the weekend.

Hercog's season numbers are respectable rather than ruthless. Ten of her 16 wins came in straight sets, a 62.5% share, her set record is 37-30 for a 55.2% set win rate, and her game differential is +36. She's 6-5 this season in matches that went to a deciding set against Andreescu's 9-7, so neither of them folds when a best of 3 match goes the distance. The real gap is how rarely Andreescu needs a decider at all.

The Ranking Climb Is What Title Runs Buy

A heavy schedule that has paid off for Andreescu

Hercog's road to the final goes through the one player in the draw who has shown, twice this season, that she knows the rest of the way.
