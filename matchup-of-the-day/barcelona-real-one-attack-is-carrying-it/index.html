The scoring gap is the whole fixture

Barcelona's output against Betis's, in the same competition.

This is a LaLiga fixture at Estadio La Cartuja, and the cleanest way into it is to count goals. Barcelona is scoring 4.25 a game across 8 fixtures this season, which adds up to 34 goals. Betis has 14 goals from the same number of fixtures, or 1.75 a game.

That is a gap of more than double, and it exists between two clubs whose records look close on a quick glance. Barcelona is 8-0-0 and Betis is 6-1-1. The table says both sides are winning; the goals say they are winning in very different ways.

Barcelona's recent scoring has not slowed

The last six results, and the away record.

You might expect an attack running this hot to cool off, and the recent run says it hasn't. Over the last 6 completed fixtures Barcelona has scored 4.5 a game and won all of them, a 6-0-0 form record. That is slightly higher than the 4.25 it has managed across the whole season.

The road does not seem to bother them either. Barcelona is 4-0-0 away from home, and has won 100% of its fixtures this season. A club that scores like this on the road leaves the opponent very little room to play for a draw.

Betis scores steadily, but not like this

What the home side's attack actually looks like.

Betis is no weak side. It sits No. 3 in the table with a 6-1-1 record, and it has won 75% of its fixtures. At home it is 4-0-0, so La Cartuja has been a comfortable place for it this season.

The attack behind that is solid rather than spectacular. Betis is scoring 1.75 a game across 8 fixtures and 2 a game over its last 6, a small step up. Even at its recent best, that is less than half of what Barcelona is producing per fixture over the same stretch.

The back lines frame the attacking story

Goals conceded, goal difference and clean sheets side by side.

Defence matters here because it shows how much of Barcelona's edge is the attack and how much is everything else. Barcelona concedes 0.88 a game, having let in 7 goals, and its goal difference is +27. Betis concedes 1.13 a game and sits at +5.

The clean sheet rate is identical, 50% for both clubs, so the defensive side does not separate them. Betis is stingy at home, allowing 0.5 a game there, and that is a real strength. But Barcelona is taking 3 points a game against 2.38 for Betis, and the goals scored column explains almost all of that difference.

Last season's meeting points the same way

What the two meetings on record say, and which season they belong to.

The head to head is short, just 2 meetings on record across the 2026 and 2025 seasons, and both went to Barcelona, a 0-0-2 record from the Betis side. The most recent one was last season, not this one: Betis 1, Barcelona 3. That scoreline is worth reading for shape, since Betis got a goal but Barcelona got three.

Those meetings are from last season and the season before, so they describe different squads and should carry modest weight. What carries more is this season's form. Betis is 4-1-1 over its last 6 and taking 2.17 points a game, which is good, while Barcelona is taking 3 points a game across the same stretch.

What the price says about goals

The FanDuel numbers on the TrustMyRecord soccer board.

The market agrees that Barcelona's scoring is the story. The goals total is 3.5, with the over at -175 and the under at +125, so the book expects an open fixture. Barcelona to win is -211, Betis to win is +410 and the draw is +415.

Betis is +1.5 at -140 on the handicap, which tells you how the book sees a Betis loss: probably by a goal, not by a landslide. That is a fair read of a home side with a good defensive record at La Cartuja. It still leaves the burden on Betis to score, and its attack is the thinner one.
