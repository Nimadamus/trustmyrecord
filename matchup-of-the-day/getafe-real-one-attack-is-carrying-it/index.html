One Attack Did the Heavy Lifting Last Season

The clearest line separating these clubs in last season's table

This is a LaLiga fixture at the Estadio Benito Villamar├¡n, and the argument for Real Betis starts with a single line of the table. Last season Betis scored 1.55 goals per fixture while Getafe scored 0.84. Across 38 fixtures that became 59 goals for Betis and 32 for Getafe, and nothing else in the comparison separates the clubs as sharply.

What makes the gap interesting is how little else differed between them last season. Both clubs won 39.5% of their fixtures, with Betis finishing No. 5 on 60 points and Getafe No. 7 on 51. The points gap came almost entirely from the draw column, where Betis sat on 15 and Getafe on 6. A club that scores freely turns losing positions into draws far more often than one that doesn't, and that's the attack showing up in the standings.

Getafe's Case Rested on Defending

How a thin attack still finished seventh last season

Getafe deserve a fair hearing, because last season their defence was the better of the two. They conceded 38 goals, 1 per fixture, against 48 for Betis at 1.26 per fixture. That's the only reason a side scoring 0.84 a fixture last season could finish No. 7 with a goal difference of -6.

The trouble is what that model does to results. Getafe's record last season was 15-6-17, more defeats than wins and hardly any draws, which is what happens when a club has no margin for a single mistake. Betis finished last season at +11 on goal difference with a 15-15-8 record. Their 8 defeats against Getafe's 17 is the attack buying cover for a leakier back line.

This Season Hasn't Changed the Shape

A handful of fixtures in, the scoring gap looks familiar

The new season is only 5 fixtures old, so treat what follows as a check on last season's story rather than a replacement for it. Betis have gone 4-0-1 so far this season, scoring 1.4 goals per fixture and taking 2.4 points per fixture. Getafe are 1-2-2 with 0.6 goals per fixture and 1 point per fixture. The attack that carried Betis last season is still doing the carrying.

The defensive picture has tilted too, at least on a small sample. Betis have kept 3 clean sheets in 5 fixtures this season, a 60% rate, while Getafe have 1, a 20% rate. Last season Getafe could lean on keeping things tight; so far they haven't managed it, and an attack scoring 0.6 per fixture can't cover for that.

What the Board Is Pricing

A low goals total against a lopsided two way price

The goals total sits at 2.5, with the under at -150 and the over at +118. That's a market expecting a tight fixture, which sits closer to Getafe's 0.84 goals per fixture last season than to Betis's 1.55. If the Betis attack does what it did last season, the over at +118 is where that view gets expressed.

On the two way price, with the draw not priced on the board, Betis are -450 and Getafe +300. Betis on the handicap at -0.5 (-167) needs Betis to finish ahead outright. Given how often Betis drew last season, 15 times in 38 fixtures, that handicap asks more of them than the scoring gap alone suggests.
