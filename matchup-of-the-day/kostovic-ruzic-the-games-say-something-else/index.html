The Game Count Is the Story

The match records undersell how far apart these two seasons are.

Start with the scoreboard nobody prints. Teodora Kostovic has won 472 games and lost 406 this season, a 53.8% share of every game she's played and a +66 differential. Antonia Ruzic sits at 456 won and 503 lost, which is 47.5% and a -47 differential. Those aren't two players a couple of results apart; they're on opposite sides of break even.

The match records say something milder. Kostovic is 23-17 this season, a 57.5% win rate, and Ruzic is 19-24 at 44.2%. Game share is the stickier of the two numbers, since it counts every game instead of flipping on a few close sets. Ruzic's record reads like a player dropping tight ones, but her games say she's been outplayed more often than not.

The edge

Kostovic is +66 in games this season, Ruzic is -47. That's the whole argument in two numbers.

The Ranking Points the Other Way

Why the higher ranked player is the one with the weaker season.

None of this squares with the rankings at first glance. Ruzic is No. 83 with 894 points, and Kostovic is No. 139 with 540, though she was No. 159 a week ago and is climbing. Ruzic slipped from No. 80 over the same week. A ranking is a long memory, and the season is telling a more current story.

The honest caveat sits in the main draw split. Kostovic is 14-13 in main draws across her 18 events, so a fair share of her surplus was built in qualifying against a softer field. Ruzic is 15-20 in main draws over 24 events, a tougher schedule but still a losing one. Strip Kostovic back to main draws and she's close to a coin flip player; Ruzic is below that even there.

The caveat

Main draws only, Kostovic is 14-13 and Ruzic is 15-20. The gap narrows, it doesn't flip.

Kostovic Finishes Early, Ruzic Drags It Out

Straight sets for one, long nights for the other.

The sets tell the same story with more texture. Kostovic is 54-39 in sets this season, a 58.1% set win rate, while Ruzic is 45-58, or 43.7%. When Kostovic wins she mostly does it cleanly: 18 of her 23 wins came in straight sets, a 78.3% share. Ruzic has won only 9 of her 19 in straights, which is 47.4%.

Here's the part that should bother anyone backing Kostovic. Ruzic is 10-7 this season in matches that reached a deciding set, and Kostovic is just 5-9. Ruzic lives in third sets because she rarely closes in two, and once she's there she's been genuinely tough. Tiebreaks barely move the needle; Kostovic is 4-4 and Ruzic 1-6, samples far too thin to lean on.

The danger

Ruzic is 10-7 when a match goes the distance. Kostovic is 5-9.

One Player Arrives Hot

The last stretch lines up with the season, not against it.

Kostovic is 8-2 over her last 10 matches, and that's where the ranking jump came from. Ruzic is 4-6 over the same stretch, which fits her drift in the other direction. Short form windows are noisy, but these agree with the season long game count rather than fighting it.

Both are 2-0 in Adana this week, so neither has backed into the Quarterfinal. That's the one place their seasons look alike, and it's two matches. A last eight at a WTA event is a real week for a player ranked No. 139; for Ruzic it's a chance to stop a slide before it becomes the shape of her year.

The form

Kostovic 8-2 lately, Ruzic 4-6. The hot hand belongs to the lower ranked player.

Where This Quarterfinal Turns

Two sets favors Kostovic; three opens the door.

Put it together and Kostovic is the better player right now by the measure that travels best, the share of games won: 53.8% this season to 47.5%. Her path is the one she's walked all year, take it in two and keep Ruzic away from a decider. She's done exactly that in 78.3% of her wins.

Ruzic's path is narrower but real. She needs this one to go long, because at 10-7 in deciders she's been the better finisher and Kostovic at 5-9 has been the shakier one. If it's a set apiece on Centre Court, the game gap stops mattering and the deciding set numbers take over. In a best of 3 sets match, though, the player winning more games is usually the one who never lets it get there.

The verdict

Kostovic wins the games, Ruzic wins the deciders. Kostovic's job is to make sure there isn't one.
