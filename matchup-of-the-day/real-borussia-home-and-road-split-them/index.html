The Split That Defines This Fixture

Dortmund at home against Betis on the road

This is a UEFA Champions League fixture at Signal Iduna Park, and the domestic numbers both clubs carry into it point the same way. Dortmund are 3-1-0 at home in the Bundesliga this season, which works out to 2.5 points per match across 4 home fixtures. Betis are 2-2-1 away from home in LaLiga, 1.6 points per match across 5 trips. That gap is the story here, and it's the sharpest contrast in the whole matchup.

The samples are small, and that cuts both ways. Four home fixtures isn't much to build a case on, but Dortmund have conceded 0.5 goals per match in them, which suggests those points come from control rather than narrow escapes. Betis, meanwhile, have shipped 1.4 per match on the road. A side that concedes that freely away from home leans on its attack to rescue results, and here it meets a home defence that rarely gives it the chance.

The edge

Dortmund take 2.5 points per match at home; Betis take 1.6 on the road. Everything else is commentary on that gap.

Betis Are Good, Just Not on the Road

A strong league line that thins out away from home

Betis are a good side, and their LaLiga line says so: 7-2-2, 23 points from 11 fixtures, 2.09 points per match and No. 4 in the domestic table. Strip out the trips, though, and the picture shifts. Away from home that rate falls to 1.6, which means most of their haul has been built in Seville.

The defensive record tells a slightly different story. Betis concede 1.36 per match overall and 1.4 away from home, so the leak is fairly constant; what changes on the road is the points return rather than the goals against. That points to an attack that does its best work at home. It also helps explain why Betis are +375 to win this one: the market is reading the same split.

Where the Real Difference Lives

Similar attacks, very different goals against

Dortmund's defence is the engine behind the home record. They've conceded 6 in 8 Bundesliga fixtures, 0.75 per match, with 5 clean sheets for a 62.5% clean sheet rate. Betis also have 5 clean sheets, but across 11 fixtures that's 45.5%, and they've conceded 15 for a goal difference of +3 against Dortmund's +8.

Goals scored are closer than the defensive gap suggests. Betis score 1.64 per match and Dortmund 1.75, so this isn't a mismatch of firepower. It's a mismatch of what each club gives back. At home Dortmund concede 0.5 per match, and that's the figure Betis have to break.

The edge

Betis score 1.64 per match and Dortmund 1.75. The difference lives at the other end of the pitch.

Form Doesn't Undo the Split

The last 6 for both clubs

Recent form doesn't close the gap. Dortmund are 3-3-0 over their last 6, 2 points per match, and still unbeaten in the Bundesliga at 5-3-0. Betis are 3-2-1 over the same stretch, 1.83 points per match, a step down from their season rate of 2.09.

The draws are worth noticing. Dortmund have drawn 3 of their last 6 and scored 1.5 per match in that run, below their season rate of 1.75. Betis have scored 1.83 per match across their last 6, so their attack is arriving in decent shape. That's the main reason Betis can't be dismissed, even if the venue numbers lean hard against them.

What the Board Is Asking

The result market and the goals total

FanDuel has Dortmund at -156, the draw at +266 and Betis at +375. Dortmund are also -0.5 (-160) on the handicap, which is effectively the same position as backing them to win. The prices already lean on the home and away split, so the real question is whether -156 captures all of it.

The goals total is 3.5, with the over at +120 and the under at -153. Dortmund concede 0.5 per match at home and Betis score 1.64 per match overall, and neither figure points toward a high scoring night at Signal Iduna Park. The under is the stronger read of the two, though at -153 it isn't cheap.
