Vancouver at BC Place, Austin on the road

The venue records that decide this fixture

This is Major League Soccer, and the fixture at BC Place comes down to a single split. Vancouver Whitecaps have gone 9-1-3 at home this season, which works out to 2.15 points per game in front of their own supporters. Austin FC travel with a 2-3-7 record away from home and 0.75 points per game on the road. That's the gap the whole evening is built on, and it's wider than anything the overall table shows.

Split each club's season by venue and both look like a more extreme version of themselves. Vancouver's 13 home fixtures have produced 9 wins against 3 defeats, so a visiting side leaving with anything is the exception. Austin's 12 away fixtures have produced 2 wins, and 7 of them ended in defeat. Records like that aren't luck over a dozen trips; they describe a club that hasn't worked out how to travel.

Goals conceded at home and away

The points split has a clear source, and it's defensive. Vancouver concede 0.69 goals per game at home this season, below the 0.91 they concede across all 23 fixtures. Austin concede 2.58 goals per game away from home, well above the 1.79 they give up across all 24. One club tightens up at BC Place and the other comes apart on the road.

Clean sheets tell the same story across the full season. Vancouver have kept 10, a 43.5% rate, while Austin have 4, which is 16.7% of their fixtures. There's no venue split for clean sheets here, so I won't pretend to know the BC Place figure, but a side conceding 0.69 a game at home doesn't leave a visitor much to work with. For Austin, conceding 2.58 a game away means they'd need to score well beyond the 1.25 a game they manage across the season just to stay level.
