Charlotte at Home Is a Different Club

The venue numbers carry this fixture

This Major League Soccer fixture at Bank of America Stadium turns on one split. Charlotte are taking 1.92 points a game at home this season, from a 7-4-2 record across 13 completed home fixtures. Chicago, on the road, are down at 1.31 points a game on a 4-5-4 record from 13 away fixtures. Same sample size, very different returns, and the venue happens to belong to the club that profits from it.

The full table is close enough to hide all of this. Charlotte sit No. 4 on 43 points and Chicago No. 5 on 39, and season records of 12-7-7 and 11-6-8 look like neighbours. It's only when you cut by venue that the gap opens: Charlotte's overall 1.65 points a game climbs at home, while Chicago's 1.56 sags away from it. That's the whole case in miniature.

The edge

Charlotte take 1.92 points a game at Bank of America Stadium; Chicago take 1.31 on the road.

Where the Split Actually Comes From

Home defending, not home scoring, drives it

The home number is built on defending. Charlotte concede 1 goal a game at home against 1.42 across the whole season, so the lift at Bank of America Stadium is mostly about keeping the ball out of their own net. Chicago run the other way: 1.52 conceded a game overall, rising to 1.85 away from home. Put those two venue figures side by side and you have the fixture's central mismatch.

Neither club is a clean sheet machine. Chicago have 6 clean sheets, a 24% rate, and Charlotte 5, a 19.2% rate, so a shutout isn't the base case. The attacks are nearly identical over the season, 1.81 goals a game for Charlotte and 1.8 for Chicago, 47 scored against 45. Goal difference of +10 against +7 is a small gap overall; the venue split is where it becomes a large one.

The mismatch

Charlotte concede 1 goal a game at home; Chicago concede 1.85 a game away from home.

The Recent Run Points the Same Way

Charlotte rising, Chicago stuck on draws

Form backs the venue story rather than fighting it. Charlotte arrive 4-2-0 over their last 6, taking 2.33 points a game and scoring 2 a fixture. Chicago are 0-4-2 over the same stretch at 0.67 points a game, and they haven't won in that run. One club is peaking at the right moment and the other is drifting.

There's a caveat worth making. Chicago's attack hasn't collapsed; they're still scoring 1.5 a game across the last 6, only a little below 1.8 on the season. The problem is turning draws into wins, 4 of them in that run, and a trip to the ground where Charlotte concede 1 a game is a hard place to fix that.

The trend

Charlotte are 4-2-0 over their last 6; Chicago are 0-4-2.

The Meetings Cut Against It

A small sample across two seasons

Here's the thread that complicates the picture. Across the 3 meetings on record, which span last season and this one, the 2025 and 2026 seasons, Charlotte are 0-0-3 against Chicago. They haven't taken a single point off this opponent in that sample. If you want the case for Chicago, it starts here.

The most recent of those meetings, on 2026-08-02, finished Charlotte 1, Chicago 2, and it belongs to this season rather than last. Fold it in with the 2025 season meetings and it's still only 3 fixtures, too thin to outweigh 13 home fixtures of evidence. It's a real reason to hold confidence down, though, not a reason to flip the call.

The caveat

Charlotte are 0-0-3 against Chicago across last season and this one, the 2025 and 2026 seasons.

What the Board Is Asking

The market leans home and leans under

The DraftKings board prices this two ways with the draw not priced, Charlotte at -165 and Chicago at +122, so neither number is the three way line a soccer reader expects. The lean is to the home club, and a venue split of 1.92 against 1.31 says that's the right direction. The more useful line is the handicap: Charlotte at -0.5 (+105), which needs a Charlotte win and pays better than even money.

The goals total sits at 3.5, with the under at -145 and the over at +105. Charlotte conceding 1 a game at home and Chicago scoring 1.5 a game over their last 6 both point toward the under, and the market has already shaded that way. We'd rather express the venue split through the handicap than pay that price on goals.

The play

Charlotte -0.5 (+105) is the cleanest way to back the home split.
