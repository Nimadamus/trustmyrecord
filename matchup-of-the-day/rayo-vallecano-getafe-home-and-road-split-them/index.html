Neighbours last season, opposites by venue now

The venue numbers separate two clubs that the table from last season could not

This is a LaLiga fixture at the Estadio Coliseum, and the cleanest way into it is the venue split. Getafe have taken 2 points a game at home this season from a 2-2-0 record across 4 fixtures, while Rayo Vallecano have managed 0.25 points a game away from home off a 0-1-3 record. Those samples are small, and they sit on top of a much flatter picture from last season, when Getafe finished No. 7 on 51 points and Rayo finished No. 8 on 50. Two clubs that were nearly inseparable over 38 fixtures last season arrive here from opposite ends of the venue split.

The reason to take the split seriously rather than wave it away is that it lines up with how each club defended last season. Getafe conceded 1 goal per game last season, and at home this term they're conceding 0.5 a game. Rayo conceded 1.16 a game across 38 fixtures last season, and away from home this season that has ballooned to 3 a game. A 4 fixture sample can't carry the whole argument, but the direction matches the club each was last season.

The edge

Neighbours in the table last season, a long way apart on venue this season: 2 points a game at home against 0.25 away.

Rayo haven't held firm anywhere yet

A road record of 0-1-3 from a club that lived on draws last season

Rayo's road numbers this season are the weakest part of either profile: a 0-1-3 record away from home across 4 fixtures, 0.25 points a game, and 3 goals conceded per game on the road. Add that they've kept 0 clean sheets in 7 completed fixtures, a 0% clean sheet rate, and you have a side that hasn't shut anyone out anywhere yet. That isn't entirely new, either; they conceded 44 goals in 38 fixtures last season.

What Rayo were last season was a draw machine: 12-14-12, a 31.6% win rate and a -3 goal difference. That profile travels badly into a ground where the home side is conceding 0.5 a game, because a club that lived on draws last season needs its own defence to hold. Away from home this season it hasn't held once, and that is the gap Getafe are being asked to exploit.

The edge

Rayo conceded 44 goals last season and are conceding 3 a game on the road this season; nothing in either number says the back line has fixed itself.

Getafe are a different club on their own ground

Unbeaten at the Coliseum this season after a volatile campaign last season

Getafe at the Estadio Coliseum this season read 2-2-0 across 4 fixtures, 2 points a game and 0.5 goals conceded a game. They've kept 2 clean sheets in 8 completed fixtures overall, a 25% clean sheet rate, which is modest; the home concession rate is where the shape shows. This is the same club that conceded 38 goals across 38 fixtures last season, 1 a game, and it simply does that job better on its own ground.

The catch is the other end of the record. Getafe went 15-6-17 last season with a 39.5% win rate, so they won more often than Rayo's 31.6% but also lost more often, a high variance club whose results hinged on whether a single goal arrived. That is exactly why the home split matters. At the Coliseum this season the losses that defined last season simply haven't come.

The edge

Unbeaten at home this season and conceding 0.5 a game there, from a club that conceded 1 a game last season.

The goals and the form point the other way

Rayo had the better attack last season and have it lately too

Goals are the honest counterweight to the venue story. Getafe scored 32 in 38 fixtures last season, 0.84 a game, against Rayo's 41 at 1.08 a game, and over the last 6 this season Getafe are scoring 0.5 a game to Rayo's 1.67. The goal differences from last season, -6 for Getafe and -3 for Rayo, say neither club dominated anybody.

Form leans Rayo's way too: 2-2-2 over the last 6 for 1.33 points a game, against Getafe's 1-2-3 and 0.83. So the overall picture favours the visitors while the venue picture favours the hosts, and that tension is the whole fixture. What tips it is that Rayo's attack, the better of the two last season and lately, has to produce at a ground where Getafe are conceding 0.5 a game this season.

The edge

Rayo scored more last season and score more lately; the Coliseum is where that edge has to survive.

The price follows form, the handicap follows the venue

Rayo are favoured on the board despite the split and the table from last season

The FanDuel board has Rayo as the favourite at +159, Getafe at +178 and the draw at +210, which reads like a price built on form and on the better attack from last season rather than on venue. The meetings back that lean: Getafe are 0-1-1 against Rayo across the 2 on record, and the most recent ended Getafe 0, Rayo 2. The table from last season, 51 points to 50, gave no reason to make either club a clear favourite.

Where the venue split earns its keep is on the handicap. Getafe +0.5 at -210 pays on a home win or a draw, and that's the only kind of result Getafe have had at home this season, 2-2-0. The total sits at 1.5 with the over at -200 and the under at +145; Getafe scoring 0.5 a game over the last 6 makes a case for the under, though Rayo conceding 3 a game on the road argues against it. Two clubs this close last season don't justify a strong position, but the split justifies a lean.

The edge

Getafe +0.5 covers a win or a draw, the only two results they've had at home this season, against a club that finished right alongside them last season.
