The Table Is Not Being Subtle

First hosts eleventh, and the points per fixture show the gap

This is Serie A, and after 9 fixtures the table isn't being subtle. Lazio sit No. 1 on 25 points from an 8-1-0 record, which works out to 2.78 points a fixture. Cagliari arrive at the Olimpico in No. 11 on 13 points at 4-1-4, taking 1.44 a fixture. Put plainly, Lazio are banking close to twice what Cagliari bank every time they play, and that's the whole story of this match.

The win rates tell it the same way. Lazio have won 88.9% of their league fixtures; Cagliari have won 44.4%, which is respectable mid table form and nothing more. The leaders haven't lost yet, and a single draw is the only time they've dropped points. Cagliari's 4-1-4 is the record of a club that wins its share and loses its share, and the table has put them exactly where that deserves.

The edge

Lazio take 2.78 points a fixture, Cagliari 1.44. Everything else on this page is commentary on that gap.

The Goals Behind the Points

Goal difference explains the table without much help

Points come from goals, and the goal columns explain the table without needing much help. Lazio have scored 22 and conceded 7 for a goal difference of +15. Cagliari have scored 8 and conceded 13, which leaves them on -5. Per fixture, that's 2.44 scored against 0.78 conceded for Lazio, and 0.89 scored against 1.44 conceded for Cagliari.

The Cagliari attack is the bigger problem of the two. A club scoring 0.89 a fixture has to keep clean sheets to collect points, and Cagliari have managed 3, a clean sheet rate of 33.3%. Lazio have 4 clean sheets, 44.4% of their fixtures, while scoring freely at the other end. Stingy at the back and productive up front is how a club ends up at No. 1.

The edge

Cagliari score 0.89 a fixture. Against a defence conceding 0.78, there is almost no margin for error.

Cagliari Travel Better Than They Look

The one split that narrows the gap, and why it shouldn't mislead you

The one wrinkle for Cagliari is that they've been better on their travels than the overall number suggests. Away from home they're 3-0-2 across 5 fixtures, taking 1.8 points a fixture, comfortably above their 1.44 overall. Lazio at the Olimpico are 3-1-0 in 4, taking 2.5 points a fixture, a touch below their season rate of 2.78.

Don't read too much into that convergence. Cagliari's away fixtures have also been open: they concede 1.8 a fixture away from home, more than their season figure of 1.44. Lazio concede 1 a fixture at home, slightly worse than their overall 0.78, but that's still a defence Cagliari's 0.89 goals a fixture is unlikely to trouble often. Samples of 4 and 5 fixtures are thin, and the 9 fixture table is the sturdier read.

The edge

Cagliari take 1.8 points a fixture away from home, but they also concede 1.8 there.

Form Widens the Gap Rather Than Closing It

The last six results point the same way as the table

Recent form doesn't give Cagliari any cover either. Over their last 6, Lazio are 5-1-0, taking 2.67 points a fixture and scoring 3 a fixture. That isn't a leader drifting back to the pack; if anything the attack has sharpened, with recent scoring running above their season rate of 2.44.

Cagliari's last 6 read 2-1-3, worth 1.17 points a fixture, which is below their own season pace of 1.44. They've scored 1 a fixture over that stretch, a slight uptick on 0.89 overall, yet the results have gone the wrong way. A mid table club trending down meets a leader holding steady, and the table gap is likelier to widen than close.

The edge

Lazio's last 6: 5-1-0 with 3 goals a fixture. The leaders are not slowing down.

The Only Crumb of Comfort Comes From Last Season

Two meetings across the 2025 and 2026 seasons, one of them a stalemate

The head to head is the one place Cagliari can find some encouragement, and there isn't much of it. The record holds just 2 meetings across the 2025 and 2026 seasons, and Lazio are 1-1-0 in them. That's unbeaten for Lazio but hardly a rout, and 2 matches from those earlier seasons say very little next to this season's table.

The most recent meeting came last season and finished Lazio 0, Cagliari 0. Cagliari earned that draw by keeping the match tight, which is the only realistic template they have here. Still, last season's stalemate came before Lazio put together a campaign that has produced 25 points from an 8-1-0 start this season.

The edge

Last season's meeting finished Lazio 0, Cagliari 0, and a stalemate is still Cagliari's best hope.

The Market Reads the Table the Same Way

What the FanDuel board says about the gap

The FanDuel board has Lazio at -209, Cagliari at +550 and the draw at +300. That's the market pricing the table honestly: a No. 1 club winning 88.9% of its fixtures hosting the club in No. 11. The handicap has Lazio -1.5 at +125, which asks the leaders to win by two clear goals.

The goals total sits at 2.5, with the over at -131 and the under at -102. Lazio's 2.44 a fixture and Cagliari's 1.44 conceded both lean toward goals, but Cagliari's own 0.89 scoring rate means most of that total has to come from one end. The cleanest expression of the central story is still the plain result, and -209 is a fair ask for a club taking 2.78 points a fixture.

The edge

At -209 the market agrees with the table; the only question is whether Cagliari can turn it into a slog.
