San Francisco caught the break of the week before anybody hit anybody

ESPN Data

Aaron Donald is on the week 1 report as Out, and the reason given is a coach decision.

The 49ers open their season in Australia on Thursday night and they’ll do it without having to account for the one player on the other roster who has historically decided games by himself. Aaron Donald is listed Out on the week 1 injury report, and the reason attached to it isn’t a hamstring or a knee. It’s a coach decision. He’s 35, he’s still carrying number 99 on the active roster, and he will not be on the field.

If you want to know what that’s worth in the abstract, the career file answers it quickly. Across 10 recorded seasons, from 2014 through the 2023 season: 111.0 sacks in 154 games, 542 tackles, 24 forced fumbles and 109.5 tackles for loss. In the 2018 season alone he had 20.5 sacks from the interior, which is a number edge rushers build entire careers on and he did it from over the guard. Nobody game plans a defensive tackle the way people have had to game plan this one.

So yes, on the face of it San Francisco got lucky. You do not want to open a season by sending a line that gave up 27 sacks last year at a front that finished 7th in the league with 47 of them, and you especially don’t want to do it with the most disruptive interior player of his generation lined up over your center. That version of Thursday night is gone.

Now the uncomfortable part, because the Rams didn’t get those sacks from him

ESPN Data

Los Angeles finished with 47 sacks last season and the season log has no Donald row after 2023.

Here’s the number that should cool the celebration down. Los Angeles piled up 47 sacks in 2025 and you can account for almost all of them without ever writing his name. Byron Young had 12. Jared Verse had 7.5. Kobie Turner had 7. Braden Fiske had 3. That’s 29.5 from four players who are all still here, and the last season with a recorded Donald stat line is 2023.

That reframes the whole thing. A Rams front that ranked 7th without needing him last year is not suddenly ordinary because he sits again. Young alone put up 12, which is more than half of what the entire San Francisco defense managed, and Turner and Fiske are the interior pair that actually took those interior snaps. The 49ers didn’t dodge the Rams pass rush on Thursday. They dodged a name.

What the absence is actually worthTMR Research

A missing star is worth the snaps he was going to take, not the reputation he arrives with. If he was going to play thirty snaps in a rotation, the break is thirty snaps of a slightly better guard matchup, and the four men who produced last season’s sack total are all still dressing. Treat it as a small edge that the market has probably already taken, and you will be about right.

The bigger problem is the trench nobody is talking about

ESPN Data

San Francisco finished last in the league in sacks and it is the club with the longer injury list.

San Francisco recorded 20 sacks in 2025. That was 32nd, which is another way of saying nobody in the league recorded fewer. Los Angeles had 47. That is a gap of 27 sacks between two clubs that finished with the same 12-5 record, and it is the single widest measured difference between them going into Thursday.

The run game does not bail that out either. The 49ers averaged 106.9 rushing yards at 3.8 a carry last season, which ranked 24th and 30th, and that is the profile of an offense that has to throw when it falls behind. Against a defense that got home 47 times, having to throw is the condition you least want to be in.

  • Alfred Collins is Out with a knee, which thins the same defensive front that finished last in sacks. San Francisco, 20 sacks in 2025
  • George Kittle and Dre Greenlaw are both Questionable with Achilles issues, and Renardo Green with a hamstring.
  • Los Angeles has 3 players on injured reserve and 1 more Questionable, and its one Out is the coach decision on Donald.

Put the two lists side by side and the club that woke up healthier is not the one that got the good news. San Francisco has 5 names on the report and 4 of them are pieces it actually planned to use on Thursday. Los Angeles has 5 as well, but 3 of those are already parked on injured reserve and were never in this week’s plan to begin with.

And they’re playing it on a cricket ground on the other side of the world

ESPN Data

A neutral site with Los Angeles as the designated home team, at the Melbourne Cricket Ground.

This one isn’t at Levi’s and it isn’t at SoFi. It’s at the Melbourne Cricket Ground, it’s a neutral site, and Los Angeles is carrying the home designation anyway. Kickoff is 5:35 PM Pacific on Thursday, which is 10:35 on Friday morning in Melbourne, so both clubs are playing a body clock game as much as a football game.

The market has this at Los Angeles -3.5 with a total of 48.5, and ESPN’s own model gives the Rams a 60.5 percent chance against 39.2 for San Francisco. Read those two together and you get the honest picture of the Donald news: a projection that sits a bit under a touchdown of separation, from a model that already knows who is playing.

The November 2025 game is the one I keep looking at. Los Angeles went into San Francisco and won 42-26, which is a scoreline you post when the offense that led the league at 30.5 points a game gets to play its normal week. Nothing about Thursday changes that offense. The news is on the other side of their ball.

The read

One call, the reasons behind it, and what would talk me out of it.

The break is real and it is smaller than it sounds. San Francisco avoids the hardest individual assignment in football and still has to block the four men who produced 29.5 of last season’s 47 sacks, while asking a pass rush that finished 32nd to slow down the offense that finished 1st in scoring. That is the game, and Aaron Donald sitting does not change the shape of it.

If you came here for the headline, take it: the 49ers got lucky. Just price the luck honestly. It is worth a few snaps against a better interior matchup, not a touchdown, and the club that still has to prove something on Thursday night is the one that could not get to the quarterback all of last season.

Why

What would change it

  • The 49ers running the ball better than the 106.9 yards a game and 3.8 a carry they managed last season, which is how you keep this front honest.
  • George Kittle and Dre Greenlaw both clearing Questionable and playing normal snap counts.
  • A neutral site and a 10:35 AM local kickoff flattening the home designation Los Angeles is carrying on paper.
  • The number moving past a field goal and a half, at which point you are paying for the same opinion at a worse price.
