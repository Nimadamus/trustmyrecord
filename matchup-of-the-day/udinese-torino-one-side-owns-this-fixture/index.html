Udinese have won every meeting on record

Two fixtures, two Udinese wins, the latest last season

This is Serie A at the Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino, and the head to head is the cleanest thing either club brings to it. Torino's record against Udinese across the 2 meetings on record reads 0-0-2, and the most recent of them came last season, finishing Torino 0, Udinese 2. There's no draw to muddy it and no narrow scoreline to argue over. Udinese simply left with everything both times.

Two meetings is a small sample, and nobody should pretend it's a law of nature. But the shape of last season's meeting matters: Torino failed to score at all, and Udinese found the net twice. Set beside how the two clubs finished last season, the 0-0-2 looks less like a fluke and more like a fair reflection of which side was the better outfit.

Last season's table already split them

Udinese finished above Torino last season

Udinese finished last season No. 10 in Serie A with 50 points from a 14-8-16 record, while Torino finished No. 12 on 45 points at 12-9-17. That's 1.32 points per game for Udinese last season against 1.18 for Torino. The gap isn't dramatic, but it runs the same way as the head to head.

The win rates last season sharpen it a little: Udinese won 36.8% of their fixtures, Torino 31.6%. Neither number belongs to a club that dominates anyone. What it does show is that the 0-0-2 sits on top of a modest but real gap in last season's table, which is why it doesn't look out of place.

The difference last season was at the back

Similar attacks, very different defences last season

The real separation last season was in defence. Udinese conceded 48 in Serie A and Torino 63, and that's the whole distance between a goal difference of -3 and one of -19. Going forward they were nearly identical last season, 45 scored against 44, or 1.18 goals per game against 1.16.

Torino conceded 1.66 per game last season to Udinese's 1.26, and that kind of leak is exactly how a side ends up blanked and beaten in a meeting like this one. Neither club has kept a clean sheet in 5 fixtures this season, a 0% rate for both. So nothing this term suggests Torino have fixed what went wrong last season.
