The offense against that defense

A scoring rate meets a defense that has not given points up

Notre Dame has scored 44 points a game. BYU has allowed 13.4. That is the matchup. A visiting offense that has been putting points on the board meets a defense that has not been giving them up, and the gap is more than a touchdown.

The records are 6-0 and 5-0, and both of those are still early. Notre Dame's figure rests on 6 games. BYU's rests on 5 games. The rates are measured. They are not a finished season, and a Saturday in October can still make them look like a small sample.

The edge

Notre Dame scores 44. BYU has allowed 13.4. That gap is why this game was selected.

How Notre Dame has been scoring

Completions, explosives, and a real run game

Notre Dame has thrown for 261.8 yards a game and completed 70.2 percent of its passes, at 9.24 yards per attempt. The rush adds 194.8 yards a game at 5.1 a carry. This is not a one note offense. The throw is efficient and the run still has volume.

The total is 456.7 yards a game and 22.5 first downs. The points follow from that, 44 a game, with a point differential of +198. The differential is the whole season so far, six games of it, not one afternoon that got out of hand.

The edge

What BYU has actually stopped

Points allowed, and a building that has not lost

BYU has allowed 13.4 points a game and is 5-0, including 3-0 at home. The points allowed are the defense. The home record is the building this game is being played in. Those are different facts, and both of them are on the page because both of them change the lean.

The Cougars have 11 sacks. That is the pressure number we have, and it is not a loud one. The points allowed are the stronger fact. A defense can keep the score down without living in the backfield, and that is the profile here.

The edge

BYU has allowed 13.4 points a game and is 3-0 at home.

The same gap runs the other way

BYU can score, and Notre Dame has not allowed it

BYU has scored 34.6 points a game. Notre Dame has allowed 11. The visiting offense is the headline, and the home offense against that defense is the reason the headline is not the whole game. Two good scoring teams are walking into two defenses that have both been stingy.

BYU has rushed for 223.8 yards a game at 6 yards a carry, with 209.6 through the air. Notre Dame has 15 sacks and a turnover differential of +8, from 9 takeaways and 1 giveaway. The Irish defense has been the one taking the ball away.

The edge

Notre Dame allows 11 points a game. BYU scores 34.6. One of those has to give.

Third down and the building

BYU converts, and has not lost at home

BYU has converted third downs at 54.4 percent. Notre Dame has converted them at 44.6 percent. The Cougars have been the better chain movers. That is a different edge from the points, and it belongs to the home side.

This is at LaVell Edwards Stadium, where BYU is 3-0. Notre Dame is 2-0 on the road. The road record is two wins in an early season, the same way the 6-0 and the 5-0 are early. The building belongs to the home side. The points, so far, belong to the visitor.

The edge

BYU converts third downs at 54.4 percent and is 3-0 at home.
