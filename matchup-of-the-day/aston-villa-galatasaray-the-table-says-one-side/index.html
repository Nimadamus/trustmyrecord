Two Tables, One Clear Gap

Galatasaray top their league; Aston Villa sit mid table in theirs.

This is a UEFA Champions League fixture at RAMS Park, and the cleanest way into it is the two domestic tables. Galatasaray sit No. 1 in the Turkish Super Lig with 31 points from 12 fixtures, a 10-1-1 record that works out to 2.58 points per match. Aston Villa are No. 12 in the Premier League on 14 points from 11 fixtures, a 3-5-3 record and 1.27 points per match. The gap isn't subtle, and it's the whole argument of this preview.

Neither league position counts for anything in the Champions League itself; a domestic rank earns no points on this pitch. What it does tell you is how often each club walks off with something to show for ninety minutes. Galatasaray have won 83.3% of their league fixtures this season, Aston Villa 27.3% of theirs. Everything else below either supports that fact or complicates it.

The edge

Galatasaray win 83.3% of their league fixtures; Aston Villa win 27.3%.

The Goals Behind the Points

The scoring and conceding numbers point the same way as the table.

Points can flatter a side that keeps winning by the odd goal, so it's worth checking the ledger. Galatasaray have scored 29 and conceded 16 across 12 league fixtures this season, a goal difference of +13, which is 2.42 scored per match against 1.33 conceded. Aston Villa have scored 14 and conceded 17 across 11, leaving them on -3 with 1.27 scored and 1.55 conceded per match. The table isn't lying about either club.

The honest complication is league strength, and nothing behind this page measures it. A Super Lig goal difference and a Premier League goal difference don't sit on the same scale, which is how a gap this size can coexist with a price that disagrees. Galatasaray aren't airtight either: a 33.3% clean sheet rate, 4 shutouts in 12, against 27.3% and 3 for Villa. If the home side has a soft spot, it's at the back rather than in front of goal.

The edge

A +13 goal difference against -3 says the points gap is earned, not lucky.

Form Only Widens It

Galatasaray have taken every point lately; Villa have stopped losing.

If the season numbers are the case for the prosecution, recent form is the cross examination, and it doesn't do Villa many favours. Galatasaray are 6-0-0 across their last 6 league fixtures, the maximum 3 points per match, scoring 2.67 a match along the way. Villa are better than their season line suggests: 2-4-0 over their last 6, for 1.67 points per match and 1.67 goals per match.

That Villa run deserves real credit. Unbeaten is unbeaten, and 1.67 points per match is a clear step up from the 1.27 they've managed across the full campaign. But four draws in six describes a side that's become hard to beat rather than one that's winning. Against a club taking every point on offer, the gap hasn't closed so much as stopped growing.

The edge

Galatasaray are 6-0-0 in their last 6; Villa are 2-4-0.

RAMS Park Sharpens the Picture

Villa travel decently, but they concede on the road.

The venue makes the table look even starker. Galatasaray are 6-1-0 at home in the league this season, taking 2.71 points per match and conceding 1.43 a match at RAMS Park. Villa have actually travelled reasonably well, 2-2-1 away from home for 1.6 points per match, a better rate than their overall 1.27.

The catch in Villa's road numbers is the defending: 2 goals conceded per match away from home. Set that beside a home attack scoring 2.42 a match across the season and the risk for the visitors is plain. Villa should find chances of their own against a defence giving up 1.43 at home, but they'd need to outscore a side unbeaten in 7 home league fixtures.

The edge

Galatasaray take 2.71 points per match at home; Villa concede 2 a match on the road.

Where the Market Parts Company

The club taking fewer points is the favourite.

Here's where the table and the board disagree. DraftKings has Aston Villa as the favourite at +120, with Galatasaray at +200 and the draw at +280. The club taking 2.58 points per match is the outsider against the club taking 1.27. That's the market leaning hard on league strength, trusting a Premier League sample over a Super Lig one.

You can argue that adjustment is fair; you can't argue it's small. Galatasaray on the handicap at +0.5 (-160) covers both the home win and the draw, the conservative way to back the table. The goals line sits at 3.5 with the over at +120 and the under at -156, so the books aren't expecting a shootout, even with a home attack at 2.42 per match and a Villa defence conceding 2 a match on the road.

The edge

The table says Galatasaray; the price says Villa at +120. One of them is wrong.
