What this Lightning vs Panthers simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Tampa Bay Lightning at Florida Panthers and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Tampa Bay Lightning put 28.1 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 26.7; Florida Panthers put up 28.0 and allowed 26.8. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Andrei Vasilevskiy and Jacob Markstrom are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Tampa Bay Lightning ran a 20.7% power play against a 81.0% kill, and Florida Panthers ran 19.5% against 82.6%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Lightning a 66.0% favourite, projected a total of 6.86 goals, and sent 20.9% of games past regulation.
