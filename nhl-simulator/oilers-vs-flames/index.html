What this Oilers vs Flames simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Edmonton Oilers at Calgary Flames and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Edmonton Oilers put 29.7 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 26.7; Calgary Flames put up 28.1 and allowed 29.9. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Frederik Andersen and Dustin Wolf are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Edmonton Oilers ran a 30.6% power play against a 80.4% kill, and Calgary Flames ran 16.2% against 77.8%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Flames a 50.1% favourite, projected a total of 6.89 goals, and sent 22.1% of games past regulation.
