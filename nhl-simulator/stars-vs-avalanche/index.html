What this Stars vs Avalanche simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Dallas Stars at Colorado Avalanche and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Dallas Stars put 25.3 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 26.2; Colorado Avalanche put up 33.7 and allowed 26.1. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Jake Oettinger and Scott Wedgewood are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Dallas Stars ran a 28.6% power play against a 84.6% kill, and Colorado Avalanche ran 17.1% against 80.3%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Avalanche a 64.4% favourite, projected a total of 6.22 goals, and sent 22.8% of games past regulation.
