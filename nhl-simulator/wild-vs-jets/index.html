What this Wild vs Jets simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Minnesota Wild at Winnipeg Jets and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Minnesota Wild put 29.2 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 29.4; Winnipeg Jets put up 26.4 and allowed 27.8. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Filip Gustavsson and Connor Hellebuyck are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Minnesota Wild ran a 25.2% power play against a 77.6% kill, and Winnipeg Jets ran 18.5% against 79.8%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Wild a 54.9% favourite, projected a total of 6.36 goals, and sent 23.1% of games past regulation.
