What this Sharks vs Kings simulation is

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. San Jose Sharks put 25.8 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 29.6; Los Angeles Kings put up 28.0 and allowed 27.2. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Yaroslav Askarov and Darcy Kuemper are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. San Jose Sharks ran a 21.2% power play against a 74.6% kill, and Los Angeles Kings ran 17.0% against 76.4%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Kings a 64.4% favourite, projected a total of 6.87 goals, and sent 21.2% of games past regulation.
