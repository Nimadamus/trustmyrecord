What this Golden Knights vs Avalanche simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Vegas Golden Knights at Colorado Avalanche and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Vegas Golden Knights put 29.0 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 24.4; Colorado Avalanche put up 33.7 and allowed 26.1. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Adin Hill and Scott Wedgewood are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Vegas Golden Knights ran a 24.6% power play against a 84.6% kill, and Colorado Avalanche ran 17.1% against 81.4%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Avalanche a 75.8% favourite, projected a total of 6.60 goals, and sent 20.2% of games past regulation.
