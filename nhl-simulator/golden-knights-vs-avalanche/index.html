How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Vegas Golden Knights put 29.4 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 24.1; Colorado Avalanche put up 33.6 and allowed 26.0. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Carter Hart and Scott Wedgewood are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Vegas Golden Knights ran a 25.4% power play against a 86.0% kill, and Colorado Avalanche ran 19.2% against 81.6%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Avalanche a 66.9% favourite, projected a total of 6.24 goals, and sent 21.4% of games past regulation.
