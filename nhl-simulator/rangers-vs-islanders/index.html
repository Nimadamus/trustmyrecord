What this Rangers vs Islanders simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on New York Rangers at New York Islanders and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. New York Rangers put 25.2 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 28.8; New York Islanders put up 28.2 and allowed 27.7. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Igor Shesterkin and Ilya Sorokin are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. New York Rangers ran a 24.7% power play against a 80.8% kill, and New York Islanders ran 16.5% against 79.9%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Rangers a 50.1% favourite, projected a total of 5.97 goals, and sent 23.8% of games past regulation.
