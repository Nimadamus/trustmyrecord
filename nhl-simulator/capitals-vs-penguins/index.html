What this Capitals vs Penguins simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Washington Capitals at Pittsburgh Penguins and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Washington Capitals put 28.0 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 28.1; Pittsburgh Penguins put up 28.6 and allowed 27.4. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Logan Thompson and Arturs Silovs are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Washington Capitals ran a 17.8% power play against a 81.4% kill, and Pittsburgh Penguins ran 24.1% against 80.1%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Capitals a 53.5% favourite, projected a total of 7.14 goals, and sent 21.6% of games past regulation.
