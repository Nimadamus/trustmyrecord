How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Montréal Canadiens put 25.6 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 28.3; Toronto Maple Leafs put up 27.2 and allowed 30.9. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Jakub Dobes and Sergei Bobrovsky are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Montréal Canadiens ran a 21.9% power play against a 82.4% kill, and Toronto Maple Leafs ran 21.3% against 77.9%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Canadiens a 57.3% favourite, projected a total of 6.82 goals, and sent 21.8% of games past regulation.
