What this Canadiens vs Maple Leafs simulation is

The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Montréal Canadiens put 26.3 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 27.8; Toronto Maple Leafs put up 26.3 and allowed 32.4. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Jakub Dobes and Sergei Bobrovsky are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Montréal Canadiens ran a 23.1% power play against a 81.2% kill, and Toronto Maple Leafs ran 21.3% against 78.2%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Canadiens a 64.1% favourite, projected a total of 7.40 goals, and sent 20.6% of games past regulation.
