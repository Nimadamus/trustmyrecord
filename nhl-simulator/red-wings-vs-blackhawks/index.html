What this Red Wings vs Blackhawks simulation is

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Detroit Red Wings put 28.2 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 27.7; Chicago Blackhawks put up 24.6 and allowed 30.0. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: John Gibson and Spencer Knight are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Detroit Red Wings ran a 22.6% power play against a 83.6% kill, and Chicago Blackhawks ran 16.9% against 77.1%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Red Wings a 55.6% favourite, projected a total of 6.32 goals, and sent 23.1% of games past regulation.
