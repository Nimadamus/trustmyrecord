What this Bruins vs Rangers simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Boston Bruins at New York Rangers and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Boston Bruins put 27.0 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 29.7; New York Rangers put up 25.2 and allowed 28.8. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Jeremy Swayman and Igor Shesterkin are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Boston Bruins ran a 23.4% power play against a 79.9% kill, and New York Rangers ran 24.7% against 77.0%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Rangers a 51.9% favourite, projected a total of 6.28 goals, and sent 23.6% of games past regulation.
