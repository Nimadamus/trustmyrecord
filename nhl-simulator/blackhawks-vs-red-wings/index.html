What this Blackhawks vs Red Wings simulation is

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Chicago Blackhawks put 24.6 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 30.0; Detroit Red Wings put up 28.2 and allowed 27.7. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Spencer Knight and John Gibson are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Chicago Blackhawks ran a 16.9% power play against a 77.1% kill, and Detroit Red Wings ran 22.6% against 83.6%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Red Wings a 63.5% favourite, projected a total of 6.28 goals, and sent 22.1% of games past regulation.
