How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Toronto Maple Leafs put 27.2 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 30.9; Boston Bruins put up 26.5 and allowed 29.2. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Sergei Bobrovsky and Jeremy Swayman are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Toronto Maple Leafs ran a 21.3% power play against a 76.5% kill, and Boston Bruins ran 20.7% against 82.4%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Bruins a 65.3% favourite, projected a total of 6.45 goals, and sent 22.0% of games past regulation.
