What this Sabres vs Bruins simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Buffalo Sabres at Boston Bruins and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Buffalo Sabres put 28.1 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 29.1; Boston Bruins put up 27.0 and allowed 29.7. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Alex Lyon and Jeremy Swayman are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Buffalo Sabres ran a 19.5% power play against a 77.0% kill, and Boston Bruins ran 23.4% against 81.9%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Sabres a 50.7% favourite, projected a total of 6.64 goals, and sent 22.7% of games past regulation.
