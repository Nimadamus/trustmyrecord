What this Avalanche vs Golden Knights simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Colorado Avalanche at Vegas Golden Knights and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Colorado Avalanche put 33.7 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 26.1; Vegas Golden Knights put up 29.0 and allowed 24.4. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Scott Wedgewood and Adin Hill are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Colorado Avalanche ran a 17.1% power play against a 81.4% kill, and Vegas Golden Knights ran 24.6% against 84.6%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Avalanche a 69.1% favourite, projected a total of 6.60 goals, and sent 20.9% of games past regulation.
