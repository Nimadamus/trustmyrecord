What this Flames vs Oilers simulation is

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Calgary Flames put 28.1 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 29.9; Edmonton Oilers put up 29.7 and allowed 26.7. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Dustin Wolf and Frederik Andersen are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Calgary Flames ran a 16.2% power play against a 77.8% kill, and Edmonton Oilers ran 30.6% against 80.4%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Oilers a 58.5% favourite, projected a total of 6.84 goals, and sent 22.2% of games past regulation.
