How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Calgary Flames put 28.0 shots on goal a game (season blend) and allowed 29.9; Edmonton Oilers put up 27.4 and allowed 26.8. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Dustin Wolf and Matt Tomkins are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Calgary Flames ran a 20.9% power play against a 76.5% kill, and Edmonton Oilers ran 31.8% against 81.7%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Oilers a 67.0% favourite, projected a total of 6.18 goals, and sent 21.3% of games past regulation.
