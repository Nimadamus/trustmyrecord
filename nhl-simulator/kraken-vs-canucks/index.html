What this Kraken vs Canucks simulation is

This page runs the TrustMyRecord NHL simulator on Seattle Kraken at Vancouver Canucks and prints the result of 25,000 simulated games. The numbers above are the average of those runs: the projected score, win probability, total and overtime share all come out of the same shot-and-conversion model that powers the full simulator. To play one game out period by period and get shots on goal, both goaltender lines and a complete box score, open it live.

How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Seattle Kraken put 25.6 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 29.5; Vancouver Canucks put up 26.0 and allowed 29.8. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Joey Daccord and Kevin Lankinen are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Seattle Kraken ran a 19.5% power play against a 71.5% kill, and Vancouver Canucks ran 21.8% against 72.2%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Kraken a 60.7% favourite, projected a total of 7.00 goals, and sent 21.9% of games past regulation.
