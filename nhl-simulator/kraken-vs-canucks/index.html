How the projection is built

Each team's shot volume is its own shots-for rate met against the opponent's shots-against rate. Seattle Kraken put 25.8 shots on goal a game last season and allowed 29.1; Vancouver Canucks put up 25.2 and allowed 29.9. Conversion combines each team's shooting with the goaltender actually in the other net, which is why the starter matters here: Joey Daccord and Kevin Lankinen are the projected starters, and swapping either one in the live simulator visibly moves the projection.

Special teams are simulated rather than assumed. Seattle Kraken ran a 20.2% power play against a 71.2% kill, and Vancouver Canucks ran 21.9% against 72.3%. Across 25,000 runs the model made Kraken a 57.5% favourite, projected a total of 6.67 goals, and sent 22.1% of games past regulation.
