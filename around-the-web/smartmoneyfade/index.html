How Closing Line Value Is Measured Here
TMR records the closing price as the last price its odds collector saw within 10 minutes before the scheduled start, from one book in a fixed priority order (DraftKings first). CLV is calculated only when that close is at the exact line the handicapper posted, and only for picks that posted a price. Avg CLV (posted price) compares the posted price to the closing price as quoted, so it still includes the book's hold. Avg CLV (no vig) removes that hold using both sides of the same book's closing market and appears only where both sides were captured. This is one book's close, not a market wide consensus, and it does not cover player props.
