Handicappers Around the Web

The internet never forgets the picks. Neither do we.

Every day, sports handicappers post their picks across social media, forums and betting communities. TrustMyRecord tracks those publicly posted selections and follows the results over time, giving bettors a transparent look at the complete record: wins, losses, units, streaks and everything in between.

We don’t rely on claims, screenshots or selective records. When a qualifying pick is posted publicly and our system can verify it, we track it.

Our goal is simple: bring transparency and accountability to sports handicapping, give bettors better information, and help people make smarter decisions.

Because at the end of the day, we want the same thing you do:

To win, and beat the books.

Public picks. Independent tracking. Complete records.

How this works: TMR reads public pick threads and posts, saves each pick exactly as written with the time it was posted, and grades it after the game ends. Units use 1 unit risked per pick and only picks that posted a price; picks posted without a price count in the record but not in units or ROI. Pending picks are never shown.
