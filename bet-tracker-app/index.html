Why bettors use a pick tracking app

Most bettors keep their record in their head, a notes app or a spreadsheet they stopped updating in week three. The wins get remembered, the losses get rounded off, and nobody else can check any of it.

A pick tracking app fixes that by doing the bookkeeping for you. TrustMyRecord goes one step further: every pick is locked and timestamped before the game starts, so your record is something you can actually show people, not just something you tell them.

Bet tracker app FAQ

Is there a TrustMyRecord app?

Yes. TrustMyRecord runs in your phone browser and installs to your home screen like an app. On iPhone, open trustmyrecord.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Is the bet tracker app free?

Yes. Creating an account, logging picks and building your public record is free. TMR Pro is an optional paid tier for research tools, not for tracking.

Do I have to connect my sportsbook account?

No. You log the pick yourself before the game, so you never hand over a sportsbook login. Every pick is timestamped when you submit it and graded automatically when the game ends.

What does the app track?

Net units, ROI, win percentage, average odds, current and longest streaks, and splits by sport and bet type for MLB, NBA, NFL, NHL, college sports and soccer.

How is this different from a betting spreadsheet or a notes app?

A spreadsheet can be edited after the result is known. TrustMyRecord locks the pick before the game, keeps every loss on the record and gives you one public link that anyone can check.
