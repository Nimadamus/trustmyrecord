How TMR Coin Works

TMR Coin is an internal reward currency for genuine activity on TrustMyRecord. Here's exactly how it's earned, what limits apply, and what it can never be used for.

TMR Coin is an internal promotional platform currency. It has no cash value. It cannot be purchased, transferred, redeemed for money, withdrawn, or cashed out -- today or at any point in the future without an explicit, separate announcement.

How you earn TMR Coin

Every reward is tied to something you actually did, verified server-side -- never something a client app merely claims happened. Each source has its own daily or per-event limit so no single action can be farmed for unlimited coins.

Contest placement/win bonuses exist as a planned reward category but are not active yet -- the exact tiers haven't been finalized. Forum-post rewards are also not active: a post can be deleted shortly after it's made, and we're not paying out on something that can be undone that easily.

What TMR Coin can't do

  • Cannot be purchased with real money
  • Cannot be transferred between accounts
  • Cannot be redeemed for cash, goods, or services
  • Cannot be withdrawn
  • Cannot be cashed out under any circumstance

The rewards catalog shows examples of future perks as "coming soon" previews only -- nothing in it is available to redeem today.

Frozen and reversed transactions

If your wallet is frozen, you'll see a clear banner on your wallet page. A frozen wallet can't earn or spend coins, but your balance and complete transaction history stay visible and are never deleted.

Every admin adjustment -- a grant, a deduction, or a reversal -- requires a written reason and shows up in your transaction history with a timestamp, so nothing about your balance ever changes silently.
