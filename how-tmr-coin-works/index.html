What TMR Coin is

TMR Coin is a rewards-points balance recorded in TrustMyRecord's own systems. Points recognise taking part — posting picks, starting discussions, being here.

Points are not money. They are not a cryptocurrency, not a token, not a security, not an investment, and not a cash-equivalent or stored-value asset. They have no cash value, are not redeemable for cash, are not traded on any exchange, and cannot be transferred off the site.

How members earn points

These are the ways to earn points that are active today:

  • 25 points — creating your account (once)
  • 10 points — posting your first pick (once)
  • 5 points — starting your first forum thread (once)
  • 1 point — each forum thread you start

Ways to earn change over time. The list above is what is currently active. TrustMyRecord may add, change or remove earning opportunities at any time, and may also grant points at its discretion.

How points can be used

  • Covering a BetLegend Pro research request after your free daily one is used
  • Items offered in the rewards catalogue
  • Tipping other members for a good post or a good call

Points can reduce what you pay us. They are never converted into money in either direction: you cannot buy points, and points cannot be cashed out.

What you should know

  • Points are not property and confer no rights.
  • Points carry no guarantee of value, availability or redemption.
  • TrustMyRecord may change how points work, what they can be used for, or end the programme entirely. If the programme ends, unused points expire without compensation.
  • Points obtained through abuse, automation, duplicate accounts or error may be withheld, reversed or cancelled.
Nobody at TrustMyRecord will ever ask you to buy TMR Coin, send funds, connect a wallet, or pay a network fee. Any message, DM, or site offering to sell you TMR Coin is a scam.

Frequently asked questions

Is TMR Coin a cryptocurrency?

No. Points exist only in TrustMyRecord's own records.

Can I buy or sell it?

No. Points cannot be purchased and are not sold. There is no trading or exchange of any kind.

Can I cash out?

No. Points have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

Can I send points to another member?

Yes — you can tip points to other members inside the site. Points cannot be moved off TrustMyRecord.

Where do I see my balance?

On your wallet page, with your full transaction history.
