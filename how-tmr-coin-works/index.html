TMR Coin

TMR Coin is a cryptocurrency: a fixed supply ERC-20 token on Base. What you earn and use on TrustMyRecord is different: site credits, an internal balance in your TrustMyRecord wallet. Site credits and the TMR token are separate, and nothing converts one into the other in either direction.

What the balance on this site is

The balance shown in your wallet here is site credits, recorded in TrustMyRecord's own systems. Credits are used for simulator runs, tips and the marketplace. Everything in this section describes how you earn them and what they do.

Site credits are not TMR tokens. They are not withdrawable, not transferable off the site and not convertible into the token on Base or into cash. They are not offered as a security or an investment, and nothing on this site promises any value.

How members earn site credits

Four earning rules are active:

  • 25 credits for creating your account, paid once you verify your email
  • 10 credits for posting your first sportsbook pick, once
  • 5 credits for starting your first forum thread, once
  • 1 credit for creating a forum thread

All other earning rules are paused, including pick wins, trivia, polls and referrals. Balances already earned are never reduced by a pause.

Ways to earn change over time. The list above is what is currently active. TrustMyRecord may add, change or remove earning opportunities at any time, and may also grant site credits at its discretion.

How site credits can be used

  • Simulator runs
  • The marketplace
  • Tipping other members for a good post or a good call

Card purchases of credits were sold at $0.02 per credit until they were turned off on 25 October 2026, and are currently closed. Withdrawals, transfers, redemption and cash redemption are all off.

What you should know

  • Site credits are not property and confer no rights.
  • Site credits carry no guarantee of value, availability or redemption.
  • TrustMyRecord may change how site credits work, what they can be used for, or end the programme entirely. If the programme ends, unused credits expire without compensation.
  • Credits obtained through abuse, automation, duplicate accounts or error may be withheld, reversed or cancelled.
Nobody at TrustMyRecord will ever message you an offer, ask you to send funds, ask you to connect a wallet to this site, or ask for a seed phrase. We do not sell TMR tokens and take no payment for them. The on-chain token is bought on a public decentralised exchange that you reach yourself using the contract address published on the TMR Coin page. Anyone selling you TMR in our name, or inviting you to an official TMR chat group, is running a scam: no such group exists.

Frequently asked questions

Is TMR Coin a cryptocurrency?

Yes. TMR Coin is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Base, at contract 0x522ddf023d0E2Cab5cB05660573165301519AE9e. The site credits shown in your wallet on this site are a separate internal balance recorded in TrustMyRecord's own records.

Can I buy it, and can I sell it?

The on-chain token can be bought in a Uniswap v3 TMR/WETH pool on Base (0.3% fee tier). Trading is open: TMR can be bought and sold there. The pool is small, about $30 of ETH, so large trades move the price sharply. Site credits are used on the site and nowhere else. There is no way to withdraw, sell or convert them. We never cold-message anyone an offer to sell TMR, so anyone doing that is running a scam.

Can I withdraw to my own wallet?

No. Withdrawals are off and none has ever been made. Treat your site credits as something you use here, not something you can take away.

Can I send credits to another member?

Yes, you can tip site credits to other members. Transfers off the site do not exist.

Where do I see my balance?

On your wallet page, with your full transaction history.
