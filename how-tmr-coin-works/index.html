TMR Coin

The currency of sports competition on TrustMyRecord. This guide covers the balance you earn on the site: you earn it by taking part and by being right, and you spend it inside the site. That balance has no cash value, cannot be bought, and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for money. TMR Coin also exists as a fixed supply token on Base that anyone can hold and trade in their own wallet, which is a separate thing and is covered on the TMR Coin page.

What the balance on this site is

The TMR shown in your wallet here is a balance recorded in TrustMyRecord's own systems. It recognises taking part and being right: posting picks, winning them, starting discussions, being here. Everything in this section describes that balance, not the on-chain token.

The balance on this site is not money. It is not a security, not an investment, and not a cash-equivalent or stored-value asset. It has no cash value, is not redeemable for cash, is not traded on any exchange, and cannot be transferred off the site. Every row in the table below describes this balance. How members earn points

Seven rules are active today. Three pay once, four repeat under a daily cap:

  • 25 TMR for creating your account, once
  • 10 TMR for posting your first sportsbook pick, once
  • 5 TMR for starting your first forum thread, once
  • 10 TMR for a pick graded as a win, up to 3 a day. Only a final graded win pays, so posting a pick and losing a pick both pay nothing
  • 10 TMR for a correctly graded prediction, up to 3 a day
  • 10 TMR for a qualified trivia session, up to 2 a day. Opening a session does not qualify it
  • 2 TMR for a qualifying forum thread, once a day

Two limits sit above all of them: 5,000 TMR per member per year, and a yearly emission budget of about 9,994,437 TMR across everyone. When the budget is consumed, earning scales down and then pauses. Balances already earned are never reduced.

Ways to earn change over time. The list above is what is currently active. TrustMyRecord may add, change or remove earning opportunities at any time, and may also grant TMR at its discretion.

How points can be used

  • Covering a BetLegend Pro research request after your free daily one is used
  • Items offered in the rewards catalogue
  • Tipping other members for a good post or a good call

Points can reduce what you pay us. They are never converted into money in either direction: you cannot buy points, and points cannot be cashed out.

What you should know

  • Points are not property and confer no rights.
  • Points carry no guarantee of value, availability or redemption.
  • TrustMyRecord may change how points work, what they can be used for, or end the programme entirely. If the programme ends, unused points expire without compensation.
  • Points obtained through abuse, automation, duplicate accounts or error may be withheld, reversed or cancelled.
Nobody at TrustMyRecord will ever message you an offer, ask you to send funds, ask you to connect a wallet to this site, or ask for a seed phrase. We do not sell TMR and take no payment for it. The on-chain token trades on a public decentralised exchange that you reach yourself using the contract address published on the TMR Coin page. Anyone selling you TMR in our name, or inviting you to an official TMR chat group, is running a scam: no such group exists.

Frequently asked questions

Is TMR Coin a cryptocurrency?

Yes — TMR Coin is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Base, at contract 0x522ddf023d0E2Cab5cB05660573165301519AE9e. The points shown in your wallet on this site are a separate internal balance recorded in TrustMyRecord's own records.

Can I buy or sell it?

The on-chain token, yes. A public Uniswap v3 pool on Base has been open since 17 August 2026, so anyone with a wallet can trade it without going through us. The balance on this site, no. It cannot be purchased, is not sold, is not traded anywhere, and cannot be moved off TrustMyRecord. We never sell TMR and will never message you an offer, so anyone doing that is running a scam.

Can I cash out?

No. Points have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.

Can I send points to another member?

Yes — you can tip points to other members inside the site. Points cannot be moved off TrustMyRecord.

Where do I see my balance?

On your wallet page, with your full transaction history.
