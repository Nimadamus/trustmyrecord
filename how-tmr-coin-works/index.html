What TMR Coin is

TMR Coin is the native rewards and utility currency of TrustMyRecord. It exists as a real token on the Base blockchain, an Ethereum layer-2 network, and can be held in any Base-compatible crypto wallet.

Your in-app balance and the on-chain token are not currently the same thing. The TMR Coin you earn and see in your wallet today lives in TrustMyRecord's internal ledger. The on-chain TMR Coin contract on Base holds the full fixed supply in the treasury wallet shown above. There is no live feature yet to move your earned, in-app balance onto the blockchain — that on-chain claim/transfer capability is planned, not available today. We'll update this page the moment on-platform and on-chain balances are technically synchronized.

Blockchain transactions are public and permanent. Anyone can look up the official contract or the deployment transaction on BaseScan at any time.

How TMR can be used

TMR Coin is earned and tracked inside the platform: your wallet balance and full transaction history. Today it has one live in-platform spending use — BetLegend Pro reports and simulations, at 5 TMR per completed report after a free first inquiry. The rewards catalog shows additional perks as "coming soon" previews — those are not redeemable yet.

TMR Coin cannot currently be purchased, traded, withdrawn, deposited, tipped, or exchanged on any market. None of those features are live.

Planned future utility includes tipping between members, a TMR-priced marketplace, and contest entry/prizes. Nothing here is a promise of financial return, price appreciation, or liquidity — see the risk notice below.

Risks

  • TMR Coin is not currently listed or tradable on any exchange or decentralized exchange. If a market ever exists in the future, its value could fluctuate significantly or go to zero.
  • TMR Coin is not an investment and carries no guarantee of value, redemption, price, or return.
  • Blockchain transactions are public and permanent. If you hold TMR Coin on-chain, transactions cannot be reversed by TrustMyRecord or anyone else.
  • Never trust a contract address you did not get directly from this official page or trustmyrecord.com/tmr-coin/ — verify every character before trusting any token labeled "TMR" or "TMR Coin."

Frequently asked questions

Is TMR Coin a real cryptocurrency?

Yes. TMR Coin is a real, fixed-supply ERC-20 token deployed on Base mainnet, and it's also TrustMyRecord's rewards currency.

What blockchain is it on?

Base, an Ethereum layer-2 network. Mainnet chain ID 8453.

What is the official contract address?

0x522ddf023d0E2Cab5cB05660573165301519AE9e on Base mainnet. This is the only official TMR Coin contract — always verify every character.

How do I earn TMR?

See the full "How members earn TMR" table above — signup (25), your first sportsbook pick (10), your first forum thread (5), and the first qualifying forum thread on each later calendar day (1). Your very first thread earns both the 5 and the 1.

Can I buy or sell it?

Not currently. There is no purchase, sale, trading, or exchange functionality live today.

What can I spend TMR Coin on?

Today, the only live spending use is BetLegend Pro reports and simulations — 5 TMR per completed report after a free first inquiry. Tipping, a marketplace, and contest entry/prizes are planned but not live.

Can I add it to my wallet?

Yes — you can manually import the official contract into any Base-compatible wallet. See "Add TMR to your wallet" above. Your in-app earned balance is not automatically reflected on-chain.

Is the supply fixed?

Yes, permanently. Total supply is 1,000,000,000 TMR. The contract has no mint function after deployment and no owner or admin role that could create more.

What happens if I send it to the wrong address?

Blockchain transactions are permanent and cannot be reversed by TrustMyRecord or anyone else. Always double-check the full destination address before sending.

How can I identify fake TMR tokens?

Only trust the exact contract address published on this page. Any other contract claiming to be "TMR" or "TMR Coin" — regardless of name or logo — is not official.
