How TMR Coin Works

TMR Coin is the platform currency of TrustMyRecord. Here's how you get it and what it's for today.

TMR Coin is the platform currency of TrustMyRecord. Every new member receives TMR Coin when they sign up. Members can earn additional coins through platform participation, achievements, referrals, contests, and competitions. Additional purchase and redemption options may be introduced as the platform develops.

How you earn TMR Coin

You start with a signup grant. Every reward after that is tied to something you actually did, verified server-side -- never something a client app merely claims happened. Each source has its own daily or per-event limit so no single action can be farmed for unlimited coins.

What's available today

Right now, TMR Coin is earned and spent inside the platform: your wallet balance, transaction history, and the rewards catalog. The rewards catalog shows examples of future perks as "coming soon" previews. Additional purchase and redemption options may be introduced as the platform develops -- nothing is promised on a timeline, and nothing is permanently ruled out either.

Frozen and reversed transactions

If your wallet is frozen, you'll see a clear banner on your wallet page. A frozen wallet can't earn or spend coins, but your balance and complete transaction history stay visible and are never deleted.

Every admin adjustment -- a grant, a deduction, or a reversal -- requires a written reason and shows up in your transaction history with a timestamp, so nothing about your balance ever changes silently.
