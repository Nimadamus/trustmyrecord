TrustMyRecord holds your TMR in its accounting ledger, the same way an exchange holds a balance, so everything here is instant and costs no gas. What the balance on this site is

The TMR shown in your wallet here is a balance recorded in TrustMyRecord's own systems. It recognises taking part and being right: posting picks, winning them, starting discussions, being here. Everything in this section describes how you earn it and what it does.

Your TMR is real TMR. TrustMyRecord holds it for you in its accounting ledger so spending it here is instant and costs no gas. It is not offered as a security or an investment, and nothing on this site promises its value will rise. On-chain withdrawal to your own wallet does not exist. How members earn TMR

Seven rules are active. Three pay a single time:

  • 25 TMR for creating your account, paid once you verify your email
  • 10 TMR for posting your first sportsbook pick, once
  • 5 TMR for starting your first forum thread, once

Four repeat, each with a hard daily cap. They came back on 26 August 2026 at a fraction of their old rates, because the point is to reward turning up rather than to hand out TMR:

  • 3 TMR for a pick graded as a win, up to twice a day. It pays on a final result, not on posting a pick, so volume alone earns nothing
  • 3 TMR for completing a trivia session, once a day. The session has to be a real attempt, not a click-through
  • 3 TMR for getting the daily poll right, once a day
  • 1 TMR for starting a forum thread, once a day
  • That is 13 TMR a day at the very most, for a member who does all of it every day. Competitions share a capped prize pool and remain the only route to a large amount of TMR

Two limits sit above all of them: 5,000 TMR per member per year, and a yearly emission budget of about 9,994,437 TMR across everyone. When the budget is consumed, earning scales down and then pauses. Balances already earned are never reduced.

Ways to earn change over time. The list above is what is currently active. TrustMyRecord may add, change or remove earning opportunities at any time, and may also grant TMR at its discretion.

How points can be used

  • Covering a BetLegend Pro research request after your free daily one is used
  • Items offered in the rewards catalogue
  • Tipping other members for a good post or a good call

TMR reduces what you pay us. Buying TMR directly and withdrawing it on-chain are both unbuilt.

What you should know

  • Points are not property and confer no rights.
  • Points carry no guarantee of value, availability or redemption.
  • TrustMyRecord may change how points work, what they can be used for, or end the programme entirely. If the programme ends, unused points expire without compensation.
  • Points obtained through abuse, automation, duplicate accounts or error may be withheld, reversed or cancelled.
Nobody at TrustMyRecord will ever message you an offer, ask you to send funds, ask you to connect a wallet to this site, or ask for a seed phrase. We do not sell TMR and take no payment for it. The on-chain token trades on a public decentralised exchange that you reach yourself using the contract address published on the TMR Coin page. Anyone selling you TMR in our name, or inviting you to an official TMR chat group, is running a scam: no such group exists.

Frequently asked questions

Is TMR Coin a cryptocurrency?

Yes — TMR Coin is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Base, at contract 0x522ddf023d0E2Cab5cB05660573165301519AE9e. The points shown in your wallet on this site are a separate internal balance recorded in TrustMyRecord's own records.

Can I buy or sell it?

The on-chain token, yes. A public Uniswap v3 pool on Base has been open since 17 August 2026, so anyone with a wallet can trade it without going through us. The TMR held for you on TrustMyRecord is spendable across the site and nowhere else. There is no way to withdraw it, sell it or convert it. We never cold-message anyone an offer to sell TMR, so anyone doing that is running a scam.

Can I withdraw to my own wallet?

No. There is no withdrawal system, and no date for one. Building it is a later phase and would need a legal review before anything could be switched on, so treat your balance as something you use here rather than something you will be able to take away.

Can I send TMR to another member?

Yes — you can tip TMR to other members. On-chain transfer out does not exist.

Where do I see my balance?

On your wallet page, with your full transaction history.
