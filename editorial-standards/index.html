Editorial Standards

The rules our matchup pages, Game Files, simulator explainers and articles follow, and how to tell us when we get something wrong.

Last reviewed September 28, 2026

Why we publish standards

TrustMyRecord exists because sports betting content is full of claims nobody can check. A site built on that idea has to hold its own writing to the same test. These are the rules every matchup page, Game File, simulator explainer and forum article from the TrustMyRecord team follows.

Every number has a source

Each statistic we publish comes from a named source or from a TrustMyRecord tool, and the page says which. For Game Files this is enforced by software: every quantitative claim is stored with its value, sample, source, the tool that produced it and the time it was retrieved, and an article containing a number with no matching record cannot be published. The details are on the research methodology page.

We separate three kinds of statement and label them so a reader can tell them apart: a fact is a measured value from a named source, a derived figure is something we calculated and can reconstruct, and analysis is our judgement. Analysis is opinion and is written as opinion.

Sample size is part of the claim

Most misleading sports statistics are true and tiny. A trend with six games behind it is described as a trend with six games behind it, and figures print their sample next to the number. Trends on matchup pages only appear after clearing minimum sample and edge gates, described in our methodology.

How automation is used

Data collection is automated. Schedules, rosters, standings, odds, historical queries and simulation runs are pulled by software, and pages such as the daily MLB matchup pages are assembled from that data on a schedule. Those pages present data and do not carry a written opinion.

Choosing which game gets a Game File, interpreting the research and writing the editorial sections is done by a person, and nothing in a Game File publishes without that review. Where AI tools help assemble or draft material, the same sourcing rule applies: a number without a provenance record does not publish.

Bylines

Game Files carry the byline TrustMyRecord Research. That is the name of our research function, not a person, and we will not invent a staff writer with a stock headshot. Where a named person is responsible for a piece of analysis, that person is named on it. Picks posted by members and by handicappers on their own profiles belong to those accounts and are graded like anyone else's.

Corrections and updates

Published analysis keeps its URL and stays where it was published after the game ends. When something materially changes before the game, such as a starting pitcher, a confirmed lineup, an injury or a corrected statistic, the page is updated and the update time is shown. A live odds widget refreshing itself is not treated as an update.

If a postgame section is added, the pregame analysis stays intact and labelled as pregame. We do not rewrite predictions after the result is known. To report an error, use the contact form and choose Picks or grading, or send it to support@trustmyrecord.com with the page and the figure in question.

Independence, sponsors and affiliate links

Some pages link to sportsbooks we have commercial relationships with, and contests may be run with a sponsor. Those relationships never change a grade, a record, a leaderboard position or a published statistic, all of which come from the same automated systems for every account. Sponsored contests and partner links are identified as such on the page where they appear.

TrustMyRecord is not a sportsbook and does not take wagers. No real money is wagered on the site, and nothing we publish is financial advice or a guarantee of any result.

Responsible gambling in our content

We do not describe any pick, trend or simulation as a lock or a sure thing, and we publish the uncertainty with the number. Content is intended for adults who can legally bet where they live. Our responsible gambling page lists where to get help.
