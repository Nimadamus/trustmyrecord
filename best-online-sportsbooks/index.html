How we judge a sportsbook

Does the money come out

Everything else is decoration. A book with great lines that slow rolls withdrawals is worse than a book with mediocre lines that pays same day. We weight published payout speed, frequency limits and fees above every other factor.

What does the number cost

A twenty cent baseball line versus a dime line is roughly a point of return on investment across a season for a favorite bettor. Standard -110 versus 5% juice is the same story on sides. We look at the recurring price, not the one-time bonus.

Will they take your bet

Published maximums and a willingness to raise them tell you whether the book wants winning players or is quietly planning to limit you the moment you show a profit.

Is the board deep enough

Halves, quarters, team totals, alternates, player props and live betting. A thin board forces you into whatever the book is offering rather than the bet you actually wanted to make.

Are the bonuses real

A bonus with a 40x rollover is not a bonus, it is a lock on your bankroll. We rate offers on whether the terms are stated openly, whether the bonus is optional, and whether the rollover is something a normal bettor clears.

Can you reach a human

24/7 chat and a phone number that answers is the difference between a graded dispute settled in ten minutes and one that eats your week. A named account rep is better again.
