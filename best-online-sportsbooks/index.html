How we judge a sportsbook

Does the money come out

Everything else is decoration. A book with great lines that slow rolls withdrawals is worse than a book with mediocre lines that pays same day. We weight published payout speed, payout frequency limits and fees above every other factor.

What does the number cost

A twenty cent baseball line versus a dime line is roughly a point of return on investment across a season for a favourite bettor. Standard -110 versus 5% juice is the same story on sides. We look at the recurring price, not the one-time bonus.

Will they take your bet

Published maximums and the willingness to raise them tell you whether the book wants winning players or is quietly planning to limit you the moment you show a profit.

Is the board deep enough

Halves, quarters, team totals, alternates, player props and live betting. A thin board forces you into whatever the book is offering rather than the bet you actually wanted to make.

Can you reach a human

24/7 chat and a phone number that answers is the difference between a graded dispute settled in ten minutes and one that eats your week. A named account rep is better again.

How long have they been standing

Offshore books come and go. Longevity, published house rules and a real help centre are the closest thing to a licence guarantee you get in this market.
