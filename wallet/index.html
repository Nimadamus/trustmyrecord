About TMR

TMR is an internal balance inside TrustMyRecord. It is earned through participation, spent on tools and content inside the site, and recorded in an append-only ledger where every movement keeps its own before-and-after balance.

What to know. Buying TMR directly and withdrawing it on-chain are not built. TMR is not offered as an investment and nobody is promising its value will rise. Anything that would change that requires a compliance structure TrustMyRecord does not have yet, and stays switched off until it does. The separate TMR Coin token on Base does trade publicly, we do not sell it, and holding a balance here gives you no claim on it.
