Delete Your Account and Data

This page covers TrustMyRecord and the BetLegend Pro app (com.betlegend.pro), which share one account. It explains exactly what is deleted, what is kept, and how to ask.

How to request deletion

Deletion is handled by a person, not a button. That is deliberate: pick records are permanent by design, and removing an account rewrites what other members' leaderboards and contest results are computed from, so each request is checked before it is carried out.

  1. Email support@trustmyrecord.com from the email address on the account. Sending from that address is how we confirm the request is yours.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account, and include your username.
  3. We reply to confirm, then complete the deletion. Personal data is removed within 30 days of the confirmed request.
Email a deletion request

If you can no longer send mail from the address on the account, write to us anyway and say so — we will ask for something else that shows the account is yours.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Permanently deleted

  • Email address and password
  • Username, display name, avatar and profile details
  • Notification and communication settings
  • Saved BetLegend Pro reports and your report library
  • Your TMR Coin wallet balance
  • Forum posts and messages, on request

Kept, with no link to you

  • Graded pick records, anonymized. Leaderboards, contest results and other members' verified records are computed from these; erasing them would silently change other people's published history.
  • Payment records held by Stripe. Stripe is our payment processor and retains transaction records as financial and tax law requires. Card details never reach TrustMyRecord.
  • Server logs, for up to 26 months, as stated in the privacy policy.

If you have a paid BetLegend Pro plan

Cancel the subscription first. Deleting the account does not cancel billing on its own — the subscription lives with Stripe, and it is cancelled through the billing portal linked from your BetLegend Pro plan. If you delete the account while a subscription is still active, email us and we will cancel it with you.

A lifetime purchase is not refunded by deletion. Deleting the account ends access to it permanently.

Deleting the app is not deleting your account

Uninstalling the BetLegend Pro app removes it and its local session from your device. Your account, your record and your data stay on TrustMyRecord until you request deletion above.
