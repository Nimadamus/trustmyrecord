What a seller storefront is

A storefront is a listing attached to your TrustMyRecord profile. You decide what to sell: single picks, or a day, weekly or monthly pass that gives a buyer your picks for that window. Each package can carry a cash price, a TMR Coin price, or both on the same listing, so buyers pay with whichever they hold.

Cash sales go through your own Stripe account, so buyers pay you directly and payouts go to your bank. TMR Coin sales settle into your TMR balance. The platform fee depends on how the buyer paid. The seller setup page shows the rate for each payment type, and the cash commission is set out in the Marketplace Terms.

What buyers can check before they pay

Every storefront links to the seller's full public ledger: record, net units, ROI and sample size, built from picks that were locked before the game started and graded automatically from the final score. A pick cannot be sold after its game has started, and a seller cannot change a pick once it is locked. A purchased pick stays hidden until payment is confirmed and then appears in the buyer's purchases dashboard.

What TrustMyRecord does not promise

Nobody here can guarantee winners, and sellers are not allowed to promise locks or risk free profit. A verified record shows what happened, not what will happen next. There is no promise of sales or earnings either: the marketplace is new, it has only a handful of sellers so far, and buyers decide whose record is worth paying for.

Why the record comes before the sale

Most pick sellers ask for trust up front: screenshots, claimed win rates, records nobody can check. TrustMyRecord flips that. Picks lock before start time, grade automatically against final scores, and stay on your public profile for good, wins and losses alike. Your storefront shows buyers that history before they spend anything.

If you are not ready to sell yet, the best time to start is still now, because every pick you lock becomes provable history later.
