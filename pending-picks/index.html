How the pregame record works

A record is only worth something if the losing picks can't vanish and the winners can't be added after the fact. TrustMyRecord proves both, without giving away anybody's picks early.

1

Locked at submission

The moment a pick is saved, the database writes a permanent copy of it with the time, the game's scheduled start and a SHA256 fingerprint. Nothing can change that copy.

2

Snapshot every 15 minutes

Every handicapper's pending picks are counted and sealed into a timestamped snapshot. You see the count and the leagues. The picks themselves stay hidden.

3

Frozen at game time

Once a game starts, its picks cannot be edited, hidden or withdrawn. A rare admin correction has to be logged with who made it and why, and the original stays on file.

4

Every pick accounted for

After the games, each snapshot shows how many of its picks were graded, are still pending, or were withdrawn. Open any profile to check yesterday's picks against today's record.

Only picks entered directly on TrustMyRecord appear here. Records of handicappers tracked from public posts are on Handicappers Around the Web. A second half wager is placed at halftime through the halftime gate and is counted as registered before the second half. Times are Pacific. Snapshot fingerprints can be checked against the public record at any time from the link on each card.
