Wager still marked Pending?

If an event has finished and your wager is still showing as Pending well after it should have been graded, report it from the ticket itself. Open your pending picks, find the wager, and use Report Ungraded Wager. Everything about the wager is attached automatically and you will be notified here on TrustMyRecord once it has been reviewed.
