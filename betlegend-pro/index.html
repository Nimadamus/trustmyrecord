How it stays honest

The constraints below are enforced in the engine, not promised in marketing copy.

No invented numbers

Estimated totals and synthetic lines are excluded from every figure. A market with no real closing number returns nothing instead of a guess.

Sample size always shown

Every rate comes with the record behind it. A 60% split on nine games is labelled as nine games, not presented as a trend.

Gaps are stated, not hidden

Where an era or market has no coverage, the report says so. Older MLB totals and pre-2010 NFL moneylines are blocked outright rather than approximated.

No outcome is promised

A verdict is a read on historical support at a price. It is not a prediction, and past splits do not carry forward.

Questions

Does BetLegend Pro tell me what to bet?

No. It reports what the historical record is for the exact bet you describe, how large the sample is, and how that compares to the break-even at your price. The call it gives is a read on that evidence — you decide what to do with it.

What data is it actually using?

A dataset of graded games across MLB, NBA, NFL and NHL with source-backed closing numbers. Estimated totals and synthetic lines are excluded from results. Soccer, first-half/quarter/first-five-inning markets and NRFI/YRFI are not covered because there is no historical data for them here.

How current is it?

The dataset covers completed, graded games and is refreshed on a schedule. It is a historical research tool, not a live odds feed — it will not price tonight's game for you.

What's the difference between the plans?

Nothing but the billing. Monthly, annual and lifetime all unlock the same unlimited access to all four tools and the full report library. Annual is the cheapest per month; lifetime is a single payment offered during launch.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Monthly and annual can be cancelled at any time from your account — you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and are not billed again. Lifetime has no recurring billing, so there is nothing to cancel.

Do I need TMR Coin?

No. Plans are bought with a card through Stripe. TMR Coin is the free on-ramp instead: you get one free report every day, and beyond that you can keep running reports with coin if you'd rather not subscribe. Subscribers are never charged coin.

Is this gambling?

No. TrustMyRecord is not a sportsbook, no real money is wagered here, and BetLegend Pro is an analysis tool. If betting has stopped being fun, help is available.
