How it works

Built on your verified record

Every TrustMyRecord pick is locked before the game and graded on the final score. That record is what makes an honest analysis possible.

1

Your picks are graded

Keep making picks on TrustMyRecord. Each graded pick carries the price you took and, where available, where the market closed.

2

Every graded pick is tested

Sport and bet type, price band, and three splits declared in advance: side, price, and home or away. Each is compared with the break-even rate of the prices you actually paid. The last 90 days and the two halves of the record are checked on their own. The closing line is its own test, not another slice. Closing lines TMR did not capture are filled from the book's recorded close where that close exists.

3

Five picks are reviewed in detail

Full pregame context is built for at most five picks: up to three recent losses in the priority category, one win whose price closed against the pick, and one recent pick from the strongest category. Those examples over-represent recent losses, so the factor count is not a reading of the whole category. Every other graded pick stays in the totals, the tests and the plan. Starters, injuries, rest, weather, goalies and line movement appear only when they were on file before the pick. A forecast from the time of the pick, prop closing prices, injury publication times, confirmed lineups, and inputs for soccer, MMA, tennis and period markets are not available.

Evidence, not excuses

The report never claims to know why a pick lost or what you were thinking. It shows what the market and the recorded inputs said before the game.

Wins get reviewed too

A win at a price the market rejected is flagged, and a loss that beat the closing line is called what it is: variance.

No promises

Ultimate helps you research and decide more carefully. It does not guarantee profit or improvement, and it says so when a sample is too small to judge.

Questions

Before you upgrade

Does Ultimate include everything in TMR Pro?

Yes. Every TMR Pro feature is part of Ultimate, unchanged: unlimited simulators, the Watchdog board, Bet Legend Pro, TrendSpotter money data and Model Builder. Ultimate adds Performance Intelligence on top.

I already have TMR Pro. How do I upgrade?

Your Pro subscription is changed to Ultimate in place, so you never pay for two plans. The difference for the rest of your billing period is charged when you upgrade, and Ultimate switches on as soon as that payment goes through. You can move back to Pro at any time and the unused Ultimate time is credited.

How is this different from my public profile?

Your profile shows what happened: record, units, ROI and splits. Ultimate tests the win rate against the break-even rate of the prices you paid, and it keeps ROI and units as their own numbers. When those signals disagree, it says the evidence is mixed. It compares your result with the range a bettor with no edge reaches at your prices, ranks where units were lost, shows what stake size did, and checks whether the result holds up over time. Then it turns the findings that clear the bar into a short plan. It does not explain why a single pick won or lost.

What is the report built from?

Your own graded TrustMyRecord picks: sport, market, the price you took, the result, and the closing line where it was captured. Each category is tested against the break-even rate of your prices, so a hot or cold run is labeled as variance when the sample cannot tell the difference.

How many picks do I need?

The report works from your first graded picks, but a category needs at least 30 graded picks before it gets a verdict. Below that it is shown as too few picks to judge.

Will Ultimate make me a winning bettor?

No product can promise that. Ultimate shows you where your record is strong, where it is weak, how sure that is, and what to research before the next pick. It never claims to know why a single pick won or lost. What you do with it is up to you.

Is Joe Public a real member?

No. Joe Public, his picks, the teams and the games in the demo are fictional, created to show what a complete report looks like.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your account. Ultimate stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then the account returns to free.
