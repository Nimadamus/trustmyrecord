Efficiency

Where the drives actually end

Third down is the down that decides whether a drive keeps going or hands the ball back, and Carolina is converting 44.4% of them against 16.7% for Atlanta. These two are seeing about 10 third downs a game, so that edge is worth roughly 2.9 extra sets of downs to Carolina, which is a drive that lives instead of a punt.

Inside the 20 the difference between a touchdown and a field goal is four points every single trip, and Carolina is finishing 100.0% of its red zone trips with a touchdown against 0.0% for Atlanta. Three trips a game at that gap is most of a score, and none of it shows up in a yardage column.

Turnovers move scores faster than anything else on the page: Atlanta is -1 on the season against -2 for Carolina. An extra possession is worth about two points of expectation on its own, and it is also the least stable number here, so it is a reason to respect a side rather than to price one.
