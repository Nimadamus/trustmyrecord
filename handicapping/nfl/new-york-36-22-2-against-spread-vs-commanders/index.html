Efficiency

Where the drives actually end

Third down is the down that decides whether a drive keeps going or hands the ball back, and New York is converting 43.4% of them against 31.6% for Washington. These two are seeing about 14 third downs a game, so that edge is worth roughly 1.6 extra sets of downs to New York, which is a drive that lives instead of a punt.

Inside the 20 the difference between a touchdown and a field goal is four points every single trip, and Washington is finishing 54.5% of its red zone trips with a touchdown against 54.5% for New York. Three trips a game at that gap is most of a score, and none of it shows up in a yardage column.
