Efficiency

Where the drives actually end

Third down is the down that decides whether a drive keeps going or hands the ball back, and New Orleans is converting 50.0% of them against 33.3% for Las Vegas. These two are seeing about 15 third downs a game, so that edge is worth roughly 2.5 extra sets of downs to New Orleans, which is a drive that lives instead of a punt.

Inside the 20 the difference between a touchdown and a field goal is four points every single trip, and New Orleans is finishing 71.4% of its red zone trips with a touchdown against 71.4% for Las Vegas. Three trips a game at that gap is most of a score, and none of it shows up in a yardage column.

Turnovers move scores faster than anything else on the page: Las Vegas is +2 on the season against -1 for New Orleans. An extra possession is worth about two points of expectation on its own, and it is also the least stable number here, so it is a reason to respect a side rather than to price one.
