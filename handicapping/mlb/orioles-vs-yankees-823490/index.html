What the numbers are worth

The tables above measure these two clubs. This reads them: each offence crossed with the staff it is facing, the bullpen gap in runs rather than in ERA, and what the posted price is charging over the fair one.

Cross each offence with the staff it is actually facing and you get a rough run environment for this game: Orioles score 4.46 a game against a staff giving up 3.73, which splits to 4.09, and Yankees score 4.59 against 4.59 allowed, which splits to 4.59. The bullpens are the gap nobody prices until the sixth: Yankees at 3.15 against 3.82 for Orioles. A modern pen throws about three innings a night, so that difference is worth roughly 0.22 runs a game to Yankees, and it lands in exactly the innings where a one run lead is either protected or handed back.
