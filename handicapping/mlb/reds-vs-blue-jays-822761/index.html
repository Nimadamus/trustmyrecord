What the numbers are worth

The tables above measure these two clubs. This reads them: each offence crossed with the staff it is facing, the bullpen gap in runs rather than in ERA, and what the posted price is charging over the fair one.

Cross each offence with the staff it is actually facing and you get a rough run environment for this game: Reds score 4.11 a game against a staff giving up 4.28, which splits to 4.20, and Blue Jays score 4.00 against 5.10 allowed, which splits to 4.55.
