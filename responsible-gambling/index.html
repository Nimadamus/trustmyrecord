Responsible Gambling

What our records show about betting, how to keep it recreational, and where to get confidential help.

Last reviewed September 28, 2026

What TrustMyRecord is, and is not

TrustMyRecord is a record keeping and research platform. We are not a sportsbook, we do not accept wagers, and no real money is wagered on the site. But the people who use our records, simulators and research often do bet, and a site that tracks betting results has a responsibility to be straight about the risks.

You must be at least 18 to hold a TrustMyRecord account, and you must be of legal age to gamble where you live before placing any real bet. In most US states that allow sports betting the minimum age is 21.

What verified records actually show

We have tracked a lot of picks, and the records tell a consistent story. Most bettors lose over time, because the price of every bet includes the book's margin. Break even at standard -110 pricing is 52.4 percent, not 50. Hot streaks happen to everyone and mean far less than they feel like they mean. Small samples are noise.

That is the whole reason this site exists: to replace screenshots and memory with a record that includes the losses. If your own record, looked at honestly, shows you losing money you cannot afford, that is useful information, not something to chase.

Habits that keep betting recreational

  • Set a budget you can afford to lose completely, and a time limit, before you start. Treat it as the cost of entertainment.
  • Never chase losses by raising your stake to win it back.
  • Keep an honest record of every bet, including the losing ones. A free TrustMyRecord account does this automatically.
  • Do not bet with money meant for rent, bills, savings or debt, and never borrow to bet.
  • Do not bet when you are upset, drinking or trying to escape a problem.
  • Remember that simulations and trends are research, not guarantees. A 60 percent projection loses four times in ten.

Warning signs

It may be time to stop or get help if you are betting more than you planned, hiding betting from people close to you, borrowing money or selling things to bet, feeling anxious or irritable when you are not betting, or chasing losses. These are common, and help is confidential and free.
