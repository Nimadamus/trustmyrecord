Your models

Saved models & forward tracking

Saved models are private, and they run on their own. From the moment you save one it scans the live board every 20 minutes, books every selection that matches its filters at the price on the board, and settles each one on the final score. You also see which picks real handicappers posted that fit the same filters. Both records are kept apart from the backtest and from the verified ledger.

Log in to save models and track them forward. Log in or create a free account.

Tracked Models

Publicly verified tracked models

A model is listed here only once it has taken its own positions off the live board, those positions have settled on final scores, and an admin has reviewed it. The record shown is the model's own book, not the picks that happened to match it. Backtest-only models never appear here.
