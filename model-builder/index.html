Model Builder

Build a betting model, test it against historical TrustMyRecord results, then track every future wager that matches your conditions.

Once tracking begins, TrustMyRecord automatically records and grades every future qualifying wager for the duration you choose. You never have to come back and run it.

  1. 1

    Build

    Define the conditions you believe in. Sport, market, side, home or away, price range, line range. Something like home favorites -150 to -250.

  2. 2

    Backtest

    Optional. See how those exact conditions did in the verified graded ledger, so you can decide whether the idea is worth tracking. This is history, not your model's record.

  3. 3

    Track

    Choose how long you want it monitored: 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 days, your own end date, or until you stop it. Then start it. That moment is where the model's own record begins.

  4. 4

    Measure

    From then on every future wager that meets your conditions is logged at the posted price and graded on the final score. Record, win rate, ROI, units, pending and settled, all out of sample.

It is a paper record, kept to one side. Nothing is bet, nothing is charged, and none of it touches your profile, your pick history or the leaderboards.

The detail

Exactly how it works

Every line here is a statement about behaviour. There are no example numbers on this page on purpose: a tool whose whole promise is that it never invents a record should not open with an invented one.

Prices
Taken from the TrustMyRecord sportsbook board, the same number shown on the sportsbook at that moment, with the book named on every bet.
Markets
Moneyline, spread and run line, total, and team total, plus their alternate lines. Only markets that can be settled from a final score.
Stake
Flat, and you set it. Every bet stores the stake it was logged at, so changing it later never rewrites the record already built.
Timing
Checked every 20 minutes. A bet is logged once, at the first qualifying price, and never re-priced or taken twice.
Grading
On the final score, by the same grader that grades every pick on TrustMyRecord. A market it cannot settle is left open rather than guessed.
Money
None. Nothing is wagered, nothing is charged, and no sportsbook account is involved.
Where it lives
Under your model only. It never appears on your profile, your pick history, your record or the leaderboards.
Stopping
Pause it whenever you like, or give it an end date. Bets already open still settle either way.

It answers one question: if you had actually been betting this idea, where would you stand? You can also backtest the idea against every verified graded pick already in the database before you switch it on, which is what the builder above does when you press Run historical backtest. That backtest is a separate population and it never enters a model's forward record.
