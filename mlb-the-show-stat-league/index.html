Stat League Questions

When does the MLB The Show Stat League start tracking?

Tracking is live. Auto tracking started on November 27, 2026. Each player's record starts the moment they link a username, and games played before that are never added.

Is the MLB The Show Stat League a stat tracker?

Yes. Link your PlayStation or Xbox username in MLB The Show auto tracking and every online MLB The Show 26 game you finish posts to your record within minutes with the final score, hits, errors and pitchers of record.

How do I get my games into the league?

Link the username you play under on the Online Gaming page, then just play. There is nothing to upload and nothing to report, and nobody has to confirm anything.

Do I need to upload box score screenshots?

No. Games come in automatically once your username is linked.

Which modes count?

Any online mode you finish, including Ranked, Battle Royale and Events. Offline games against the CPU do not count.

What stats does the league track?

Wins and losses, runs scored and allowed, run differential, hits and errors for both sides, and the winning, losing and save pitchers for every game. Standings and the leaderboard update from those games.

Can I stop tracking?

Yes. Use Stop tracking in the auto tracking panel at any time. Games already posted stay on your record.

Is the Stat League free?

Yes. There is no entry fee, no prize pool and no wagering. You play for a verified record and a spot on the leaderboard.

Which consoles can play?

MLB The Show 26 players on PlayStation and Xbox. Link the username for the platform you play on.
