    The Watchdog record, as graded

    Every graded pick by a tracked handicapper outside TMR who already had 25 or more graded picks before that game. Winners and losers count the same, 1 unit per pick. A record of what already happened, not a forecast.

    How these figures are counted

    How records are graded
    1. ReadPublic picks on Covers, X, Reddit, TheRX and other forums, collected around the clock.
    2. StampEach pick is saved with the time it was posted. A pick posted after the game starts never counts.
    3. GradeGraded on the final score at the posted price, 1 unit risked per pick.
    4. PublishRecord, units, ROI, win rate, recent form and league splits on a free public profile.

    Independent, not self reported

    No handicapper submits or edits their own record. Deleted posts, forgotten losses and picks made after kickoff cannot change it.

    Anonymous on purpose

    Every handicapper appears as a tracking number, never a name or a link. TMR grades records; it does not promote anyone or send you to buy their picks.

    The picks behind the number

    A record tells you how someone did. The pick history tells you how: which leagues, which prices, which stretch of the season.

    Questions

    Membership and billing

    How much does it cost?

    TMR Pro is $49.99 a month or $500 a year. TMR Ultimate is $99.99 a month. Prices are in US dollars, before any applicable tax.

    Is there a free trial?

    No. TMR Pro is $49.99 a month or $500 a year, and you can cancel any time from this page.

    How is billing handled?

    Payments run through Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's secure checkout, never on TrustMyRecord. Manage membership opens Stripe's billing page for your card and invoices.

    Can I cancel? What happens next?

    Yes, any time, with Cancel subscription on this page. No contract and no fee. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, then your account returns to the free version of each tool. Your record and picks stay exactly as they are.

    When can I subscribe?

    Now. Pick a plan above. Checkout is handled by Stripe.

    Features and access

    What does TMR Ultimate add?

    Everything in TMR Pro plus Performance Intelligence on your own graded picks: your win rate against the break even rate of the prices you paid, units and ROI, where chance still explains the result, what stake size did, and a plan built from the findings that clear the bar. It is not open for purchase yet. See the sample report.

    Where do the Watchdog's pending picks come from?

    From public posts by the handicappers TrustMyRecord tracks on Covers, X, TheRX, Reddit and other sites. Handicappers are shown by tracking number, never by name, and only games that have not started appear.

    Are TrustMyRecord members' picks on the board?

    No. Members' picks stay private until their games start, for everyone, including subscribers.

    Is Bet Legend Pro the same tool sold on its own?

    Yes. It is sold separately at $49 a month, and TMR Pro and TMR Ultimate include it in full.

    Data and methodology

    How are handicapper records graded?

    Each pick is captured from the original public post before the game and graded on the final score. Records are never self reported, and every record opens to the graded picks behind it.

    Do the handicappers on the board win?

    Some do and many do not. When verified figures are available, this page shows the graded result of the proven group with the dates it covers, whichever way it goes. It describes past picks only.

    Is Model Builder a backtester?

    Mainly no. A model tracks forward from the moment you build it, graded as games settle. An optional preview shows how the same conditions did in the graded history.

    Does a membership change my record or ranking?

    No. A subscription buys research tools only. It does not change your verification, badges, grading or leaderboard rank.
